 Office of General Counsel Precedent Opinion

**What the case is about**

Where a claimant has arthritis and instability of the knee, does 38 C.F.R. § 4.71a authorize multiple ratings under diagnostic codes 5003 and 5257?

**Impact on VBA**

A claimant who has arthritis and instability of the knee may be rated separately under diagnostic codes 5003 and 5257.
