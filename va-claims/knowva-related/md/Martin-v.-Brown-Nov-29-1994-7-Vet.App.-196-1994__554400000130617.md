Decision Assessment Document
Martin v. Brown, Nov 29, 1994, 7 Vet.App. 196 (1994)
U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims (Court)

**What the case is about:**

The Court held that when pension benefits are paid as an accrued benefit, the payment meets the pension-income-exclusion provision of 38 U.S.C. 1503(a)(2) and is not countable income for the purpose of determining entitlement to VA death pension.

**Impact on VBA:**

The Court's holding has been incorporated as guidance in the Manual under V.iii.1.I.

**Summary of the facts and Court’s reasons:**

The surviving spouse claimed that the Board of Veterans' Appeals (Board) erred by including accrued pension as countable income for the purpose of determining entitlement to improved death pension benefits.  The Veterans Court held in a March 1994 decision that accrued pension benefits, which compensate for underpayments pursuant to 38 U.S.C. Chapter 51, are not to be classified as pensions under Chapter 15; therefore, there is no basis for their exclusion as countable income under 38 C.F.R. 3.372.   The surviiving spouse filed a motion for reconsideration, which was granted by the Court.

The Court vacated its March 1994 decisio.  In a new decision, promulgated in November 1994, the Court held that accrued pension benefits are excluded as countable income for death pension purposes.
