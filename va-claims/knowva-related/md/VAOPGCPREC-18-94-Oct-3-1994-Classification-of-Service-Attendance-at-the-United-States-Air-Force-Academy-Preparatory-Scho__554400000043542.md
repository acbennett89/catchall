Decision Assessment Document
 VAOPGCPREC 18-94, Oct 3, 1994, Classification of Service - Attendance at the United States Air Force Academy Preparatory School
 Office of General Counsel Precedent Opinion

**What the case is about**

Whether service as a precadet at the United States Air Force Academy Preparatory School (USAFAPS) may be considered "active duty" service for purposes of title 38, United States Code.

**Impact on VBA**

Characterization of an individual's service at the USAFAPS for purposes of entitlement to veterans' benefits depends upon the status in which the individual enters the USAFAPS.  Service by an individual who attends the USAFAPS as a reservist called to active duty for the sole purpose of attending the USAFAPS constitutes "active duty for training" and thus is not within the definition of "active duty" under 38 U.S.C. § 101(21)(A).  This includes persons who are enlisted directly from civilian life or from the Air National Guard for the sole purpose of attending the USAFAPS, as well as members of reserve components who are called to active duty for this purpose.  Service by an enlisted active duty servicemember who is reassigned to the USAFAPS without a release from active duty constitutes a continuation of the servicemember's "active duty."

**Summary on facts and findings**

General Counsel explored the various statutes and regulations pertaining to what constitutes "active duty."  There are differing regulations with respect to what constitutes "active duty" for education benefits and for compensation and pension benefits.  Under the various regulations, the service of persons attending the USAFAPS can be classified according to their status at the time of entering the USAFAPS.  Persons serving as enlisted personnel at the time of entry into the USAFAPS continue to serve under the prior enlistment; they are merely reassigned and are not relieved of their service obligation.  In contrast, persons who were previously reservists, Air National Guard members, or civilians are enlisted as members of the Air Force Reserve and called to active duty for the sole purpose of attending the USAFAPS.  This service appears to meet the definition of "active duty for training" for title 38 purposes.

The provisions of 38 C.F.R. §§ 3.6(b)(4) and 3.6(c) should be amended to reflect this decision, however, this action will be deferred pending a request to the General Counsel for an opinion as to the applicability of this opinion to the other service's preparatory schools.
