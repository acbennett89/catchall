Decision Assessment Document

Bagwell v. Brown, Jul 3, 1996, 9 Vet.App. 337

U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims (Court)

**What the case is about:**

Extraschedular evaluations

**Impact on VBA:**

See M21-1, III.iv.6.B.4.a

**Summary of the facts and Court’s reasons:**

In July 1992, the veteran was granted entitlement to disability compensation for blindness in his left eye resulting from inadequately performed cataract surgery at a VAMC.  The disability was treated as if service-connected under 38 U.S.C. § 1151 and assigned a 30% rating and special monthly compensation on the basis of the "loss of use of left eye with no light perception.”  As a result, his combined evaluation was increased from 70% to 80%.  The veteran filed a Notice of Disagreement arguing that he was entitled to a rating greater than 30% because 30% did not take into account his and his wife's pain and suffering.  He argued that the rating was not sufficient to compensate for expenses incurred as a result of his prolonged hospital stays, including his wife's transportation to and from VAMCs.  He also argued that he should be rated 100% based upon his inability to obtain employment.  The BVA found that the appropriate rating was governed by Diagnostic Code 6070 which provides a 30% rating for blindness in one eye where the other eye has vision of 20/40 or better.  The BVA concluded that an extraschedular evaluation under 3 8 CFR § 3.3 2 1 (b)(1) was not warranted because the record did not show frequent periods of hospitalization after November 1991 or a marked interference with employment.  The BVA referred the issue of total disability due to individual unemployability to the regional office for action.

In this case, the BVA denied an extraschedular evaluation without the regional office first considering that issue.  On 5/28/96, the Court of Veterans Appeals (the Court) issued a remand in this case because the Board did not "refer the matter for decision in the first instance by the appropriate VA officials”, the denial of an extraschedular rating in this case was remanded for compliance with *Floyd.* Acting upon VA's motion for reconsideration, the Court vacated the 5/28/96 decision and replaced it with this one.
