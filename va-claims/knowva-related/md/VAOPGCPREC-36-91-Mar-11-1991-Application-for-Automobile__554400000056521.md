Decision Assessment Document
 VAOPGCPREC 36-91, Mar 11, 1991, Application for Automobile
 Office of General Counsel Precedent Opinion

**What the opinion is about:**

Does the present language of 38 U.S.C. § 3202 enable the VA to accept an application for an automobile authorized to an eligible veteran who is incompetent and where the application is signed by a spouse as payee?

Note:  This opinion, previously issued as Opinion of the General Counsel 9-76, dated August 25, 1975, is reissued as a Precedent Opinion pursuant to 38 C.F.R. §§ 2.6(e)(9) and 14.507.  The text of the opinion remains unchanged from the original except for certain format and clerical changes necessitated by the aforementioned regulatory provisions.

**Impact on VBA:**

See M21-1, IX.i.2

**Summary of the facts and findings:**

See OGC opinion for facts.

A spouse-payee may file an application for an automobile on behalf of an incompetent veteran and such an application may be properly accepted and processed.  Op.Sol. 273-52 is therefore rescinded insofar at it is inconsistent with this opinion.
