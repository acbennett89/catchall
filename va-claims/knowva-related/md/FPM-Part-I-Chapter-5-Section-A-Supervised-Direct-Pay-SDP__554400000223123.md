**I.5.A.1.a . General Information on Evaluating a Beneficiary’s Ability to Manage Their VA Funds**

The Field Examiner (FE) must evaluate the adult beneficiary’s capacity to manage funds without Department of Veteran’s Affairs (VA) supervision as is shown through the beneficiary’s real-world financial performance in meeting their basic needs.  This includes an assessment of the beneficiary’s understanding of basic life needs (i.e., food, shelter) and a prioritization for these needs, financial awareness, and a review of their financial history.  The FE must complete a detailed evaluation and document their findings in the field examination as described in [FPM, Part I, 5.A.1.b](#1b).

The FE’s evaluation is used to determine if the beneficiary who has been rated incompetent by VA may be eligible for supervised direct pay (SDP) temporarily as described in [FPM, Part I, 5.A.2.a](#2a).

**I.5.A.1.b . Evaluating and Documenting the Beneficiary’s Capacity to Manage Funds**

The FE must evaluate the beneficiary’s ability to independently manage funds in the best interest of the beneficiary and their dependents, if any, by asking probing questions about the beneficiary’s

* understanding of the

  + sources and amounts of their income, and
  + fixed monthly expenses such as rent, mortgage, utilities, clothing, food, and medical bills
* memory of recent transactions and ability to allocate appropriate funds to fixed monthly expenses and discretionary items
* ability to pay monthly bills in a timely manner
* actions they currently take or would take to conserve funds remaining after the payment of expenses and plans to prepare for large, irregular purchases or unexpected expenses, and
* knowledge of financial exploitation and strategies to prevent being a victim.

After completing the evaluation, document in the field examination the beneficiary’s

* awareness of monthly
  + income, and
  + expenses
* ability to
  + allocate funds to monthly expenses, and
  + pay monthly expenses, and
* capacity to handle VA funds.

Additionally, the FE must corroborate the beneficiary’s ability to manage funds through their interview with the beneficiary and, to the extent possible, a review of

* financial documentation, and
* evidence obtained through contact in person, by telephone, or by letter with a person who has direct and regular contact with the beneficiary, such as the beneficiary’s friend, relative, or care provider when the beneficiary authorizes the FE to do so.

If there is evidence the beneficiary has the capacity to manage their funds and an SDP appointment is being considered, follow the guidance in [FPM, Part I, 5.A.2.d](#2d) for completing the required comprehensive assessment.

***Notes***:

* The beneficiary is not required to conserve excess funds.  If the beneficiary demonstrates knowledge in the ability to conserve excess funds, practical application of this action is not required.
* A beneficiary’s best interest may also include the individual’s choice for non-essential items such as recreation and entertainment.  When assessing a capacity to manage funds, an allowance should be made for these discretionary purchases based on the beneficiary’s needs and/or wants while ensuring necessary expenses such as mortgage/rent, utilities, and food have first been prioritized.
* Fiduciary hub (hub) personnel must not allow their personal biases or feelings to intrude in the course of assessing the abilities of a beneficiary.  Fairness and courtesy must be shown at all times.  When, after careful consideration of all procurable and assembled data, a reasonable doubt arises regarding the degree of capacity, such doubt will be resolved in favor of the beneficiary.

***Reference***:  For more information on documenting information gathered during the field examination in the Veterans Benefits Management System (VBMS), see [FPM, Part I, 2.E.1.d](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153953/).

**I.5.A.2.a . General Information on SDP**

**Supervised Direct Pay (SDP)** refers to an adult beneficiary to whom benefits are paid directly when the facts indicate that they can handle their financial affairs under the continuing supervision of the Fiduciary Hub Manager (FHM).

If during the field examination, the hub finds that that the beneficiary can manage funds payable without limitation, the FHM may authorize the payment of VA benefits directly to an adult beneficiary in the Fiduciary Program.  The SDP method allows the beneficiary to continue managing their funds while the evidence obtained during the field examination is being reviewed and the rating issue of the beneficiary’s competency is re-evaluated.

During the temporary period, VA will provide limited supervision to ensure the beneficiary is capable of managing their funds without the appointment of a fiduciary.  Upon completion of the rating to re-evaluate the beneficiary’s competency, which should be completed within six months from the SDP appointment, the hub will

* remove the beneficiary from the Fiduciary Program if rated competent, or
* appoint a fiduciary if the rating of incompetency is continued.

Long-term payment of funds in an SDP capacity must never occur, as VA is acting as *de facto* fiduciary in these cases.  If the SDP beneficiary gives away, or has been deprived of, all or part of their income, a misuse allegation may be made against VA.

**I.5.A.2.b . Inappropriate Use of SDP**

SDP is not appropriate to

* allow a trial period to determine if the beneficiary has the capacity to manage funds
* satisfy the requirements of clinical mental health treatment
* lessen the impact to the self-esteem of the beneficiary, or
* solve other personal issues.

SDP is not appropriate for a beneficiary

* with documented cognitive conditions likely to deteriorate rapidly, for example, progressive dementia
* with substantial funds in a Personal Funds of Patient (PFOP) account
* with accrued monthly benefits
* currently under a court-appointed guardianship or Uniform Veterans Guardianship Act as they legally may not enter into any contracts, including maintaining a bank account and signing checks, or
* capable of only handling a portion of the insurance dividends or monthly installments to which they are entitled.

***References***:  For more information on

* SDP when the beneficiary is hospitalized or institutionalized, see [FPM, Part I, 5.A.2.c](#2c), and
* PFOP accounts, see [FPM, Part II, 1.C.1](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153966/).

**I.5.A.2.c . SDP When the Beneficiary Is Hospitalized or Institutionalized**

When a beneficiary is hospitalized or institutionalized at the time of the IA, consideration must be given to the reason for hospitalization and whether the stay is likely to be of long duration.

SDP is inappropriate when hospitalization is expected to continue for a lengthy or indefinite period.  The facility’s policy regarding patient receipt of funds must be reviewed when considering SDP method of payment.  If the FE determines that SDP is appropriate for the hospitalized or institutionalized beneficiary, the field examination must document the

* expected length of stay in the facility
* facility’s policy regarding a patient in receipt of funds, and
* reason(s) another method of payment was not selected.

***Notes***:

* An ***institutional setting*** refers to a facility in which a beneficiary is confined or receiving professional or skilled care, such as a nursing home or hospital.  An institution may accept private pay and Medicaid recipients as well as beneficiaries under VA contract or domiciliary care.  Institutions also include penal facilities or mental institutions.
* The term ***institutionalized*** refers to a beneficiary being placed in such a facility.  Community residential care, assisted living without 24-hour nursing care, personal care homes, and similar facilities are not institutions for purposes of VA’s Fiduciary Program.

***Reference***:  For more information on inappropriate use of SDP, see [FPM, Part I, 5.A.2.b](#2b).

**I.5.A.2.d . Evidence of the Beneficiary’s Capacity to Manage Funds**

The FE must identify *specific evidence* that demonstrates the beneficiary’s capacity to manage their funds through the comprehensive assessment as described in [FPM, Part I, 5.A.1.b](#1b).

Additionally, the FE must attempt to obtain copies of financial documents demonstrating the beneficiary’s ability to manage funds.  If unable to obtain copies during the field examination, the FE must

* advise the beneficiary to send in copies of the financial documents, and
* record the period of financial documentation reviewed for inclusion in the *Supervised Direct Pay Assessment*.

***Note***:  Do not delay closing the field examination work item or referring the *Supervised Direct Pay Assessment* while waiting for the beneficiary to submit copies of the financial documentation.

The FE may also obtain and document any authorized third-party statements that support the determination regarding the beneficiary’s abilities when the beneficiary authorizes the FE to do so.

**I.5.A.2.e . Advising the SDP Beneficiary**

Upon determining that an SDP appointment is appropriate, the FE must assist the beneficiary in the development of a budget.  The budget is developed based on the recurring income and expenses documented in accordance with [FPM, Part I, 5.A.1.b](#1b).

The FE shall advise the SDP beneficiary

* of the process and required evidence necessary to be rated as able to manage their VA funds and assist the beneficiary in filling out the VA forms, if necessary
* that the VA will

  + conduct monthly telephonic contact to ensure monthly expenses are paid, and funds are managed in a way that routinely meets their best interests, and
  + end the SDP appointment if the beneficiary does not use VA funds (monthly, one-time retroactive, or transferred funds) for timely payment of expenses associated with their or their dependent’s care, support, or maintenance (for example, food, shelter, or medicines).

The FE will also ask the beneficiary for a preferred day of the week and time they would like to be contacted for each of the monthly calls.  This information will be documented in the final field examination.

***Note***:  The *Beneficiary Notification SDP Letter* contains the budget and a *Supervised Direct Payment Fund Usage Worksheet* to assist the beneficiary in tracking expenses.

***References***:  For more information on

* the conservation of funds when a beneficiary is assigned an SDP appointment, see [FPM, Part I, 5.A.1.b](#1b)

**I.5.A.3.a . Requesting Restored Competency**

If evidence supports the beneficiary is capable of managing funds payable without limitation, the FE must request the establishment of the appropriate end product (EP) with special issue indicator for restored competency evaluation to remove the beneficiary from the program.  The *Supervised Direct Pay Assessment* must accompany the request.

**I.5.A.3.b . Assessment for Competency Review Requirements**

A *Supervised Direct Pay Assessment* detailing the comprehensive assessment evidence obtained during the field examination must be completed to support determinations of competency.  This assessment must

* describe the

  + beneficiary’s knowledge of their income and expenses, and
  + length of time that the beneficiary has managed their VA benefits successfully, and
* identify

  + the medical evidence utilized in the rating of incompetency and describe how the evidence obtained during the field examination conflicts with the evidence previously of record
  + the financial documentation that demonstrates the beneficiary’s payment history, and
  + any other evidence demonstrating the beneficiary’s ability to manage funds.

A sample of the *Supervised Direct Pay Assessment* outlining the comprehensive evidentiary requirements can be found in [FPM, Part I, 5.A.3.c](#3c) for reference purposes.  Hubs will create the assessment within VBMS.

***Important***:  If the evidence is not sufficient to support all aspects of the *Supervised Direct Pay Assessment* to confirm a capacity to manage funds, a referral for a rating of competency is not appropriate and the beneficiary cannot be appointed SDP.

**I.5.A.4.a . General Information on SDP Periodic Telephonic Contact**

[38 CFR 13.110(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=2c89d61e6c324379852e2be89891cae4&mc=true&node=se38.1.13_1110&rgn=div8) requires VA to provide limited and temporary supervision of beneficiaries receiving direct payment under an SDP appointment.

In accordance with [38 CFR 13.110(c)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=2c89d61e6c324379852e2be89891cae4&mc=true&node=se38.1.13_1110&rgn=div8), hub personnel must also reassess the SDP beneficiary’s ability to manage VA benefits, prepare a report that summarizes the findings, and refer the information with a recommendation and supporting evidence to the rating authority for application of [38 CFR 3.353(b)(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=2c89d61e6c324379852e2be89891cae4&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1353&rgn=div8).

To ensure proper supervision, hub personnel must contact SDP beneficiaries on a monthly basis to review their fund usage and assess their ability to manage VA funds in a way that routinely meets their best interest.  This monthly supervision will be completed telephonically and will continue while awaiting a redetermination of the prior rating of incompetency, not to exceed six months.

***References***:  For more information on

* SDP appointments, see [FPM, Part I, 5.A.2](#2) and
* capacity to manage funds, see [FPM, Part I, 5.A.1](#1).

**I.5.A.4.b . Supervision of a Beneficiary**

During each monthly telephone call, hub personnel must determine if there have been any changes in the SDP beneficiary’s condition or circumstances that would affect their ability to manage VA funds.

Prior to contacting the beneficiary

* review the electronic claims folder (eFolder) for any new information since the SDP appointment, and
* determine the preferred day of the week and time the beneficiary wants to be contacted as documented on the field examination during the SDP appointment.

Confirm contact information (addresses, telephone numbers, etc.) and make updates to VA systems if there are any changes.

Inquire about any changes in the beneficiary’s

* disabilities and their effect on the beneficiary’s ability to manage funds, and
* life that are affecting the beneficiary’s ability to manage VA funds.

***Notes***:

* Follow procedures in [FPM, Part I, 5.A.4.f](#4f) if the beneficiary’s disabilities affect the beneficiary’s capacity to manage VA funds.
* Only document reported changes.

Due diligence must be shown when attempting to contact the beneficiary.  Follow the steps in the table below when attempting to complete the monthly SDP review telephone calls.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Step** | **Action** |
| 1 | Attempt to call the beneficiary at the most recent telephone number(s) available at the beneficiary’s preferred day and time identified in the field examination.  If multiple telephone numbers are of record, attempt contact to each number.  A second attempt to each number at a different time must be made if the first attempt is unsuccessful.  If possible, leave a voicemail message requesting a call back.    ***Important***:  Do not include personally identifiable information in the voicemail.    Document the attempts on a *[VA Form 27-0820](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-27-0820-ARE.pdf)* for inclusion in the eFolder. | 3 | If no response to the final call attempts after 24 hours, document the attempts on *[VA Form 27-0820](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-27-0820-ARE.pdf)* for inclusion in the eFolder and send a *Locate Beneficiary* *Letter* advising the beneficiary that   * the hub has not received a response to the required monthly SDP review telephone call attempts, and * a fiduciary may be appointed if a second consecutive month is missed.   At a minimum, the letter must include   * the dates and times of the attempted calls, and * information on what hub personnel will do in establishing a fiduciary.   Establish a development activity to conduct the telephonic contact for the next month review in 14 days.    ***Important***:   * The current EP remains pending during this time. * Do not initiate the whereabouts unknown process as part of this procedure.  If the beneficiary’s whereabouts are ultimately unknown, this will be determined through the successor initial appointment (SIA) process as established in Step 4. |
| 4 | If Steps 1 and 2 must be repeated for a second consecutive month   * follow the procedures in [FPM, Part I, 2.A.3.b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153947/) to request an SIA, and * request cancelation of the pending EP for competency. |

**I.5.A.4.d . Documenting the SDP Scheduled Telephone Contact**

The information obtained during the telephonic contact with the SDP beneficiary must be documented on a *[VA Form 27-0820](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-27-0820-ARE.pdf)*.  Once completed, upload the *[VA Form 27-0820](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-27-0820-ARE.pdf)* to the eFolder.

***Important***:  At a minimum, the report must contain the following information:

* changes regarding the beneficiary’s circumstances that may affect the beneficiary’s ability to manage funds
* confirmation that monthly expenses were paid
* reasons for unpaid expenses, and
* a statement regarding the assessment of the beneficiary’s ability to continue managing funds.

**I.5.A.4.e . Sample: Report of Monthly Telephonic Contact**

The basic outline below is a sample that can be used in the body of *[VA Form 27-0820](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-27-0820-ARE.pdf)* when documenting the monthly telephone contact.  Additional information that is pertinent for the review should be included as necessary during the call.

**Monthly Report of Telephonic Contact for Beneficiary on Supervised Direct Payment**

Month of Review:  **{Provide the month being reviewed.}**

Person Conducting Review:  **{Name of hub individual conducting the monthly review.}**

* Supervising of Beneficiary:  **{List any changes in the beneficiary’s condition or circumstances that would affect the ability to manage VA funds.}**
* Supervision of funds:
  + Income:  Was the beneficiary aware of their source and amount of income?  *Yes*/*No*
    - If *no*, document the beneficiary’s reason for not knowing.
    - Document any changes in income sources or amounts to include any changes that will result in a change in the budget.
  + Expenses:  Was the beneficiary aware of their expenses?  *Yes/No*
    - If *no*, document the beneficiary’s reason for not knowing.
    - Confirm the previous month’s expenses from the prior contact were paid, document any unpaid expenses and the reason for the lapse in payment; and document any new expenses that must be included in the budget.
  + Assets:  Does the beneficiary have any assets and, if so, was the beneficiary aware of the assets and/or transfer of any assets?  *Yes/No*
    - If *yes*, document the balance on all assets from the initial appointment and/or previous monthly contact and inquire about the reason behind any decreases in assets if not apparent.
* Other:  **{Provide any additional details as necessary.}**
* Statement regarding the beneficiary’s ability to manage funds.

**I.5.A.4.f . Reassessment of Ability to Manage Funds**

Hub personnel will consider the information obtained about the beneficiary’s circumstances and financial information to reassess the beneficiary's ability to manage VA benefits during each monthly telephone contact.

Use the table below to determine the appropriate actions based on the reassessment.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **If the beneficiary ...** | **Then the hub will document the assessment and ...** |
| demonstrates the ability to manage funds | establish a diary to conduct the telephonic contact again in 30 days. |
| *does not* demonstrate the ability to manage funds | * follow procedures in [FPM, Part I, 2.A.3.b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153947/) to request an SIA, and * request cancelation of the pending EP for competency. |

Examples of when a fiduciary must be appointed immediately include situations where information was received indicating

* bills were late or underpaid when the beneficiary had enough funds to pay the expenses
* there have been large expenditures not for the benefit of the beneficiary
* the beneficiary’s medical situation has resulted in an increased risk of poor financial decision making, or
* excessive gifts or loans not consistent with the beneficiary’s past practices have been given.

***Reference***:  For more information on a misuse allegation from an SDP beneficiary, see [FPM, Part II, 3.A.2.b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153972/).

**I.5.A.4.g . Reviewing the Status of the Competency Decision**

When the beneficiary is appointed under SDP, the hub submits a *Supervised Direct Pay Assessment* and establishes the appropriate EP with the SDP special issue indicator so that the rating authority may make a competency decision.  The status of the pending decision must be reviewed, as SDP is limited to six months from the date of the appointment.

Prior to completion of the monthly telephonic review, hub personnel must review VA systems to determine the status of the EP and request expedited processing as the days pending approaches six months.

Use the table below to determine the appropriate action based on the status of the EP.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **If the EP …** | **And …** | **Then …** |
| is pending | it has been *less* than four months since the appointment | follow procedures in [FPM, Part I, 5.A.4.h](#4h) to establish a diary to contact the SDP beneficiary again in 30 days. |
| is pending | it has been *more* than four months since the appointment | * request the EP be expedited, and * follow procedures in [FPM, Part I, 5.A.4.h](#4h) to establish a diary to contact the SDP beneficiary again in 30 days. |
| was completed | the decision found the beneficiary incompetent | establish an SIA EP. |
| was completed | the decision found the beneficiary competent | follow the procedures in [FPM, Part I, 2.E.3.h](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153953/) for processing a beneficiary’s restored ability to manage financial affairs. |
| was not completed | it has been six months since the SDP appointment | establish an SIA EP. |

***References***:  For more information on

* controlling a *Supervised Direct Pay Assessment* and the appropriate EP, see [FPM, Part I, 2.E.3.c](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153953/)
* determining restored competency, see [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 6.A.4.b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177974/)
* processing rating decisions that conclude a beneficiary VA previously rated incompetent is now competent, see [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 6.D.6.a](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177977/)
* fiduciary removal when supervision requirements cease, see [FPM, Part II, 2.C.3.b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153971/), and
* establishing and routing EPs, see the *VBMS Core User Guide*.

**I.5.A.4.h . Follow-up Actions**

Complete the development activity associated with the telephone contact and close the EP.  Establish a follow-up telephone contact diary to contact the SDP beneficiary in 30 days from the date of the completed telephone contact.

***Note***:  When the appropriate diary is established, the telephone contact EP will automatically be created to allow for timely oversight.

If six months from the date of the SDP appointment, the beneficiary must either be removed from the program by a rating of competency, or a fiduciary must be appointed.  If a rating of competency is not received, remove the beneficiary from SDP by

* following the procedures in [FPM, Part I, 2.A.3.b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153947/) to request an SIA, and
* inputting a permanent note in VBMS stating that a fiduciary is being appointed due to no current rating of competency having been completed for a beneficiary within six months of placement in SDP status.
