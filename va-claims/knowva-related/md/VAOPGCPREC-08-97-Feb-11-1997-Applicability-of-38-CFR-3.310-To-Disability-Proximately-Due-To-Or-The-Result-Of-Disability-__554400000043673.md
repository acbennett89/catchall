 Office of General Counsel Precedent Opinion

**What the case is about**

May compensation be paid, pursuant to 38 C.F.R. § 3.310, for a disability which is proximately due to or the result of a disability for which compensation is payable under 38 U.S.C. § 1151?

**Impact on VBA**

Disability compensation may be paid, pursuant to 38 U.S.C. § 1151 and 38 C.F.R. § 3.310, for disability which is proximately due to or the result of a disability for which compensation is payable under section 1151.
