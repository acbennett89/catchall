**Part 01 Introduction**

**Section 1.01 Systems Overview**

1. TheFinancial Management System (FMS) is VA’s front-end financial accounting system. FMS is the system of record for VA’s General Ledger, and it supports the collection, processing, dissemination and reporting of financial information and transactions. FMS has bi-directional interfaces with multiple VBA systems that have payment information or financial accounting transactions. These VBA systems include c. – i. below.
2. The Integrated Financial and Acquisition Management System (iFAMS) is a VA-wide system that will gradually replace FMS. iFAMS is a cloud-hosted system that will support accurate and timely payment processing of VA financial transactions. The system provides the ability to track and monitor budget formulation to execution, payments to vendors and Veterans, financial reporting and other key processes. iFAMS is the mandatory financial system used for managing VBA general operating expense (GOE) financial activities by the VBACO business lines and staff offices, Regional Offices (RO), and other VBA facilities.
3. Finance and Accounting System (FAS) - FAS was a module within the Veterans Service Network (VETSNET).   FAS managed accounts receivables and financial accounting transactions at the detail level for compensation, pension and some Veteran Readiness & Employment (VR&E) benefits.  VETSNET interfaces with FMS at the summary level to update the general ledgers.
4. Enterprise Management of Payments, Workload, and Reporting (eMPWR)-VA – eMPWR replaced FAS on September 15, 2024, and has the same functionality as FAS along with new workload and workflow management capabilities.
5. Veterans Benefits Management System - Long Term Solution (VBMS-LTS) - This system establishes accounts receivable on Chapter 33 education debt, and interfaces with eMPWR-VA.
6. Centralized Administrative Accounting Transaction Systems (CAATS) - CAATS is used by Regional Offices (ROs) in preparing accounting and payment documents for processing. It interfaces with FMS nightly and downloads data from FMS daily. Its Accounts Receivable module allows users to establish accounts receivable for non-benefit debts that include vendor, employee payroll, employee travel, reimbursement, jury fees and station debts. *Note:* All the GOE related activities have been migrated to the Integrated Financial and Acquisition Management System (iFAMS) and should not be processed in CAATS.
7. Corporate Case Management System (CWINRS) - This system processes payment vouchers for most of the VR&E benefits. CWINRS integrates with CAATS for referral and payment transactions.
8. Insurance General Ledger System (IGLS) - IGLS is a system used by VBA to record and manage life insurance policies.  IGLS sends financial data to FMS daily with a monthly bidirectional interface to record obligations and expenses.

**Section 1.02 Other Accounts Receivable System**

1. Centralized Accounts Receivable System (CARS) is used by the Debt Management Center (DMC) to monitor, track, and record collections on debts under DMC’s jurisdiction.
2. Benefit debts Accounts Receivables established in VBA’s benefit systems are referred to CARS.
3. Veterans Account Management System (VAMS) is the working file, generated daily by CARS, to assist the DMC staff. VAMS retrieves and displays CARS master record data, processed transactions, and calculates administrative fees and interest due, in some cases.

**Section 1.03** **Charging Interest, Administrative Costs, and Penalties**

1. When permitted by law VA will charge interest and administrative costs on its debts. In addition to interest and administrative costs, VA will charge penalties on non-benefit debts.
2. In accordance with 38 USC § 5315, VA will not charge interest and administrative fees for the following:
   1. a loan, loan-guaranty, or loan-insurance program;
   2. disability compensation program;
   3. a pension program; or
   4. an educational assistance program

Refer to the [VA Financial Policy, Volume XII, Debt Management, Chapter 08](https://department.va.gov/financial-policy-documents/financial-document/chapter-08-interest-administrative-costs-and-penalty-charges/) 080502 Exceptions to Fees, Penalties, and Costs.

**Part 02 Responsibilities**

**Section 2.01 Debt Management Center**

DMC is responsible for the collection of accounts receivable (debts):

1. Debts arising from overpayments of compensation, pension, and education benefits
2. 35B burial debt
3. Chapter 31 47B debts
4. Chapter 32 – Post Vietnam Educational Assistance Program and Restored Entitlement Program for Survivors (REPS) (as of July 2018)
5. Debts as a result of default on VA guaranteed or direct home loans
6. GOE debts in iFAMS (except current employee salary debts that are under DFAS’s jurisdiction)

**Section 2.02 Regional Office Support Services Division Finance**

1. Regional Office (RO) Support Services Division (SSD) Finance activity is responsible for the collection of accounts receivable (debts), which are not managed by DMC.
2. The SSD Finance is responsible for the following accounts receivables (debts):

1. Station debt (see M24-3, Chapter 02)
2. Fiduciary debt (see M24-3, Chapter 03)
3. Restitution debt (see M24-3, Chapter 04)
4. Third party fraud cases (See M24-3, Chapter 05)
5. Chapter 31 credit memos submitted through the Invoice Payment Processing System (IPPS), and
6. Accounts receivable established prior to May 1, 1988, as a result of the death of the person entitled or increased due to the death of a payee who was receiving compensation or pension benefits at the time of death.

3. The SSD Finance activity is also responsible for managing accounts receivable 63C and 31J offsets.  SSD Finance activity will prepare a paper or electronic VA Form 4-1103A, Accounts Receivable Record with Charges (See attachment at the bottom of this Chapter) or equivalent to maintain these accounts receivables, which include:

1. Offsets that prevent duplication of education benefits for overlapping periods when a student changes from one benefit program to another.
2. Recurring SBP/RSFPP premium offsets at the Veteran’s or military service’s request.
3. Offsets for Veteran’s attorney fee (31J offset).
4. Offsets to collect debts under the RO’s jurisdiction when a payee has two different file/payee numbers.
5. Court ordered garnishments when Veterans waive all of or part of their military retirement pay to receive VA disability compensation benefits. The waived amount of military retired pay may be considered for garnishment.

**Part 03 Establishing Accounts Receivables (Debts)**

**Section 3.01 Fiscal Transactions for Non-Veteran Accounts Receivable**

1. Station debts not chargeable to a Non-Veteran without a file number, will be established in FMS through CAATS. Finance activities will follow instructions in the [M24-3 Chapter 02 Station Debt](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000197711/) and [M24-3 Chapter 03 Fiduciary Misuse Debt](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000197740/).
2. Overpayment of burial allowances and accrued benefits owed by a non-Veteran will be established in VETSNET, respectively using transaction code 04E. In order to accomplish this, an identification number must be assigned which will be used in place of a file number.  (Do not use a Veteran’s social security number as the file number.) The identification number will be assigned by the local office as follows:

1. Identification:  9
2. Last 2 digits of Station Number: 10
3. Serially Assigned Number:  001
4. Terminal Digit:  99
5. Payee Number:  30

Items 1,4 and 5 of this identification number are the same for all accounts. Item 2 will reflect the last two digits of the station number of the station having jurisdiction over the claims folder. Item 3 is a number serially assigned by the station beginning with 001. Each station will establish its own control to ensure continuity of numbers within the station. The file number of the case from which this overpayment originated will be entered in the Reference Number of FIST-F25 screen. Significant digits will be preceded by zeros to fill the nine spaces of the reference number.

**Section 3.02 Establishment of Receivables on Terminated Accounts or Deceased Veterans / Beneficiaries**

1. VBA will establish debts against deceased Veterans, beneficiaries, or any debtors (fiduciary, vendor, etc). When there are no accrued benefits / proceeds / returned payment available for offset **AND** no prospect of collecting from the beneficiary or the estate, the debt can be written off.  (For additional information, see [M24-3 Chapter 09 Debt Write-Off](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000198075/), section 1.01, c.3.)
2. At the time terminations are processed under VBA’s benefit systems, any receivables created by the transaction in an amount of less than $5, after application of any available offset deductions, will not be established in the accounts or records.
3. However, this rule does not apply to Combat Related Special Compensation (CRSC), Concurrent Retirement and Disability Payments (CRDP) and Audit Error Worksheet (AEW) debts.
4. Prior to the authorization of the award, the Veterans Service Representative will refer an AEW debt that has an amount previously paid by the Defense Finance and Accounting Service, Coast Guard, or Public Health Service to the station Finance to establish a receivable.
5. All field stations that process debts to offset C&P awards for CRSC/CRDP/AEW payments will process the debts of less than $5.00 in the same manner as they process the debts for more than $5.00.

**Section 4.02 Responding to Debtors' Inquiries**

1. **CARS/VAMS.** VBA benefit systems update CARS every processing cycle.  CARS reconciles with eMPWR-VA three times a week (except for Chapter 35 debts which are reconciled monthly) and eMPWR monthly.  As a result, VAMS has current principal balance and payment information available to selected employees in ROs. VAMS also shows if a waiver request is pending, interest charges, and payments DMC applies. (See Attachments Section below for the COWC VAMS User’s Guide).
2. **Regional Office SSD Finance**.  Any inquiry or evidence that affects DMC’s collection process will be referred to DMC immediately.  Debtors who call a RO to make a waiver of collection request will be told to send a written request with a [VA Form 5655, Financial Status Report](http://vaww.va.gov/vaforms/va/pdf/VA5655.pdf) to DMC directly. Similarly, debtors calling to report a bankruptcy, death, etc., will be asked to send documentation directly to DMC.  The mailing address for DMC is:

* U.S.  Department of Veterans Affairs
* Debt Management Center
* P.O. Box 11930
* St.  Paul, Minnesota 55111-1930

3. Inquiries received by DMC relating to benefit eligibility or non-receipt of checks will be referred to the RO of jurisdiction.  However, DMC will explain to debtors any matter concerning their debt collection process.
4. SSD Finance Offices should submit inquiries to DMC using the LEAF system https://leaf.va.gov/NATIONAL/189/dmcgencorrreq/.

**Section 4.03 Receipts and Cash Collections**

1. Checks received at ROs for debts under DMC’s jurisdiction will be mailed to DMC.  (For details, see [M24-2 Chapter 03 Agent Cashier and OTCnet](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000197753/))
2. Cash collections received at ROs for debts under DMC’s jurisdiction will be deposited and transferred to DMC (Station 389) through CAATS Deposits – Cash Receipt Sub-module and Cost-Revenue/Suspense Transfers – Between Stations Sub-module, respectively.  ALAC will forward the Journal Voucher created by CAATS to DMC.
3. When the RO receives a payment with no debt on the record, the payment will be deposited in the suspense account (36F3875) and transferred to DMC through CAATS Cost-Revenue/Suspense Transfers – Between Stations Sub-module once the debt has been established. If no debt is established, the RO will return the funds to the payee.
4. Payroll deductions will be transferred in the same manner.  If separate payments are received on different dates for the same debtor and are transferred on the same transaction, each payment must be shown separately with its applicable receipt date, i.e., the pay date for payroll deductions.
5. ROs are restricted from entering waiver (07D) or partial waiver transactions (07C) for debts under DMC’s jurisdiction.

**Section 4.04 VA Debts of VA Employees**

1. **Federal Employee Benefit Debt.**  Policy guidelines for collection of Federal employee benefit debts from VA employees are contained in [VA Policy, Volume XII, Chapter 3, Employee Debts](https://department.va.gov/financial-policy-documents/financial-document/chapter-03-employee-debt/).  In general, Federal employee benefit debts are under DFAS’s jurisdiction.  For details on payment methods for current employee debt established in DCPS, see [Volume XII, Chapter 3, Paragraph 030504](https://department.va.gov/financial-policy-documents/financial-document/chapter-03-employee-debt/) Collection Activities.
2. **VA Benefit Debt.**  These are the guidelines for Administrative Collection on any VA benefit debt owes by a Veteran / Beneficiary, who is also a VA employee:

1. Any [VA Form 4-1100, Agreement to Pay Indebtedness](http://vaww4.va.gov/vaforms/va/pdf/VA1100.pdf), or voluntary schedule of salary offsets, specifying the amount and periodic rate of repayment, and manner of repayment, will be forwarded to the Director, VA Debt Management Center, St. Paul, Minnesota 55111,  marking the envelope DO NOT OPEN IN MAIL ROOM, when the receivable is under DMC jurisdiction.
2. Any requests for withholding from an employee's monthly compensation or pension payment to apply against a VA benefit debt will be in writing.  The request will specify the amount to be withheld each month and the effective date.  The request will be similarly forwarded to the Director, VA Debt Management Center, St.  Paul, Minnesota 55111, marking the envelope DO NOT OPEN IN MAIL ROOM, when the receivable is under DMC jurisdiction.
3. Any remittance received from the employee at the ROs will follow the same procedures above in [Section 4.03](#_Toc98333014) Receipts and Cash Collections.
4. Any request for payroll deduction will be in writing.  Unless contradicted, the request will be honored.  Following receipt of the payroll deduction, [VA Form 4564, Transfer of Disbursing Authority](http://vaww.va.gov/vaforms/va/pdf/VA4564.pdf) will be prepared to transfer the amount to DMC.  The transfer document will be annotated with complete identifying data, including VA file number.   Multiple accounts may be included on the same VA Form 4564.

**Section 4.05 Requests for Waiver of Collection**

See [M24-3 Chapter 08 Committee on Waivers and Compromises](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000198074/) for details.

1. ROs will forward all waiver correspondence on benefit debts under DMC’s jurisdiction to DMC using the LEAF system <https://leaf.va.gov/NATIONAL/189/dmcgencorrreq/>. Stations should search for VA Form 4-1837 in VBMS to verify if the waiver has already been completed before sending requests to DMC.
2. If DMC receives and processes a waiver request within 30 days of the date of the first Notice of Indebtedness (NOI) letter, all collection action will be suspended pending a waiver decision.
3. If DMC identifies that a waiver request was received within 30 days of the first NOI but is being processed after collection by offset has already started, DMC will issue an 06B for the withholding and suspend further collection pending a waiver decision.
4. Only DMC will issue these refunds in order to avoid the potential for multiple 06B’s being processed.
5. DMC will also process any necessary refunds when a waiver granted decision is received, as well as processing the 07C.
6. There shall be no waiver consideration for Veteran, Non-Veteran, or Beneficiary for third party debts that results from the receipt of a benefit payment by a non-payee who has no claim or entitlement to the benefit payment. Third party debt is defined as an erroneous payment received by Veteran, Non-Veteran or Beneficiary that was not the intended payee.

**Section 4.06 Compromise Offers**

See [M24-3 Chapter 02 Station Debt](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000197711/) and [M24-3 Chapter 03 Fiduciary Misuse Debt](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000197740/) for details.

1. SSD Finance must forward all compromise offers for accounts under DMC jurisdiction to DMC.  ROs are not to make compromise decisions concerning debts under DMC jurisdiction.
2. Station Finance Officer determines if an offer on a debt is acceptable if it is less than or equal to $1,000.00, however, the Station Finance Officer can only accept a compromise offer of 50 percent or more on the total amount of the debt. Compromise offers of less than 50 percent that are not rejected by the Finance Officer will be referred to the COWC of jurisdiction for resolution.

1. If a debt is greater than $1,000 up to $100,000, the offer is sent to the St. Paul COWC.
2. If a debt is greater than $100,000, the offer is sent to the District Counsel of jurisdiction for review and approval prior to referral to the Department of Justice.

**Section 4.07 Adjustments in Offset of Benefit Payments**

See [M24-3 Chapter 07 Debt Offset Through Deduction](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000197715/) for details.

1. **Establishment or Change in Recurring Deduction (Offset).**  DMC and the SSD Finance will be able to enter a fiscal transaction 18 for establishing a recurring deduction and a fiscal transaction 18A for updating an established recurring deduction.
2. **Reduction in Offset Amounts.** Under special circumstances, SSD Finance may review accounts under DMC jurisdiction to determine if reductions in offset amounts would be appropriate.  These circumstances should be limited to those cases where extreme financial hardship appears imminent.  If reductions would be appropriate, the RO must advise DMC of their intended actions and forward copies of all financial documents used in support of their decisions.

*NOTE: Coordination is most important in this circumstance since many debtors request reductions in their offset amounts from the RO and DMC simultaneously.*

**Section 4.08 Separation Pay**

See [M21-1, Part VI, Subpart ii, Chapter 2 - Recoupment of Separation Benefits](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000179478/M21-1-Part-VI-Subpart-ii-Chapter-2-Recoupment-of-Separation-Benefits?query=seperation%20pay) for details.

1. Separation pay is paid to Veterans who are released or discharged from active-duty military service without requesting it. [**38 CFR 3.700**](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=551958c4c12b9d27d79484f158b3f4bb&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1700&rgn=div8) prohibits the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) from paying compensation to a Veteran who receives certain separation benefits from the Department of Defense (DoD) or Department of Homeland Security (if the Veteran served in the Coast Guard).
2. The action that the VBA takes to recoup separation benefits is not debt related; it is a recoupment from the Veteran’s disability benefit and cannot be paid back as a lump sum to the VA. Section **[10 U.S.C § 1174](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1174a&num=0&edition=prelim)** and **[38 C.F.R. § 3.700 (a)(5)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/chapter-I/part-3/subpart-A/subject-group-ECFRef6e3386723219b/section-3.700)** expressly prescribe how a separation payment will be offset or recovered, i.e., by withholding disability compensation. VA cannot circumvent those provisions by choosing to treat the amount of separation pay to be recouped as an overpayment or indebtedness to be recovered through any debt-collection methods. Overpayments and indebtedness result from the receipt of a benefit payment in excess of the amount due. This is not the case with the recoupment of separation pay based on receipt of disability compensation, as neither have been paid in excess of the amount due. By law and regulation, a Veteran may not pay off a separation pay recoupment balance in lieu of disability compensation payment deductions. Further, in accordance with **[U.S.C. § 1174(h)(1)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1174a&num=0&edition=prelim),** without a later qualification for retired or retainer pay, DoD does not participate in the recoupment of separation pay.
3. If a Veteran inquires on qualifying for a home loan from the VA while a recoupment of separation benefits is active, Veterans can contact the Home Loan Guaranty division via phone at 1-877-827-3702. They’re available from 8:00AM to 6:00PM Eastern Standard Time.
4. For information on researching separation recoupments to avoid duplicate separation recoupments, refer to the attachment titled Recouping Severance or Separation Pay.

**Section 4.09 Dispute on the Existence or Amount of Debt**

1. If VA receives a dispute on the existence or amount of a benefit debt, all collection action will be suspended pending a verification of the validity of the debt.
2. VA will resume collection action once an adverse decision is reached on the debtor's request for verifying the existence or amount of a benefit debt.
