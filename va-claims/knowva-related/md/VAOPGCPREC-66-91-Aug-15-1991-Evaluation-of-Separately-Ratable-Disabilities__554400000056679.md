Decision Assessment Document
 VAOPGCPREC 66-91, Aug 15, 1991, Evaluation of Separately Ratable Disabilities
 Office of General Counsel Precedent Opinion

**What the opinion is about:**

May the disability ratings assigned several separately ratable conditions of common etiology, none of which is evaluated at 100 percent, be combined so that a single rating of 100 percent can be established when the computation reaches 95 percent or more and may the remaining ratings for separately ratable conditions arising from the same etiology then be combined to achieve a 60 percent rating in order to establish entitlement to the special monthly compensation rate provided by 38 U.S.C. 1114(s) (formerly 314(s))?

**Impact on VBA:**

Manual M21-1, IV.ii.2.H was revised based on the General Counsel Precedent Opinion.

**Summary of the facts and findings:**

The threshold requirement for entitlement under 38 U.S.C. § 1114(s) is "a" disability rated as total.  If a veteran does not have a single service- connected disability rated as total (100 percent), he cannot be eligible for compensation at the 38 U.S.C. § 1114(s) rate.

See OGC opinion for facts.
