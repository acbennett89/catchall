All other entries on this chart reflecting a rate for children show the rate payable for children under 18 or helpless.  T**o find the amount payable to a 70%-disabled Veteran with a spouse and four children, one of whom is over 18 and attending school, take the 70% rate for a Veteran with a spouse and three children ($******2,226.45******) and add the rate for one school child ($246.00).  The total amount payable is $2,472.45.****

For example, a Veteran has an A/A spouse and two minor children and is 70% disabled.  Add $141.00 (additional for A/A spouse) to the rate for a 70%-disabled Veteran with dependency code 12 ($2,150.45). The total amount payable is $2,291.45.**
