**Decision Assessment Document**

***NOVA v.*** ***Secretary of Veterans Affairs***

**48 F.4th 1307 (September 20, 2022)**

**U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit (Federal Circuit)**

**What the case is about:**

A Veteran advocacy organization and several Veterans petitioned for review of the Department of Veterans Affairs' (VA) interpretation of diagnostic code (DC) provisions for disability benefits regarding prosthetic replacement of knee joints. The Federal Circuit ultimately determined that prior to February 7, 2021, DC 5055 did not exclude partial knee replacements, and thus held VA’s July 16, 2015, Knee Replacement Guidance interpretive rule to be arbitrary and capricious.

**Impact on VBA:**

The Adjudication Procedures Manual’s (M21-1) references to the historical July 16, 2015, Knee Replacement Guidance interpretive rule for claims with rating periods prior to February 7, 2021, will be removed and/or updated to comport with the Court’s decision.

**Summary of the facts and Court’s reasons:**

Petitioners asked the Federal Circuit to review the VA’s interpretation of two diagnostic codes (DCs): DCs 5055 and 5257, both found at 38 C.F.R. § 4.71a. VA had set out its interpretation of DC 5055 in Agency Interpretation of Prosthetic Replacement of a Joint, 80 Fed. Reg. 42,040 (July 16, 2015) (the “Knee Replacement Guidance” or “Guidance”), and in the Adjudication Procedures Manual M21-1 (M21-1) at section III.iv.4.A.6.a (the “Knee Replacement Manual Provision”).

For the reasons discussed below and over a dissenting opinion, the Federal Circuit concluded that the Knee Replacement Manual Provision is not a reviewable agency action. However, it also held that the Knee Replacement Guidance is arbitrary and capricious under the controlling precedent of *Hudgens v. McDonald,* 823 F. 3d 630 (Fed. Circ. 2016).

As relevant here, Petitioners challenged the July 16, 2015, interpretive rule VA published as Knee Replacement Guidance in the Federal Register that inserted an explanatory note into the Rating Schedule clarifying that DC 5055 was only applicable to total knee replacements. Substantive formal notice and comment rulemaking revised DC 5055 as part of the February 2021 update to the musculoskeletal DCs.

Regarding VA’s July 16, 2015, Knee Replacement Guidance interpretive rule, the Federal Circuit panel majority concluded that it could not overcome subsequent controlling precedent in *Hudgens*, which held that DC 5055 did not exclude partial knee replacements, and thus found VA’s July 16, 2015, Knee Replacement Guidance to be arbitrary and capricious. The majority determined that it was still bound by *Hudgens*’s conclusion that deference to VA’s interpretation of the Rating Schedule was not warranted where “numerous inconsistent rulings by the Board” foreclosed VA’s proffered interpretation of DC 5055. The majority also concluded that it remained bound by *Hudgens*’s application of the pro-Veteran canon to DC 5055. The dissenting judge disagreed that *Hudgens* dictated the outcome in this case and would have deferred to VA’s interpretation of DC 5055 as published in the July 16, 2015, Knee Replacement Guidance interpretive rule.

Effectively, this ruling means that adjudicators should rate any relevant partial knee joint replacement claim that is or was pending and not finally adjudicated as of September 20, 2022 (the date of *NOVA* decision) and that includes a rating period prior to February 7, 2021, under the pre‑ February 7, 2021, version of DC 5055 for rating periods prior to February 7, 2021. Consideration of 38 C.F.R. § 3.951 (“Preservation of disability ratings”) is necessary when rating subsequent periods. Rating Schedule changes made as a consequent of formal notice and comment rulemaking effective February 7, 2021, properly revised DC 5055 regarding prosthetic replacement of knee joints, and those current criteria should be utilized from February 7, 2021, again with due consideration to 38 C.F.R. § 3.951.
