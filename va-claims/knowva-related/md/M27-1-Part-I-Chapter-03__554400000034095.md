## 3.01 General Guidelines for Interactions [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: September 30, 2026

1. ### About Interactions

   The National Contact Centers (NCCs) and Public Contact Teams (PCTs) conduct timely, accurate,
   high-quality interactions with Veterans, Service members, Survivors, dependents, caregivers, and
   other stakeholders who contact the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) for benefits information and
   assistance.

   Interactions may be conducted in-person or via various electronic channels (i.e., telephone, video
   conference, Ask VA (AVA), chat).

   NCC and PCT employees must:

   * provide complete and accurate information
   * ensure they do not mislead the individual with whom they are interacting by providing
     information that is not verified in the system of records (subject to ID Protocol requirements
     in M27-1 I.3.06.)
   * actively listen to properly assist the individual
   * attempt to provide benefit information before referring the individual to general information
     resources (i.e. VA.gov and the [Federal Benefits for Veterans,
     Dependents, Survivors, and Caregivers](https://www.va.gov/opa/publications/benefits_book.asp) benefit book), and
   * ask clarifying questions to determine the correct course of action.
2. ### Thanking Individuals for Their Service

   Public Contact Representatives (PCRs) must thank the Veteran or Service member for their service (or
   the survivors/dependents/family members on behalf of the Veteran/Service member) at any point during
   the interaction.
3. ### Speaking in Absolutes

   There are many variables in the claims and appeal process for VA benefits and services. PCRs must
   refrain from speaking in absolute terms.

   Examples include (not all inclusive):

   | **Don't Say** | **Instead Say/Do** |
   | --- | --- |
   | You are/are not eligible for benefits. | You may file a claim for benefits and VA will make a decision. |
   | A decision on your claim will be made when… | Utilize status of claim/appeal scripts. |
   | VA always pays benefits on the 1st of the month. | Review the payment for the current month to verify pay date, amount, deductions, etc. |
   | You will get back pay. | The effective date of benefits will be determined as part of the decision on your claim. The effective date will determine the amount of any retroactive payment due. |
4. ### Providing Forms In-Person

   During in-person interactions, when a written request and/or VA form(s) is required to address the
   issue/concern, PCRs must offer to assist the requestor with completing and submitting the
   appropriate request/form(s) unless noted otherwise, according to M27-1 I.5.09.

## 3.02 Opening and Closing Interactions [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: September 30, 2023

1. ### Greeting

   To enhance the customer service experience and to ensure the account information is reflected on the
   screen from the beginning of each phone call, NCC PCRs are required to use one of the following
   greetings. The greeting used will depend on whether the caller successfully entered information in
   the Interactive Voice Response (IVR) system with Computer Telephony Integration (CTI).

   * Standard Greeting- No information in CTI:
     + VA/Department of Veterans Affairs
     + First name of agent (“This is [name]/[Name} speaking,” etc.)
     + To better/best assist you may I have your/the Veteran's claim/file number or Social
       Security number (SSN)?
     + Thank you (after receiving claim/file number or SSN)
     + How may I help you?
   * CTI Greeting:
     + VA/Department of Veterans Affairs
     + First name of agent (“This is [name]/[Name} speaking,” etc.)
     + To verify I have the correct record, may I have your/the Veteran's claim/file number or
       the last four digits of the Social Security number (SSN)?
     + Thank you (after receiving claim/file number or last four of SSN)
     + How may I help you?

   PCT PCRs must appropriately greet the customer, introduce themselves, solicit the Veteran's
   claim/file number or SSN if not already available, and ask how they may help the customer.

   *Note**s*:

   * Cisco Finesse screens must be visible on PCRs' monitors for quality review purposes.
   * If the PCR's greeting is interrupted, and the caller indicates the question is general in
     nature, the PCR is not required to request the SSN/claim number and is not required to
     subsequently thank the caller for providing that information.
2. ### Closing

   At the end of an interaction, PCRs are required to ask one of the following closing questions.

   * “Is there anything else I can help you with today?” or
   * “Have I answered all your questions today?” and
   * “Goodbye/Bye”

   *Exception**s**:*

   * For talkative customers, as a method of interaction control, PCRs may instead use a summary
     statement to close the interaction.
   * For escalated interactions, PCRs are not required to use the standard closing. PCRs should
     follow guidance in M27-1 I.2. for Escalated Calls/Supervisor Queue, Handling Harassing or
     Cursing Individual, and/or De-Escalation Techniques.

   *Note**s*:

   * PCRs are required to ask the question once per interaction, when applicable. PCRs are not
     required to repeat the closing question after any subsequent discussion.
   * “Have a nice/great day” or a similar sentiment must not be used during death-related
     interactions.

## 3.03 Systems/Computer Down [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: September 30, 2026

1. ### Field User Notification of Systems/Computer Down

   PCRs must provide the mandatory script when instructed via Office of Field Operations (OFO) Business
   Applications staff email. When systems are available Business Applications staff will send a
   subsequent email indicating use of the script must cease.

   PCRs may use the outlined additional suggested scripting, along with the alternate closing option,
   when the mandatory script is in effect.
2. ### Systems/Computer Down Scripting

   PCRs must utilize the following scripting as applicable.

   | **Mandatory Script** | *"VA, this is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_. I apologize for any inconvenience; unfortunately, our computer systems are currently unavailable. I can answer any general questions you may have."* |
   | --- | --- |

   | **Suggested Scripts** | Only one system is down/systems go down during the interaction | *"I apologize for any inconvenience; unfortunately, the system I need to access to answer your question(s) is currently unavailable."* |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   |  | Inquirer requests information that requires system access and VA.gov is available | *"With your VA.gov account, you can (insert action/information inquirer has requested) online. The VA.gov website is available at* [*https://www.va.gov/*](https://www.va.gov/)*."*  PCRs must provide information on VA.gov and how to obtain an account, if needed. |
   |  | Inquirer requests information that requires system access and VA.gov is unavailable | *"Unfortunately, I will need access to your information in order to (insert action/information inquirer has requested) so I am unable to assist you at this time."* |
   |  | Inquirer requests information on when the system will be back online, and timeframe is known | *"You may call back**/return/schedule a new appointment* *in \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ hours* *and* *we will be happy to assist you. Again, I apologize for any inconvenience."* |
   |  | Inquirer requests information on when the system will be back online, and timeframe is unknown | *"At this time, we do not know when the system will be back online. Again, I apologize for any inconvenience."* |
   | **Alternate Closing Option****s** | If inquirer asks a general question to which the PCR has provided a complete response | *"Do you have any additional general questions I can help you with today?"* |
   |  | If inquirer indicates they do not have a general question, or the question posed is not general in nature | *"I apologize for the inconvenience. Thank you for* *contacting us**."* |

## 3.04 Recording Interactions in CRM/UD-O [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: September 30, 2026

1. ### Requirement to Record Interactions

   NCC and PCT employees with access to the Customer Relationship Management Unified Desktop Optimized
   (CRM/UD-O) system are required to utilize CRM/UD-O to manage and record all interactions.
2. ### Interaction Types/Subtypes

   CRM/UD-O users must categorize interactions by selecting the applicable interaction type and subtype
   from the list of interaction types available.

   In instances where mandatory interaction types and subtypes are required, CRM/UD-O users must use the
   identified types. PCRs must not edit system notes documenting these mandatory interaction type and
   subtypes.

   Each unique issue must be categorized by type/subtype for interactions with multiple issues. PCRs do
   not need to categorize the same issue multiple times when one type/subtype covers multiple actions
   of the same kind (i.e. multiple letters are requested).

## 3.05 Sensitive Records [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: September 30, 2026

1. ### About Sensitive Records

   Certain records are assigned a level of sensitivity, which restricts access to the records, based on
   the characteristics of the Veteran, their beneficiaries, or status as an employee of the Department
   of Veterans Affairs.

   PCRs must not reference the sensitive level of the individual's record unless directly asked by the
   individual.

   *Important*: If the interaction involves an emergency/crisis, PCRs must speak to their coach
   prior to initiating the referral request typically required by M27-1 I.3.05.b. and c. to determine
   the appropriate course of action. Standard referrals to the Veteran Crisis Line should be
   considered, when appropriate.
2. ### Sensitive Level 7

   Within the NCCs and PCTs, records with a Sensitive Level 7 must be referred to a PCR with Sensitive 7
   system access.

   For interactions requiring access to VA records,

   * NCC PCRs must transfer the individual to the Sensitive 7 queue.
   * PCT PCRs must follow local procedures for referring the individual to another PCT PCR with
     Sensitive 7 access.
3. ### Sensitive Level 8 or Higher

   Within the NCCs and PCTs, records with a Sensitive Level 8 or above must be referred to the
   Restricted Access Claims Center (RACC). The RACC operates the St. Paul Customer Care Line,
   1-888-232-2571, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. CT.

   NCC and PCT PCRs must promote the use of VA.gov for future requests if the individual's issue can be
   addressed through utilization of VA.gov. PCRs must advise of steps to obtain a VA.gov account, as
   needed.

   PCRs must follow the guidance in the below table for individuals with a record marked as Sensitive 8
   or above who have an inquiry that requires access to their record.

   | **If…** | **Then…** |
   | --- | --- |
   | the interaction is during St. Paul Customer Care Line operating hours | * NCC PCRs must provide the St. Paul Customer Care Line telephone number and   transfer the call to the care line queue. * PCT PCRs must   + provide the St. Paul Customer Care Line telephone number and transfer     the call to the care line queue during telephone interactions, and   + provide the St. Paul Customer Care Line telephone number and operating     hours during in-person or video interactions. |
   | The interaction is outside St. Paul Customer Care Line operating hours | NCC and PCT PCRs must  * provide the St. Paul Customer Care Line telephone number and operating hours, * submit a request through the Callback Request SharePoint, and * advise someone will call them within 24-48 hours and that call backs are made   between 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. CT. The individual may request a specific   callback date and time and this information must be entered in the SharePoint   entry. |
4. ### Status of Request for Callback

   PCRs must follow the guidance in the below table for individuals requesting the status of a
   previously submitted callback request.

   | **Step** | **Action** |
   | --- | --- |
   | 1 | Enter the SSN/file number of the Veteran in the [Sensitive 8/9 Callback Status Check Site](https://dvagov.sharepoint.com/sites/VBABASqualitytraining/SiteAssets/requestStatus.aspx). |
   | 2 | If the status is:  * Pending: Advise the callback request is pending and will be completed as quickly   as possible. If during St. Paul Customer Care Line operating hours, follow   procedures in M27-1 I.3.05.c. for a transfer/referral to the care line queue. * Request Complete: Advise the callback was completed. If the individual is   requesting to speak with the St. Paul Customer Care Line again, follow   transfer/referral procedures in M27-1 I.3.05.c. * Unable to Complete: Advise the callback was attempted but was unable to be   completed. Follow procedures in M27-1 I.3.05.c. for a transfer/referral to the   St. Paul Customer Care Line queue. |
   | 3 | If the individual's issue can be addressed through utilization of VA.gov, promote the use of VA.gov for future requests. Advise of steps to obtain a VA.gov account, as needed. |
5. ### Pardons and Commutations

   PCRs must advise Veterans seeking assistance in restoring benefits based on Proclamation 10887 to
   submit:

   * A written statement noting the receipt of a Presidential Pardon and requesting their benefits
     adjusted accordingly, and
   * A copy of the Certificate of Pardon
     + **NOTE:** VA Form 21-4138, Statement in Support of Claim, is sufficient to
       submit a claim to reinstate benefits.

   PCRs must advise the Veteran to submit the required statement and evidence according to Centralized
   Mail Processing.

   The St. Paul Regional Office Restricted Access Claims Center (RACC) is responsible for processing all
   cases related to Presidential Pardons and Commutations.

## 3.06 Identification Protocol (IDP) [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: February 2, 2026

1. ### About Identification Protocol

   First party individuals have an inherent right under the Privacy Act to their own information, or to
   the information of the person they represent. PCRs must ask required ID Protocol questions to verify
   that they are speaking to the first party, or an authorized individual, prior to divulging protected
   information or making changes to a record.

   Protected information includes anything pulled from a living Veteran's/beneficiary's record
   *except* the current monthly benefit amount. For a deceased Veteran, protected information is
   anything pulled from the record *except* Veteran status, dates of service, branch of service,
   and location of gravesite in a national cemetery. Records of deceased Veterans *may* be
   released to next of kin if disclosures will not be injurious to the physical or mental health of the
   person on whose behalf the information is sought or cause repugnance or resentment toward the
   decedent.
2. ### ID Protocol Requirements

   All ID Protocol questions must be completely answered directly by the first party inquirer. If the
   first party provides verbal permission to speak to a third party, the first party must still answer
   the basic AND enhanced (when applicable) ID Protocol questions.

   Powers of attorney (POA), Veteran Service Officers (VSO), County Veteran Service Officers (CVSO), VA
   assigned payees (Fiduciary, Guardian, Custodian), and appointed private attorneys or agents are
   considered first-party inquirers and must be asked the same ID protocol questions as if they were
   the Veteran or beneficiary.

   *Note*: ID protocol is not required for

   * VA employees
   * Individuals reporting a first notice of death
   * Claimants submitting an initial intent to file (ITF) when there is no record, and
   * Third party development letter recipients.
     + VA Employees must provide a business need for the requested information and be verified
       in Outlook.
     + PCRs must ask for enough information to be sure the correct Veteran's record is
       accessed.
     + PCRs may only discuss the letter received with the third party.

   Responses to ID protocol requirements must be verified in the system of records. After asking

3. ### Authorized Third Parties/21-0845

   For interactions with authorized third parties, PCRs must review the record for a valid VA Form
   21-0845, *Authorization to Release Information to a Third Party*.

   Authorized third parties must verify Identification Protocol questions as if they are the first-party
   inquirer, along with the addition of the security question listed on the completed VA Form 21-0845.
   PCRs may provide protected information to the authorized third party subject to limitations listed
   on the VA Form 21-0845.

   *Note**s*:

   * More than one security question answer populated on the VA Form 21-0845 does not invalidate the
     form; however, when conducting IDP, PCRs must select only one question and the authorized third
     party must successfully provide the answer to the selected question.
   * If the VA Form 21-0845 has all questions answered, but only one question with a “X” next to it,
     the PCR can still select any question with an answer.
   * Provided answers do not have to correspond exactly to the selected question. Examples:
     + The question selected is, “The city and state your mother was born in”, and the answer
       is, “Minneapolis”, then the form is considered valid and the third party would need to
       provide “Minneapolis” to successfully pass IDP. The third party would not need to
       provide “Minneapolis, Minnesota”.
     + The question selected is, “The name of the high school you attended”, and the answer is,
       “Dodge Charger”, then the form is considered valid and the third party would need to
       provide “Dodge Charger” to successfully pass IDP. Providing only “Dodge” or “Charger”
       would not be sufficient.
   * If the VA Form 21-0845 is submitted once, then resubmitted again with changes to the document
     (whiteout, lined out text, etc.) but no new signature and date, the new form is not valid
     because the original signature is not certifying the updated information.

   PCRs must offer information about the process to appoint an authorized third party if there is
   indication of the need for assistance on the part of the claimant or beneficiary.

   PCRs must document revocation of a current, valid VA Form 21-0845 on VA Form 27-0820, *Report of
   General Information* and route according to M27-1 I.5.07.

   If the most recent VA Form 21-0845 on file is invalid, PCRs must review the record to determine if a
   letter has been sent notifying the first and/or third party the form does not contain substantially
   complete information required to authorize access. If the letter has not been sent PCRs must submit
   VA Form 21-0820 according to M27-1 I.5.07 to document the 21-0845 is invalid. PCRs must advise the
   third party to resubmit the VA Form 21-0845.

   Additional information about the VA Form 21-0845 and required criteria is located in [M21-1
   I.i.3.](https://vaww.vrm.km.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_kanew/help/agent/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001034/content/554400000181479/M21-1-Part-I-Subpart-i-Chapter-3-Other-Information-Disclosures) Information about proper signatures on VA Forms is located in M21-1, II.i.2.B.
4. ### Accredited Representative Portal (ARP)

   The Accredited Representative Portal (ARP) is a secure online tool for accredited representatives to
   efficiently establish representation and manage their work supporting Veterans through the benefits
   claims process. VA will continue expanding the functionality of ARP by enhancing and building upon
   features to continuously improve the portal and meet the evolving needs of our users.

   Actions for Accredited Representatives:

   * Review and Process Requests: Accredited Representatives must ensure requests for representation
     are reviewed and processed accordingly and that any pending disability compensation or
     dependency claims are submitted.
   * For Benefits Delivery at Discharge (BDD) claims, during the VA Form 21-526EZ submission process,
     representatives must ensure they select the checkbox to indicate it is a BDD claim. For BDD
     claims, there will be an option to upload a Separation Health Assessment (SHA) - Part A.
   * Intent to File (ITF): Representatives can submit ITF requests through the portal. The system
     will confirm the
     representative has authorization and a pending ITF is not on file prior to ITF submission in the
     ARP.

   User Access

   A Login.gov or ID.me account is required to sign in to the ARP. If a representative has previously
   used ID.me to sign in to SEP, they will be able to use the same ID.me account to sign in to the ARP.

   Users also need to be an accredited VSO representative, attorney, or claims agent to gain full access
   to ARP features. When a representative signs in to the ARP, it will verify whether the user has an
   active accreditation in OGC's database.

   * If there is an active OGC accreditation, the user is “authorized” and access to portal features
     will be granted.
   * If there is not an active OGC accreditation, the user is “unauthorized” and access to portal
     features will be restricted.

   PCRs must respond to questions about the ARP using the information in this block and the ARP Support
   Line Guide. For issues that cannot be answered using these resources, advise the representative to
   submit an email request for assistance to RepresentativePortalHelp@va.gov.

   When form submissions fail to process PCRs must:

   * Offer to complete an intent to file according to M27-1, I.3.12.
   * Advise the Veteran's representative of alternate submission options (i.e. VA.gov as the

## 3.08 Security PIN [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: February 2, 2026

1. ### About the Security PIN

   Veterans and survivor beneficiaries may utilize an optional Security PIN for processing changes to a
   payment address or direct deposit account through the NCC and PCT. The security PIN is currently
   unavailable for apportionees or other dependents in receipt of benefits and for incompetent Veterans
   and beneficiaries. Currently, the Security PIN may only be established and changed through the
   NCC/PCT.

   If a Security PIN is of record, all first party requestors authorized to make updates to a payment
   address or direct deposit information (i.e., authorized third parties, etc.) must provide the PIN as
   mandated by ID Protocol requirements. CRM/UD-O currently allows only one Security PIN to be
   established per record. If there is more than one survivor beneficiary, and a PIN is of record, PCRs
   must verify which beneficiary the PIN belongs to by reviewing the CRM/UD-O Solicitation tab and
   system notes.

   *Exceptions:* PCRs must enter a note documenting non-solicitation of a bank account due to one
   of the reasons below. PCRs are not required to promote direct deposit to a bank account on:

   * interactions where the individual already expressed intent to add direct deposit to a bank
     account

2. ### Unbanked Beneficiary Review Mandatory Scripting

   PCRs must use the following mandatory script to solicit for direct deposit to a bank account:

   *“I see that you are currently receiving benefit payments via methods other than direct deposit to
   a bank account. We would like to encourage you to use direct deposit to receive your VA
   benefits. Direct deposit keeps your money safe and protected and you could receive your payment
   faster. I would be happy to assist you with updating your records on this call.”*

   If the Veteran/beneficiary is uncomfortable completing ID Protocol, PCRs must use the following
   mandatory script:

   *“I understand that you may not be comfortable verifying your information on this call. You can
   make the update on VA.gov or you can call us back at (800) 827-1000 Monday through Friday,

   The Veterans Benefits Banking Program (VBBP) offers VA beneficiaries – including those who have been
   unable to open bank accounts in the past – the opportunity to deposit their benefit funds directly
   into existing or new bank accounts offered by participating banks or credit unions and offers access
   to additional financial counseling and education.

   PCRs must refer beneficiaries interested in learning more or applying for a bank account to <https://benefits.va.gov/banking> and <https://www.veteransbenefitsbanking.org/>.
   PCRs may need to locate and provide the telephone number for participating banks to beneficiaries
   without computer access.

   PCRs must follow existing direct deposit update procedures to establish or update direct deposit
   information.

## 3.12 Intent to File and Request for Application [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: September 30, 2026

1. ### Intent to File

   Claimants must file a complete claim, using the prescribed standard form, before a claim will be
   accepted and established in the system. A complete claim is a completely filled-out standard form.
   Any communication that is not a complete claim will be considered a request for the proper
   application form.

   Claims cannot be documented by VA employees. Employees can, however, complete an intent to file (ITF)
   a claim.

   An individual's statement of his or her “intent to file a claim” must contain sufficient biographical
   information to identify the individual and they must submit it using one of the following methods:

   * initiation of an electronic claim by the claimant via VA.gov or by a representative via the
     Accredited Representative Portal (ARP)
   * written on the intent to file form (21-0966, *Intent to File a Claim for Compensation and/or
     Pension, or Survivors Pension, and/or DIC*), or
   * orally communicated to VA call center or other public contact personnel.

   If the individual submits the ITF in one of these three ways, it secures the individual's potential
   effective date and provides the claimant up to one year to complete the appropriate standard claim
   form and gather evidence to support the claim. Upon receipt of an intent to file, VA will notify the
   claimant of the appropriate standard claim form required. If a claimant submits a communication that
   does not meet the requirements of an ITF, VA will still notify the claimant of the standard claim
   form, if appropriate, but no potential effective date will be secured.

   *Notes*:

   * PCT employees: If a claimant visits an office in-person and is ready to file the standard claim
     form for the benefit they are seeking, provide the claimant with the standard form to complete
     and accept the claim. Advise the claimant on the benefits of electronic filing and the
     requirements of the Fully Developed Claim process. For claimants who are not yet ready to file
     the standard claim form, follow intent to file procedures.
   * ITF does not apply to Higher Level Reviews or Appeals to the Board of Veterans' Appeals.
   * ITF does apply to claims filed under 38 U.S.C. 1151. For more information about 1151 benefits
     see M21-1 VIII.iv.6.
   * Intent to file for benefits means the claimant has stated a desire to apply for benefits. Simply
     asking about a benefit or eligibility criteria is not enough to establish intent.
   * Authorized third parties appointed by VA Form 21-0845 are not able to submit an ITF on behalf of
     the claimant. PCRs must advise the authorized third party of the options for the claimant to
     submit the ITF.
     + Exception: The Authorized third party submits VA Form 21-0972, Alternate Signer
       Certification, according to M21-1 II.i.2.B.3.c.
   * ITFs can be submitted in CRM/UD-O without the claimant passing Standard Identification Protocol
     (IDP) as long as PCRs are certain that they are submitting the ITF for the correct record. If
     not, follow procedures for Veterans and claimants without a record in the below table.

   When a claimant or their representative (fiduciary, appointed Veteran Service Organization (VSO), or
   VA recognized attorney) expresses the intent to file a claim, PCRs must take the following actions.

   | **Step** | **Action** |
   | --- | --- |
   | **1** | Search for a record for the claimant.  * If no record exists, go to the next Step. * If a record exists, skip to Step 5. |
   | **2** | Open a copy of the 21-0966, *Intent to File a Claim for Compensation and/or Pension, or Survivors Pension and/or DIC*. Fill out the 21-0966 on the claimant's behalf. All fields must be populated completely, including the PCR's name in the signature block. Save the form as a PDF to your desktop. |
   | **3** | Upload the completed 21-0966 to the Centralized Mail Upload Tool or, if the Upload Tool is unavailable, fax to the Centralized Mail fax lines. |
   | **4** | * Advise the claimant that a standard form must be submitted within one (1) year   of the date the intent to file was documented in order to retain the potential   effective date. Promote the use of the Fully Developed Claim (FDC) program and   answer any questions regarding the claims process.   + A claimant filing a compensation and/or pension claim must be directed     to VA.gov to apply online. Provide instructions on VA.gov procedures, as     needed.   + A claimant filing a survivor benefit claim, or a claimant who does not     wish to use VA.gov, must be directed to the standard forms.   + All claimants should be encouraged to seek the assistance of a VSO when     filing a claim. VSOs may complete the standard claim form on behalf of     claimants using the ARP. * Proceed no further. |
   | **5** | Determine the type of claim being filed.  * If the claimant is requesting a Board Appeal or Higher-Level Review of a closed   claim (or contention on that claim)   + no ITF is needed, and   + proceed no further. * If the claimant is requesting an initial claim (original claim/new/claim for   increase) or Supplemental Claim, go to Step 6. |
   | **6** | Categorize the interaction as:  Type: Claim  Subtype: ITF/Generate ITF |
   | **7** | Determine if an ITF is already of record for the type of benefit the claimant is planning to claim (i.e., compensation, pension, or survivor benefits).  * If an active ITF is of record for the benefit type sought, proceed to Step 8. * If an active ITF is not of record, skip to Step 9. The ITF statuses are as follows:  *Active* - complete ITF received, pending receipt of complete claim for corresponding benefit  *Duplicate* - ITF received for same benefit as another active ITF  *Expired* - One year passed after ITF received and no claim for corresponding benefit received  *Claim Received* - complete claim for corresponding benefit received within one year of ITF  *Incomplete* - information on ITF not sufficient for acceptance |
   | **8** | Inform the claimant that an additional ITF is not needed, but the appropriate standard form is required. Provide the ITF date as shown in the system (month, day, year).  * If the claimant has not yet submitted the standard form, no further action is   needed. * If the claimant has already submitted the standard form for the type of benefit   sought, but is adding a contention to the claim, proceed to step 9. |
   | **9** | Input the intent to file data into the system. |
   | **10** | Advise the claimant that a standard form must be submitted within one (1) year of the date the intent to file was documented to retain the potential effective date. Promote the use of the Fully Developed Claim (FDC) program (except for Supplemental Claims) and answer any questions regarding the claims process.  * A claimant filing a compensation and/or pension claim must be directed to VA.gov   to apply online as the fastest option. Provide instructions on VA.gov   procedures, as needed. * A claimant filing a survivor benefit claim, or a claimant who does not wish to   use VA.gov, must be directed to the standard forms. * All claimants should be encouraged to seek the assistance of a VSO when filing a   claim. VSOs may complete the standard claim form on behalf of claimants using   the ARP. |

   To request correction of improperly entered ITF data more than 24 hours after submission, refer the
   case to the National Service Desk Solution Center by utilizing the YourIT system. Within 24 hours of
   submission, ITF data is editable in VBMS.

   For additional information on intent to file, see [M21-1

4. ### Status of Claim Expectations

   When providing status of a pending claim(s), PCRs must:

   * clarify which claim(s) the individual is inquiring about, if multiple claims are pending
   * review all systems/notes for relevant information
   * discuss open tracked items (including requests for federal records that have expired but not yet
     been received by VA)
   * review and provide pending exam/Disability Benefit Questionnaire (DBQ) information including
     appointment dates and times
   * not release claim-level suspense dates and/or reasons
   * provide the full status of claim script if an individual inquires about a timeframe for
     completion after providing the Repeat Inquirer script
   * ensure the correct status script is provided, regardless of what is suggested by CRM/UD-O , and
   * finish providing the required script, at any point during the interaction, after an interruption
     by the individual.

   **Note:** If the inquirer states they do not wish to hear the script, the PCR is not
   required to provide the script.

## 3.16 Status of Compensation & Pension Exam [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: September 30, 2026

1. ### Disability Benefit Questionnaires

   Disability Benefits Questionnaires (DBQs) are forms created for use in the evaluation process for
   disability benefits. The DBQ can be completed by VHA, VA contract clinicians, and private
   physicians.

   PCRs may direct claimants or appellants to the [public DBQ site](https://www.benefits.va.gov/compensation/dbq_publicdbqs.asp) upon
   request for the form(s).
2. ### Compensation and Pension (C&P) Examination Information

   C&P exams can be conducted by Veteran Health Administration (VHA) locations or by contracted
   examiners.

   PCRs must use the tracked items and exam information to provide the status of a C&P exam request.

   When PCRs receive an inquiry regarding a C&P exam, they are expected to:

   * Review the appropriate system (CRM/UD-O and/or vendor site) and provide the type of appointment
     requested, appointment location, date and/or time.
   * Provide the telephone number and offer to transfer the caller, if applicable, to the appropriate
     facility when the claimant or appellant requests the exam be rescheduled, and it is prior to the
     original appointment date or when the PCR cannot access information to answer the question about
     the contract exam.

   PCRs must not advise inquirers that they will not need to report to exams when the exam request
   indicates the clinician can utilize the ACE process. The ACE process allows the clinician to review
   the evidence and exam request and then determine if an appointment with the claimant is required.
3. ### Rescheduling C&P Exams

   When a first party requests to reschedule an upcoming C&P exam, PCRs must take the following
   actions:

   | **Step** | **Action** |
   | --- | --- |
   | 1 | Advise the individual to attempt to reschedule the exam by contacting the VA medical facility or C&P vendor, provide the telephone number, and offer to transfer callers. |
   | 2 | Complete VA Form 27-0820, using the mandatory "Exam Reschedule- Future Date" CRM/UD-O quick write and route according to M27-1 I.5.07. |

   To request a missed C&P exam be rescheduled, PCRs must take the following actions:

   | **Step** | **Action** |
   | --- | --- |
   | 1 | Review the record to determine if the claim is still Open:  * If No, proceed to Step 2. * If Yes, proceed to Step 3. |
   | 2 | Advise the individual to submit VA Form 20-0995, Decision Review Request: Supplemental Claim.  Take no further action in this table. |
   | 3 | Generate VA Form 27-0820. It must include the reason the Veteran/claimant missed the exam and if they are willing to attend the rescheduled exam.  Route the VA Form 27-0820 according to M27-1 I.5.07. |
   | 4 | Advise the individual the request will be reviewed and, if good cause for missing the exam was demonstrated, the exam will be re-requested. |
4. ### Bills for Vendor C&P Exams

   If a Veteran or Service member receives a bill(s) related to a C&P exam conducted by a contract
   vendor, PCRs must advise the Veteran or Service member to immediately contact the vendor
   involved (listed on the appointment letter for that examination) for resolution.

   The Veteran should provide to the vendor:

   * A copy of the bill received and
   * Identify the date and location of the appointment.
5. ### Contract Exam Vendor Complaints

   If a claimant has a bad experience with the provider at their claim exam, or wants to submit a
   complaint about a specific provider practice, they can report their issue using the following
   methods:

   * Give feedback on the contractor's customer satisfaction survey.
   * Write a letter and submit it as part of the claim file.
   * Call the contractor who scheduled the exam.
   * Contact NCC via telephone or Ask VA (AVA).
   * Contact the local Regional Office.

   When the NCC or PCT receives a complaint, PCRs must:

   * Document the request on VA Form 27-0820, *Report of General Information*, and route
     according to M27-1 I.5.07. Including the following information:
     + Examination Vendor/Diagnostic Testing Vendor
     + Details of complaint including:
       - Name(s) of involved parties
       - Date(s) of exam(s)
       - Exam location

   **Note:** AVA Responders may copy/paste complaint details from Submitter Question field.

   For any allegations of discrimination, harassment or assault, follow guidance in M27-1 I.2.06.

## 3.17 Releasing Information from Claim Decision Notification Letters [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: September 30, 2023

1. ### Claimants Who Have Not Received Notice

   PCRs may release the following information from decision notices for claims in a Closed status to
   individuals who have not received the notice via mail or downloaded it via VA.gov:

   * Combined service-connected and/or non-service-connected rating percentage
   * Monthly payment amount
   * Retroactive payment amount and payment date

   PCRs may confirm the information eligible for release verbally or in writing. PCRs may alter standard
   letter templates, ensuring the information released is accurate and verified in the record.

   PCRs must not provide the basis for the decision or any other details from the decision notice,
   including effective dates.

   PCRs must ask the claimant if they have a VA.gov account.

   * If yes, advise that the decision notice can be downloaded using their account.
   * If no, advise of the benefits of having an account and advise of steps to obtain a VA.gov
     account.

   *Exceptions*:

   * If requested by a visually impaired claimant, PCRs must explain the substance of the decision
     notice by providing a general summary and responding to any questions. If a visually impaired
     claimant further requests the decision notice be read to them, PCRs must read the letter in its
     entirety and answer any related questions. Visually impaired claimants are identified with a
     *"Blind"* flash in the system.
   * This guidance does not apply to Board of Veterans' Appeals decisions prior to VBA's
     implementation and closure of the corresponding end product.
2. ### Claimants Who Have Received Notice

   For claimants who received the decision notice, PCRs may discuss the notification letter in its
   entirety and must answer any related questions.

7. ### Limited Payability

   Paper checks can be cashed up to one year from the date on the check. If a payment was not negotiated
   within that time, it will automatically be cancelled under limited payability statutes. PCRs must
   confirm the payment information of record is accurate and submit a request for the payment to be
   rereleased on a VA Form 27-0820, *Report of General* *Information* routed according to
   M27-1 I.5.07.
8. ### Requests for an Audit

   PCRs must attempt to verify and explain the correct amount of VA payments, including retroactive
   payment(s), when requested. PCRs must review payment information (e.g. previous payment amounts,
   debts, severance/separation payment recoupments, previous notification letters, etc.) and provide
   confirmation of correct payment amounts. PCRs must not offer an audit in place of explaining a
   payment.

   * If the payment is verified as correct and the requestor understands the explanation, no further
     action is required.
   * If the payment is verified as correct and the inquirer still wants to submit an audit request,
     PCRs must advise the individual to submit the request in writing with specific timeframes and
     the purpose for the audit.
     + There is no specific form required for an audit request.
   * If the payment is not correct, PCRs must generate VA Form 27-0820 to request the correct amount
     be paid, but do not use the “Audit Request” quick write.
     + Send the VA Form 27-0820 to a Designated Management Official (DMO) via encrypted email
       for review.
   * If there is not enough information to verify the payment was correct or incorrect, PCRs must
     follow the below procedures to submit the Audit Request.

   Note**:**PCRs do not necessarily need to provide a line-by-line
   calculation to the inquirer. If the inquiry is to confirm a retroactive payment was the correct
   amount, and PCRs can verify the amount was paid correctly, then an explanation of how VA calculates
   retroactive payments may be sufficient.

   PCRs must not offer to submit an audit of a Veteran/beneficiary's award when system information
   provides confirmation the Veteran/beneficiary was paid correctly or incorrectly. If system
   information is insufficient to verify the amount paid, PCRs must:

   * Use the "Audit Request" CRM/UD-O quick write when generating the VA Form 27-0820,
     Report of General Information.
     + PCRs must include the purpose for the audit request in the VA Form 27-0820 and include
       what system records were reviewed (i.e. Payment History, timeframe, etc.)
   * Send the VA Form 27-0820 to a Designated Management Official (DMO) via encrypted email for
     review.

   PCRs must submit VA Form 27-0820 when a Veteran/beneficiary wants to withdraw a previously submitted
   financial audit request. The VA Form 27-0820 must clearly identify the audit request the individual
   wants to withdraw. PCRs must route this VA Form 27-0820 according to 27-0820 Routing Procedures

   Required DMO Actions

   * Review the VA Form 27-0820 for completeness and verify the purpose for the submission is clear.
   * Review System Information and confirm the submitted 27-0820 is valid (i.e. confirmation of
     correct payment could not be confirmed with available information or incorrect payment).
     + For NCC DMOs, review the call in Calabrio to ensure the PCR correctly assessed the
       situation and provided correct information and explanations to the individual.
     + For NCC and PCT DMOs, if the PCR did not take correct actions, complete service recovery
       efforts and remedial training, as needed.
   * If the PCR took correct actions and the 27-0820 is valid, upload the VA Form 27-0820 to the
     Centralized Mail Portal.

## 3.21 Debts [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: September 30, 2026

1. ### Advising Beneficiaries on Debts

   If inquiries are received, PCRs must advise beneficiaries on their options regarding the debt.
   Beneficiaries can manage their debt or find out about payment options, including payment plans,
   compromise offers and waivers at: https://www.va.gov/manage-va-debt/.

   PCRs are responsible for

   * determining if a debt exists and the debt amount/balance
   * providing an explanation of the debt
   * referring inquiries to the appropriate business lines
   * determining if a payment plan has been established
   * providing the payment plan amount, and
   * providing options for repaying, reducing, or waiving the debt.

   PCRs must refer inquirers to DMC for information about:

   * Refunds of withholdings when the inquirer indicates they are experiencing a financial hardship
   * Establishing payment plans for a debt
   * The filing and status of waiver requests, compromise offers and/or
   * Issues when inquirer states they are in a bankruptcy status

   If after thoroughly reviewing the electronic record regarding compensation and pension debts and the
   information in the system is insufficient to answer the beneficiary's questions, PCRs must initiate
   VA Form 27-0820, *Report of General Information*, to request the beneficiary be contacted
   regarding the debt and route according to M27-1 I.5.07.

   When a Veteran requests to verify their award amount, and the award is being recouped due to a debt
   or severance/separation pay, PCRs must verify if they are attempting to verify the gross (full
   amount of award before deductions) or net (amount of the award they actually receive).

   **Note:** PCRs must not refer Veterans to the DMC if their award is being recouped to
   pay back Severance or Separation pay
2. ### VA Medical Center Debts

   For VA Medical Center (VAMC) debts, refer the beneficiary to the VA Health Resource Center (HRC) by
   providing the telephone number, 1-866-400-1238, and offering to transfer the call when appropriate.

## 3.23 Point of Interaction (POI) Processing [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: September 30, 2026

1. ### General Information on Point of Interaction Processing

   To increase customer service and first interaction resolution, designated Processing Teams are
   authorized to establish claim End Products (EPs) or contentions, complete information corrections
   prevented by technical issues, and file documents in correct locations. All PCRs are authorized to
   correct information in a record.

   PCRs must correct:

   * errors in name (misspelling and changes due to marriage/divorce only),
     + Minor misspellings and last name changes due to marriage/divorce do not require
       supporting evidence to make the correction according to M21-1 II.iii.3.B.
     + Name changes that are not a result of a marriage/divorce require the signed decree from
       a judge or court showing the legal name change or a Social Security card that reflects
       the individual's new name.
   * Veteran or spouse date of birth (except dependent child's DOB), or
     + Verified by a birth certificate or verification of the individual's date of birth
       through a Social Security match.
   * service information when the discharge document for the corresponding period of service is
     viewable in the eFolder.

   PCRs must advise inquirers that any other updates to a Veteran or beneficiary's record must be
   submitted in writing.

   **Note:** PCRs must verify receipt of evidence supporting the correction and complete
   updates using the POI-Information Correction- Job Aid.

   PCRs must submit POI requests for the following issues:

   * Claim Establishment
   * Dependent added to incorrect record
   * Erroneous Death
   * Information Correction when a technical issue prevents the update
   * Misfiled Document(s) in VBMS

   PCRs must review the record and not submit a duplicate POI submission when one is open for the same
   issue.
2. ### POI Exemption Referrals

   PCRs must not submit a POI, and instead must make appropriate referrals for the following issues:

   * Inquiries received about non-Compensation & Pension (C&P) claim and appeal types (i.e.,
     VRE, EDU, INS, VHA, NCA). PCRs must provide the division's telephone number and offer to
     transfer, as applicable.
   * Updates to the Master Veterans Index (MVI) that can be seen on the left pane in CRM/UD-O to the
     Health Eligibility Center (HEC) at 1-877-222-8387.
3. ### Claim Establishment

   PCRs must not submit erroneous POI requests to establish a claim. PCRs must:

   * Ensure the application form submitted by the claimant is complete and valid for the benefit
     sought.
     + If the form is complete and valid, PCRs must proceed with POI submission according to
       the below guidance.
     + If the form is not complete and valid, advise the individual to submit a valid
       application (i.e. correct or completed form) and, review the record for an Intent to
       File (ITF). Submit the ITF according to M27-1 I.3.12., as necessary.

   PCRs must submit a POI request to the Processing Team who will establish an EP or contention when:

   * a complete claim form (including VA Forms 20-0995, *Supplemental Claim* or 20-0996,
     *Higher Level Review*) or VA Form 27-0820 with required quick write as listed in M27-1
     I.5.07.e is located in:
     + The CMP for more than seven (7) calendar days when the Claim Requires Priority
       Processing according to M21-1 X.i.1., or
     + The CMP for more than thirty (30) calendar days, or
     + The VBMS eFolderand the claim EP has not been established, or
     + The VBMS eFolder and contentions have not been added to an already established EP.
   * a Board of Veterans' Appeals (Board) remand or grant was issued more than thirty (30) calendar
     days ago.

   *Note*: PCRs must follow guidance in M27-1 I.3.18 for dependency claims.

   PCRs must take the following actions.

   | **NCC PCRs** | **PCT PCRs** |
   | --- | --- |
   | * Follow the steps in the POI- CRM/UD-O PCR Job Aid (available in the Contact   Center KM Portal) to submit a POI Action in CRM/UD-O. * In the Description field, include the following:   + VBMS: Receipt Date, Document Type, Subject, Document ID or  * CMP: Doc Type, and VA DOR (Date of Receipt) | * Establish the appropriate EP or contention in VBMS. * Follow the steps in the POI- CRM/UD-O PCR Job Aid (available in the Contact   Center KM Portal) to “mark request complete” in POI Action in CRM/UD-O. * In the Description field, include the VA form number and document receipt date. *Note***:**If a PCT-PCR does not have access or is unable to establish the appropriate claim they must follow guidance for NCC-PCRs.  * VBMS: Receipt Date, Document Type, Subject, Document ID or  * CMP: Doc Type, and VA DOR (Date of Receipt) |

   *Report of General Information*, providing the appropriate evidence, PCRs must take the

## 3.24 Claim and Appeal Processing Referrals [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: September 30, 2026

1. ### Claim and Appeal Referral Requirements

   Claims and appeals processing referrals are escalated reports submitted via VA Form 27-0820,
   *Report of General Information*. These referrals are submitted when warranted by the below
   requirements.

   | **Category** | **Requirements** |
   | --- | --- |
   | Discrepant Data | Discrepant data occurs when there is conflicting information within VA systems. PCRs must initiate referrals to request confirmation of the correct information and an update to the system. Examples of situations where a referral to correct discrepant data is required (not all inclusive):  * Claim closed, but notification letter not available in the system of records. * Claim denied, but evidence/information that may have impacted the decision was   available in the mail portal at the time the decision was completed, and the   evidence is now located in the eFolder with no EP 930 established. * Incorrect requirements provided in correspondence for submitting a claim or   appeal action   + claim submitted via VA electronic claim submission system is cancelled     due to generation of an outdated version of the VA Form and claimant     advised claim must be submitted on the current version of the VA Form.     See [M21-1,     III.ii.1.C.8.a](https://vaww.vrm.km.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_kanew/help/agent/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001034/content/554400000014112) for additional information on this issue   + claim decided prior to AMA rollout, claimant advised must follow new     appeal process, or   + claim decided after AMA rollout, claimant advised must follow old appeal     process. For corrections to name, date of birth, and service information when the discharge document for that period of service is viewable in the eFolder, PCRs must follow procedures in M27-1 I.3.23. |
   | Priority Claim Status | Priority claims are carefully monitored for overdue suspense dates and are quickly moved through the claims process by eliminating delays between actions and stages. ## 3.26 Erroneous Death Notices [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: September 30, 2026

1. ### Erroneous Reports of Death

   On occasion, VA receives an erroneous report of the death of a Veteran or beneficiary. Reports of
   death may come from the general public or government interagency data matching programs. An
   erroneous report may result in VA records reflecting a Veteran or beneficiary who is alive as
   deceased and corresponding suspension or termination of VA benefit payments.
2. ### Request for Award Resumption

   PCRs are responsible for reporting the erroneous information and taking action to request award
   resumption and reissuance of missing payments. For Veterans and beneficiaries whose VA records
   reflect either *deceased* or a pending *SSA death match*, but who are in fact alive,
   PCRs must take the following actions.

   | **Step** | **Action** |
   | --- | --- |
   | **1** | Document the information on VA Form 27-0820, *Report of General Information*, utilizing the “POI: Erroneous Report of Death” CRM/UD-O quick write and route according to M27-1 I.5.07. |
   | **2** | Follow guidance in M27-1 I.3.23 to submit a Point of Interaction (POI) request. |
   | **3** | Follow all financial hardship procedures if the Veteran or beneficiary indicates a hardship because of missing payments. |
3. ### Verification of SSN

   PCRs must verify whether the SSN of the individual who was reported deceased is correct in the VA
   record.

   * If it is correct, no action is needed.
   * If it is not correct, PCRs must document a request to update the individual's SSN in the VA
     record and include the correct SSN on an additional VA Form 27-0820, routing in accordance with
     M27-1 I.5.07.d. PCRs must advise the individual of the required evidence to support an update to
     the SSN if the information is not already of record.

## 3.29 Fiduciary Referrals [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: September 30, 2026

1. ### Privacy Act Implications

   PCRs must consider the following Privacy Act implications during interactions with incompetent
   beneficiaries, fiduciaries, representatives, and third parties:

   * VA does not recognize outside Powers of Attorney (POAs).
   * While incompetency is at issue, the beneficiary's privacy is protected, and claim-specific
     information cannot be provided to third parties without the beneficiary's permission.
   * VA Form 21-22a, *Appointment of Individual as Claimant's Representative*, can only be
     used in very limited situations (for one claim only) and the name of the person appointed is not
     updated in the system. The form must be scanned into the beneficiary's eFolder to verify the
     name of the appointed person to provide information.
   * Once a fiduciary is appointed, they must answer all the same ID Protocol questions that the
     beneficiary would have to answer prior to releasing protected information.
   * Incompetent beneficiaries cannot execute a VA Form 21-0845, *Authorization to Release
     Information to a Third Party*. A VA Form 21-0845 executed prior to an incompetency
     determination remains valid until revoked or replaced with a new authorization form signed by
     the fiduciary.
   * Information regarding accepting the appointment of a representative for claimants in the process
     of being declared incompetent can be found under in [M21-1
     I.i.2.A.3.b](https://vaww.vrm.km.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_kanew/help/agent/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001034/content/554400000181475/M21-1-Part-I-Subpart-i-Chapter-2-Section-A-General-Information-on-Power-of-Attorney-POA).
   * For an unauthorized third party who is trying to assist their disabled family member/friend,
     protected information cannot be provided without first properly identifying the beneficiary and
     obtaining their permission to speak with the third party. However, PCRs should:
   * answer questions in general terms.
   * answer specific questions about a letter the individual reads aloud.
   * provide alternative means to get information and/or assistance, such as:
   * a request in writing with the beneficiary's signature (or mark with two witnesses if unable to
     sign)
   * suggest the beneficiary submit a request via AskVA, or
   * refer the individual to the beneficiary's appointed POA and/or a county service office.

1. ### General Information about Abuse, Fraud, and Misrepresentation

   Fraud occurs when facts are intentionally misrepresented or withheld in the interest of obtaining or
   retaining Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) benefits.

   Abuse or waste may occur when VA benefits are not used for their intended purpose, funds are not
   spent effectively, and/or assets are not safeguarded.

   PCRs may receive reports of potential abuse, fraud, and/or misrepresentation from Veterans,
   beneficiaries, other stakeholders, and members of the public.

   When an individual alleges abuse, fraud, and/or misrepresentation regarding VA benefits, PCRs must

## 3.31 Military Payments [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: September 30, 2026

1. ### Military Retired Pay and Other Military Payments

   Military retired pay (MRP) benefits may be paid based on age, length of service, or disability.
   Federal law ([38 U.S.C. 5304(c)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/5304))
   generally prohibits duplication of payments when a Veteran receives MRP. If a military retiree is
   granted VA compensation, they must tell us which benefit they elect to receive. Concurrent
   Retirement and Disability Pay (CRDP) allows military retirees to receive both military retired pay
   and VA compensation. Combat Related Special Compensation (CRSC) is a program that was created for
   disability and non-disability military retirees with combat-related disabilities.

   Other military payments which were paid in a lump sum upon separation from service require a total
   withholding of all VA benefits until the lump sum amount has been recouped. These payouts are
   normally found on the DD-214 and in the Military History tab of UDO. Some military benefits may
   require partial withholding of VA compensation benefits for a specific time period, or the
   appropriate amount of the lump sum has been recouped.

   PCRs must answer inquiries about

   * the withholding and payment process
     + eligibility criteria and program information for CRDP and CRSC.
     + recoupment amounts and balances for lump sum military payments, and
     + the difference between a debt and an award adjustment.

   PCRs must refer:

   * Individuals with further inquiries about military retirement benefits to Defense Finance and
     Accounting Services (DFAS) at 1-800-321-1080.
   * Coast Guard retirees and annuitants to the Coast Guard Pay and Personnel Center at
     1-800-772-8724.
   * Individuals to the DEERS Support Office (DSO) at (800) 538-9552 for assistance with updating the
     Veteran or a dependent's name in DEERS.
2. ### Recoupments and Financial Hardship

   When a Veteran asserts the recoupment of a lump sum military payment (i.e. severance or separation
   pay) is an undue burden based on their current financial situation they can request VA reduce the
   amount being recouped each month. This request is allowed whether the claim has been decided, and
   the recoupment is already in place, or if recoupment has only been proposed.

   PCRs are required to advise:

   * about eligibility requirements
     + this is not a request to waive the recoupment and there is no guaranteed withholding
       amount
     + there is no prescribed form for making the request and to submit the written request for
       reduction along with VA Form 5655, *Financial Status Report**.*
     + VA will only accept a request from the Veteran, their VA-recognized representative, or
       fiduciary, and
     + the Veteran will be notified when a decision is made.

   For more information see M21-1, VI.ii.2.
3. ### Drill Pay

   The term drill pay refers to the monetary benefits a reservist or member of the National Guard
   receives for performing active or inactive duty training.

   PCRs are responsible for responding to questions regarding:

   * why drill pay adjustments are required
   * requirements to return VA Form 21-8951, *Notice of Waiver of VA Compensation or Pension to
     Receive Military**Pay**and Allowances**,*
     within sixty (60) days
   * options for completing VA Form 21-8951 and the impact of each option on VA benefits
   * status of pending adjustments
   * the rate of withholding and/or effective dates
   * referral to Debt Management Center for setting up payment arrangements or waiver/compromise
     requests, and
   * requests to send/provide VA Form 21-8951-2*, Notice of Waiver of VA Compensation or Pension
     to Receive Military**Pay**and Allowances**.*

   *Note*: VA Form 21-8951 must be submitted by the Veteran. NCC PCRs must not document a
   response to the form via VA Form 27-0820, *Report of General Information*. However, PCT PCRs
   may provide the form, assist in filing out, and route according to M27-1 I.5.

## 3.34 Crisis Interactions [Back to top](#toc) Change Date: February 2, 2026

1. ### Suicidal Individual

   PCRs must recognize the signs of a crisis. An individual may not specifically state they are going to
   commit suicide. PCRs must be able to analyze and pick up on clues that could indicate the individual
   is in danger of harming themself or others. Listed below are some phrases that could indicate crisis
   and require that the PCR ask probing questions to determine if additional action is necessary:

   * I don't want to live anymore.
   * My family would be better off if I was no longer here.
   * I am in too much pain to deal with this anymore.
   * I don't want to go on.
   * I am giving away everything I own since I won't need it anymore.
   * There is no way to solve this problem, I don't have any options.

4. ### Threat to Employees or VA Facilities

   Employee safety is a priority, and workplace violence will not be tolerated. Threats to employees and
   VA facilities are taken very seriously.

   Upon receipt of a threat, PCRs must

   * immediately notify a supervisor using:
     + the "Emergency" button in CRM/UD-O for NCC and PCT PCRs, or
     + the panic button for PCT PCRs, or
     + instant message (IM) for employees that receive a threat by email/chat/AVA
     + immediately fill out the Physical Threat on Employee or Bomb Threat Checklist online
       form and send to Management.

   *Note*: PCT employees conducting in-person interactions must follow local emergency procedures
   and may activate a panic button if the office is equipped.

   During the interaction, management must

   * assist the PCR in any way possible and begin efforts to notify the affected office's Director's
     Office as soon as possible. Initial contact of the situation does not need to wait until the
     conclusion of the interaction.

   After the interaction, management must

   * immediately forward the completed form to the affected office's Director's Office by email (or
     hand carry if appropriate), and
     + call the affected office's Director's Office, if emailed.

   *Note*: It is critical that the affected Director's Office is notified of the threat as
   quickly as possible. Management should attempt to gain the assistance of their own Director's Office
   to contact another RO or VA Medical Center.

   Natural disasters, i.e., hurricanes, tornadoes, floods, etc., impact Veterans and beneficiaries in

   * document requests for time limit extensions and reports of missed local hearings or Compensation

     because of the natural disaster.
   * follow guidance in M27-1 I.3.24 regarding Board of Veterans' Appeals (Board) hearings.

     additional questions.

     are impacted or displaced by a natural disaster to access virtual care services.

   the income verification match if the eFolder doesn't show the most recent version of the VA
   Form(s) 21-22, 21-22a, or 21-0845, or that the form has not been signed within the past five (5)
   years.

   For unauthorized third parties, PCRs must get permission from the beneficiary to speak to the
   individual regarding IVM information. PCRs must complete proper ID protocols according to M27-1
   I.3.06 with the beneficiary in order to discuss IVM information with the third party.
10. ### Requests for copies of the Federal Benefits for Veterans, Dependents, Survivors, and Caregivers booklet

    PCRs must advise requestors about the various options for accessing the booklet:

    * The online version can be downloaded for free at:

    <https://www.va.gov/opa/publications/benefits_book.asp>.

    * VA medical centers, Regional Offices or Vet Centers may have hard copies of the book available
      for free.
    * The book can be purchased online from the Government Printing Office (GPO) for $10 at: <https://bookstore.gpo.gov/products/federal-benefits-veterans-dependents-survivors-caregivers-2025>.

1. ### Report of Privacy Act Violation

   When a Veteran/beneficiary/third party reports they received another individual's information (e.g.
   another individual's letter, sees another Veteran's dependent information on VA.gov, etc.) PCRs must
   take the following actions:

   | **Step** | **Action** |
   | --- | --- |
   | **1** | Thank the individual for reporting the situation and apologize for the inconvenience. |
   | **2** | For correspondence packages sent from claims processors through Centralized Printing, review the Veterans Benefits Management System (VBMS) Package Manager.  * Locate the Distribution where the violation occurred. * Review the documents in the Distribution to determine if a claims processor   added incorrect documents to the package.   + If yes, meaning there is a document(s) in the package that is for     someone other than the intended recipient, note this on the 27-0820,     *Report of General Information*, as indicated below.   + If no, meaning all documents in the package were for the intended     recipient, note this on the 27-0820 as indicated below. |
   | **3** | Complete VA Form 27-0820, *Report of General Information*, using the “Privacy Act Violation” CRM/UD-O quick write:  * date of reported incident * mailing date and/or receipt date of the correspondence * details of incident to include the name of individual(s) whose record or   information was affected * if information improperly received was a prepared letter, obtain the information   of the individual who prepared the correspondence item, if available, for   example:   + first line of information under the heading, “In reply refer to” section   + identification information provided after the enclosure line in the     letter   + note whether the correspondence was sent through Centralized Printing     and whether any incorrect documents were included in the Distribution. * the name, address, telephone number, and Social Security number and/or claim   number, if appropriate, of the individual reporting the incident * the status of the documents   + if the inquirer has the documents in their possession,   + if the inquirer has destroyed the documents,   + if the inquirer has already mailed the documents back to VA, or   + if the inquirer indicates he/she will return the documents without     waiting for contact, instruct him/her to return them to the office shown     on the letterhead, to the attention of the Freedom of Information Act     (FOIA)/Privacy Act (PA) Officer. *Note*: If the caller received a CD, the CD must be returned. |
   | **4** | Advise that they will be contacted to discuss retrieval of the documents.  *Note*: PCT PCRs must notify the local Privacy Officer if the inquirer brings the documents to the PCT office. |
   | **5** | Route the VA Form 27-0820 via encrypted email to your supervisor or designee for immediate action. |
   | **6** | When the Veteran/beneficiary reports they saw someone listed as a dependent (e.g. on VA.gov or a letter from VA) and they indicate this person is unknown to them and has never been their dependent, PCRs must follow additional guidance in M27-1 I.3.23.h. |
