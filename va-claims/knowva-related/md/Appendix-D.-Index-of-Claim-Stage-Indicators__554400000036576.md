**D.1.a . Purpose of Tracked Items**

The purpose of tracked items is to control receipt or non-receipt of information requested from the claimant, beneficiary, or other information provider.  Status of individual tracked items will be visible through eBenefits.  Tracked items must be updated with the appropriate date and disposition to reflect the status of the corresponding request or development action.

 ***Reference***:  For more information on

* using tracked items, see
  + [M21-1, Part III, Subpart i, 2.F.3](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000046277/)
  + *Veterans Benefits Management System (VBMS) User Guide*, or
  + [*Modern Award Processing-Development (MAP-D) User Guide*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/21/Systems/mapd.htm)
* processing solicited and unsolicited mail, see [M21-1, Part II, Subpart i, 2.A.5.d](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000174858/), and
* mail management, see [M21-1, Part II, Subpart i, 2.C](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000174860/)

**D.1.b . Index of Tracked Items**

Use the table below to identify the appropriate tracked items, and the corresponding details to include claim level suspense reasons and initial suspense dates.

 ***Notes***:

* A tracked item is considered non-actionable if no action is required at the expiration of the suspense date, and will therefore be automatically closed if no response has been received at that time.
* A tracked item classified as an exception to automatic ready for decision (RFD) or ready to work (RTW) will not result in the claim automatically moving to RFD/RTW status if marked as *Received*, *Closed*, or *In Error* on the same calendar day the last open tracked item is completed.
* The Enhanced Non-Permanent Award Display (ENAD) process creates a system-generated “Proposal to Reduce” tracked item.

 ***References***:  For more information on follow up suspense dates, see:

* [M21-1, Part III, Subpart ii, 1.A.1.b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000014108/) for federal record requests, and

## **2.  Claim Statuses**

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information about claim statuses, including

* [purpose of claim statuses](#2a)
* [index of claim statuses](#2b)
* [claim status updates for non-rating end products (EPs) requiring a rating decision](#2c)
* [actions for non-rating claim ready for rating action](#2d), and
* [actions for RTW non-rating claims](#2f).

---

**Change Date**

December 11, 2024

---

**D.2.a . Purpose of Claim Statuses**

Claim status is used to determine the current phase of the claims process and can potentially control the claim level suspense reasons.  Claim status must be properly updated to indicate the accurate stage of processing.

---

**D.2.b . Index of Claim Statuses**

Use the table below to determine the appropriate claim status.

| **Claim Status** | **Definition** |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Default status for claims not in any other status. Typically stays in this status during claims development. |
| RFD | Signifies that the claim is ready for a rating. |
| Ready to Work | Signifies that the claim is ready for a non-rating decision. |
| RDC | Signifies that a rating decision has been completed and is awaiting promulgation. |
| Rating Correction | Signifies that a rating correction is needed. |
| Rating Incomplete | Signifies that a rating decision was returned to corporate from the work pending column. |
| Closed | The claim is complete with no further action possible. |
| Cancelled | The claim is cancelled with no further action possible. |

---

**D.2.c . Claim Status Updates for Non-Rating EPs Requiring a Rating Decision**

Current auto-RFD/RTW functionality within VBMS will move a non-rating claim to the incorrect lifecycle phase which could trigger National Work Queue to misroute non-rating claims that require a rating decision.  To prevent these claims from moving to the incorrect lifecycle phase and to ensure proper routing, a claims processor must take the appropriate action.

***References:*** For the appropriate actions for non-rating claims that

* require either no additional development or due process prior to rating, see [M21-4, D.2.d](#2d), or
* require a period of suspense prior to rating, see [M21-4, D.2.e](#2e).

---

**D.2.d . Actions for Non-Rating Claim Ready for Rating Action**

For claims that require either no additional development or due process prior to rating, the claims processor must manually update the claim to reflect RFD claim status.

When a claims processor determines a claim is ready for rating action

* do not add a tracked item, and
* manually updates claim status to RFD by clicking the COMPLETE WORK ITEM icon and selecting READY FOR DECISION from the drop-down menu.

***Note***:If a claims processor accidentally closes the last tracked item prior to updating the claim status, the claim will automatically move to RTW status and VBMS will remove the claim from the claims processor’s work queue.  In this scenario, the claims processor must select the EDIT CLAIM DETAIL icon and update the claim suspense to READY FOR DECISION.

This action will update the claim status to RFD and ensure proper routing.

---

**D.2.e . Actions for RTW Non-Rating Claims**

When possible and in accordance with proper procedures, all necessary actions should be completed concurrently, rather than piecemeal.  When all development or due process is complete for a non-rating claim that does not require rating action, the appropriate and necessary next action for the claims processor is to update the status to RTW ***and*** generate the award.

***I******mportant***:  Claims processors should strive to work all actionable end products  (EPs) *within the scope of work.*

***Exception***:  Per [M21-1, Part VII, Subpart i, 1.C.1.e](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000176615/), if a claim that might result in the assignment of a combined disability evaluation of at least 30 percent is concurrently pending, postpone a decision on the claim for additional compensation for a dependent until *after* the rating activity decides the disability claim.

## ****4.  Deferral** Reasons**

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information about deferral reasons, including

* [purpose of deferral reasons](#4a), and
* [index of deferral reasons](#4b).

---

**Change Date**

February 4, 2026

---

**D.4.a . Purpose of Deferral Reasons**

The electronic deferral functionality within Veterans Benefits Management System (VBMS) allows a claims processor to return a claim to an earlier place within the claims cycle to correct avoidable and unavoidable erroneous actions.  The most accurate deferral reason must be selected when generating deferrals.  Selecting the most accurate deferral reason

* provides justification on the use of the deferral functionality and for the need of the deferral being generated to return the claim to an earlier places within the claims cycle
* ensures understanding of the deferral and that the proper action(s) can be taken to resolve the deferral while allowing the claim to continue through the claims process
* allows for proper employee transactional credit for action related to the deferral process, and
* allows for proper workload management, both at the national level and the local level.
