Decision Assessment Document
 VAOPGCPREC 12-96, Nov 21, 1996, Recoupment of Armed Forces Severance Pay from Veterans’ Disability Compensation
 Office of General Counsel Precedent Opinion

**What the case is about**

Whether 38 C.F.R. § 3.700(a)(3) or any other legal authority requires withholding of a veteran’s Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) disability compensation to recoup the amount of nondisability severance pay received by the veteran from the veteran’s armed forces component upon discharge from military service.

**Impact on VBA**

Section 1174(h)(2) of title 10, United States Code, which provides that there shall be deducted from any disability compensation under laws administered by the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) an amount equal to the amount of separation pay received under section 1174 or severance pay or readjustment pay received under any other provision of law, requires that VA recoup from a veteran’s VA disability compensation the amount of “nondisability severance pay” received by the veteran under section 631 of Pub. L. No. 96-513.  The statement in 38 C.F.R. § 3.700(a)(3), which reflects the statute requiring recoupment of disability severance pay, that, “[t]here is no prohibition against payment of compensation where the veteran received nondisability severance pay” is of no effect as it is inconsistent with 10 U.S.C. § 1174(h)(2).

**Summary on facts and findings**

The General Counsel looked at a case where the veteran was administratively discharged involuntarily from the Air Force because of misconduct or moral or professional dereliction and who received nondisability severance pay authorized by section 631 of Pub. L. No. 96-513.  The veteran maintained that the lump-sum payment received from the Air Force should not be recouped from VA disability compensation since the payment represented severance pay not due to a disability.  After a review of section 1212 of title 10, Pub. L.  96-513, 38 C.F.R. § 3.700 and M21-1 provisions, the General Counsel held that VA must recoup the nondisability severance pay when it has been authorized under section 631 of Pub. L. No. 96-513.

This opinion pointed out that prior provisions of title 10 authorizing severance pay for certain Air Force and Army officers was repealed by Pub. L. No. 96-513, effective September 15, 1981.  It was noted that 38 C.F.R. § 3.700(a)(3) contained a statement that there was no prohibition against payment of compensation where a veteran had received nondisability severance pay.  That statement was accurate in 1959 when the regulation was added, but is inconsistent with the 1981 law and, since it is of no legal effect, should be deleted.  Further, the opinion noted that provisions of M21-1, Part IV, paragraphs 20.02b(2) and 20.40 could be read as suggesting that recoupment of severance payments made pursuant to section 631 of Pub. L. No. 96-513 from VA disability compensation is not required.

Delete from 38 C.F.R. § 3.700(a)(3) the obsolete statement that there is no prohibition against payment of compensation where a veteran has received nondisability severance pay.  Amend M21-1, Part IV, paragraphs 20.02b(2) and 20.40 to read that recoupment of severance payments made pursuant to section 631 of Pub. L. No. 96-513 from VA disability compensation is required.
