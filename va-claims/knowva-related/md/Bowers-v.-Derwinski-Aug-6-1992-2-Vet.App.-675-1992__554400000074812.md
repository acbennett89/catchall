Decision Assessment Document
Bowers v. Derwinski, Aug 6, 1992, 2 Vet.App. 675 (1992)
U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims (Court)

**What the case is about:**

The Court held that when 38 CFR 4.1 and DC 7800 and DC 7806 are read together, it is the frequency and duration of the outbreaks and the appearance and virulence of the skin disorder during the outbreaks that must be addressed.  To determine the appropriate evaluation for a skin disorder, an accurate and fully descriptive medical examination with emphasis upon the limitation of activity imposed by the disabling condition upon the person's ordinary activity is needed.  The Court also held that examinations must be done when the infection is in its active stage.

**Impact on VBA:**

The Court's holding has been incorporated into guidance contained in Manual M21-1, III.iv.3.B.

**Summary of the facts and Court’s reasons:**

The Court found no evidence that an examination was conducted during the active stage of the infection.  It is the frequency and duration of the outbreaks and the appearance and virulence of them during the outbreaks that must be addressed, in terms of whether there is "marked disfigurement" (sufficient itself for a 30% rating) under DC 7806 or "severe" scarring under DC 7800.  Periodicity is only one factor to be considered.  Statements from appellant are somewhat vague as to the virulence, frequency of the outbreaks and the size of the lesions.  The appellant clearly feels that they are grossly disfiguring, so much so that he avers refusing to leave his house during the periodic outbreaks.  His statement may or may not be self-serving.  What is needed is an examination done when the infection is in its active stage.  Although the veteran is reluctant to be seen during these periods, he can hardly refuse to undergo the examination if he desires a fair rating.  In assessing the degree of disfigurement, the examination should also consider the, apparently, unrelated acne and whether it contributes to the overall disfigurements.  Since "the limitation of activity imposed by the ... condition" is necessarily dependent upon the frequency and duration of the outbreaks, a complete rating would require the addressing of that question by medical experts.
