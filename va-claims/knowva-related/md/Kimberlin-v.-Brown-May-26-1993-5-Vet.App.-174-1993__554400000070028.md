Decision Assessment Document
Kimberlin v. Brown, May 26, 1993 , 5 Vet.App. 174 (1993)
U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims (Court)

**What the case is about:**

The Court held that Dependent Educational Assistance (DEA) under Chapter 35 is authorized when a permanent and total disability compensation rating is assigned on the basis of paired organs under 38 U.S.C. 1160.

**Impact on VBA:**

The Court's holding has been incorporated into the guidance contained in Manual M21-1, IV.ii.2.K.

**Summary of the facts and Court’s reasons:**

The veteran (7/61-8/62) was totally deaf in the left ear prior to entry on active duty, and was initially 10% service-connected based on aggravation of right ear hearing loss.  The evaluation was later increased to 100% due to bilateral deafness, but DEA was denied.  The Court noted that the denial was based on a General Counsel Memorandum which was later reissued as a precedent opinion.  The Court interpreted the law differently, however, and reversed the BVA denial.  The Court declined to reconsider its original decision on 6/16/93.

The Court deemed 38 U.S.C. 3501(a)(1)(A)(ii) and (a)(8) dispositive because the child's relationship was undisputed and because it appeared that the qualifying rating resulted from service-connected disability.  In this connection, the Court noted that the veteran's disability would not have been rated as total "but for" his service-connected condition.
