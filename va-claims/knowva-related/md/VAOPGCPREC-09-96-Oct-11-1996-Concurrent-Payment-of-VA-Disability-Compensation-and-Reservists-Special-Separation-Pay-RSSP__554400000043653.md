Decision Assessment Document
 VAOPGCPREC 09-96, Oct 11, 1996, Concurrent Payment of VA Disability Compensation and Reservists' Special Separation Pay (RSSP) and Reservists' Involuntary Separation Pay (RISP) Under Pub. L. No. 102-484
 Office of General Counsel Precedent Opinion

**What the case is about**

Whether VA disability compensation must be offset to recoup the amount of Reservists' Special Separation Pay (RSSP) received by a veteran under Pub. L. No. 102-484?

Whether VA disability compensation must be offset to recoup the amount of Reservists' Involuntary Separation Pay (RISP) received by a veteran under Pub. L. No. 102-484?

**Impact on VBA**

The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) is not authorized under section 4416(b) of Pub. L. No. 102-484 to offset VA disability compensation to recoup the amount of Reservists' Special Separation Pay (RSSP) received by a veteran pursuant to section 4416(b).

Section 4418(c) of Pub. L. No. 102-484 which provides that the provisions of section 1174(h)(2) are applicable to Reservists' Involuntary Separation Pay (RISP) and 38 C.F.R. § 3.700(a)(5) require VA to offset disability compensation to recoup the amount of RISP received by a veteran pursuant to section 4418 provided that the VA compensation is for a disability incurred in or aggravated by service prior to the date of receipt of the RISP.

**Summary on facts and findings**

The statutory language of sections 4416(b) and 4418, Pub. L. 102-484 govern whether VA disability compensation must be offset to recoup the amount of RSSP or RISP received by a veteran under those sections.  Section 4416(b) does not contain a provision which requires VA to offset disability compensation to recoup the amount of RSSP received by a veteran.  Thus, a veteran's disability compensation benefits will not be used to offset RSSP payments.

Section 4418 does contain a provision which requires VA to offset disability compensation to recoup the amount of RISP payments provided that the VA compensation is for a disability incurred in or aggravated by service prior to the date of receipt of the separation pay.

M21-1, Part IV, Chapter 20, Subchapter VI provides information about recoupment of various types of military separation pay.  Information about the offset of RISP payments and non offset of RSSP payments should be added to that subchapter.

Amend M21-1, Part IV, Chapter 20, Subchapter VI to include information about the requirement to offset RISP payments and the lack of a requirement to offset RSSP payments to reservists.
