Decision Assessment Document
 VAOPGCPREC 15-92, Jul 15, 1992, Effect on Pension Eligibility of Property Held as a Life Estate
 Office of General Counsel Precedent Opinion

**What the opinion is about:**

The veteran and his spouse transferred three farm properties to their children for a nominal consideration, retaining a life estate interest.  Following the veteran's death, his spouse derived some rental income based on a lease agreement with her son.  General Counsel noted that she retained an ownership interest in the properties which included income rights, and that the transfer had been tantamount to a gift.  Accordingly, it was concluded that the transfers should not be recognized as reducing the corpus of estate pursuant to 38 CFR 3.276(b).  General Counsel also noted that there is authority to amend this regulation so that only the value of the life interest would be considered.

**Impact on VBA:**

Provisions of 38 CFR 3.276(b) should be reviewed to determine whether or not a revision is warranted based on the General Counsel commentary.  In addition, provisions of M21-1, Part IV, 16.40d should be amended to address title transfers when a life-estate interest is retained.

**Summary of the facts and findings:**

Transfer of a property interest, to someone other than a relative in the claimant's household, is to be disregarded under 38 CFR 3.276(b) when a life estate interest is retained.
