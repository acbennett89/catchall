**I.A.1.a. CSNTC Overview**

Per [**5 CFR 410.201**](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?c=ecfr&sid=3098262948269d8650f63b050af2012e&rgn=div5&view=text&node=5:1.0.1.2.58&idno=5#se5.1.410_1201), Compensation Service is responsible for creating training programs that support an agency workforce capable of achieving agency mission and performance goals, and facilitating continuous improvement of employee and organizational performance.

The CSNTC provides guidance to ensure all employees who process compensation claims are empowered to develop and acquire the technical skills necessary to maintain competency in their positions, deliver excellent customer service, and improve the Veteran experience.

---

**I.A.1.b. CSNTC Excluded Training**

CSNTC ***does not*** apply to

* Pension Management Centers (PMCs)
* positions that fall under the jurisdiction of the National Call Center (NCC) and Office of Field Operations (OFO) or any business line other than Compensation Service
* supervisor or management training
* informal training such as mentoring sessions, and
* VA-wide or VBA-wide mandatory training such as Cyber Security, No FEAR, Privacy, Ethics, Prevention of Sexual Harassment, VBA Active Threat, and Records Management.

---

**I.A.1.c. RO Responsibilities**

Regional Offices (ROs) will support and encourage employee training to improve the delivery of compensation claims to Veterans.

Responsibilities of the Training Manager and/or designee:

* Establish VSR/RVSR station training plans at the beginning of every fiscal year (FY) or in support of station identified needs for remediation.
* Stations are responsible for 25 hours for MSCs for FY26.
* CS does not limit additional hours for FY26 RVSR or VSR Pre Determination Competency Based Training System (CBTS).
* CS will not mandate a specific number of training hours for Pre VSRs, Post VSRs, Pre/Post VSRs, BEST VSRs, or RVSRs for FY26, however stations must still develop and submit training plans based on previous year trending data, or selected topics deemed necessary by station leadership.
* Ensure compliance for all training identified via the Competency Based Training System (CBTS) for Pre VSRs, Post VSRs, Pre/Post VSRs, BEST VSR, and RVSR.
* Submit initial training plans to Compensation Service by the eighth day of November each FY.
* Submit quarterly training compliance reports to Compensation Service by the eighth day of each quarter during FY for MSCs and CAs.

**Note:** System compliance includes ensuring all TMS actions and the supervisor assignment of courses in the employee workbench are cleared within 30 days of CBTS assessment completion. System compliance also includes **ensuring required remediation,** or mitigation of course work**,** identified by the CBTS Diagnostic Assessment report. All actions regarding CBTS assignment of assessment, supervisor approval or mitigation and completion must be completed within 90 days of the CBTS Diagnostic Assessment being provided to the employee.

The RO will ensure

* compliance with this directive
* continuous monitoring for local quality trends to identify areas of improvement, and
* scheduling and delivery of instructor-led training.

ROs must ensure that local instructors are

* qualified to deliver training and it is "highly recommended" the instructor be ELITE certified. For additional information on ELITE training please contact the ELITE staff at [VBAHCSELITEProgram@va.gov](mailto:VBAHCSELITEProgram@va.gov)
* utilizing training materials authorized by Compensation Service, and
* administering courses according to the approved lesson plans.

***Example***:  If the lesson plan states that the course is “instructor-led,” then the instructor must provide instructor-led training by utilizing the associated training packet provided by Compensation Service.

**I.A.2.a. CSNTC Audience**

The CSNTC is required annual training for all employees in Veterans Service Centers (VSCs) who process compensation claims, to include

* Veterans Service Representatives (Pre VSR, Post VSR, Pre-Post VSR, & BEST VSR).
* Rating Veterans Service Representatives (RVSRs)
* Leadership selected or assigned training based on trends and analysis.
* Quality Review Team (QRT): Authorization Quality Review Specialists (AQRSs) and Rating Quality Review Specialists (RQRSs).
* Public Contact Team (PCT) VSRs (who process compensation claims).
* Pre-Discharge Military Services Coordinators (Pre-D and MSCs) Claims Processors who process Integrated Disability Evaluation System (IDES).
* Military Sexual Trauma (MST) Claims Processors.
* Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI) Claims Processors.
* Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS) Claims Processors, and
* Claims Assistants (CAs).

**I.A.2.b. Virtual & In-Person Progression (VIP) Training Description**

VIP is a course of study delivered by Compensation Service to entry-level employees assigned to VSR, RVSR and Pre-Discharge MSC Claims Processor positions.

VIP training provides employees with hands-on training on computer applications, policies, and procedures related to claims processing.

VIP training instruction and delivery consists of self-paced modules, instructor-led web-based training (IWT), and Regional Office virtual/traditional classroom sessions. RVSR training and VSR trainings are conducted in the VBA Virtual Schoolhouse (VSH) environment. BEST training is blended, as the classroom portion of training is completed via VBA Virtual Schoolhouse with credit given in TMS.

VSR and RVSR VIP training schedules can be located on the Compensation Service Training Homepage.

VIP training course curricula can be located within the VBA Learning Catalog by filtering the 'Curriculum', 'Compensation', and 'VIP' categories.

***Note***:  Pre-Discharge MSC Claims Processors will attend Pre VSR VIP-Training.

**I.A.2.c. Compensation Service VIP Ancillary Training (VAT) Curricula**

VAT Curricula consist of one-time mandated training items. These one-time mandated courses focus on specific topics that the identified target audience must complete at least once during their career in the job position after VIP graduation. VAT Curricula must be completed in addition to Compensation Service CBTS (as applicable and mandated/direct training.

The VAT curriculum must be completed by all employees in the target audience who meet the criteria.

***Reference:*** For more information on the one-time mandated items, see the **[Compensation Service Training Plans web site.](http://cptraining.vba.va.gov/C&P_Training/)**

**I.A.2.d. VAT 45-Day and 75-Day Proficiency Assessments**

Following completion of VSR VIP classroom training, employees will complete online proficiency assessments at two different intervals.

For the 45-Day Assessment, employees must take this assessment as early as 45 calendar days but no later than 55 calendar days after finishing Pre/Post/Best VSR VIP classroom training.

For the 75-Day Assessment, employees must take the  assessment as early as 75-calendar days, but no later than 85-calendar days after completion of Pre/Post/VIP training.

Adherence to these timelines is necessary to ensure employees have the same baseline opportunity to learn and demonstrate their skills in their new position before taking the Proficiency Assessments at the designated intervals in their learning journey.

**Note:** The administration of the 45-Day and 75-Day Assessment is completed via central direct assignment in coordination with VIP program management.

**I.A.2.e. Compensation Service FY26 Mandated/Directed Training Curriculum (all cohorts)**

FY26 mandated and directed training courses **for all cohorts**are developed for specific topics identified by Compensation Service based on national quality trends and emerging issues.

All FY26 mandated/directed training items are:

* determined by Compensation Service in collaboration with stakeholders or based on identified trends.
* identified in the [**VBA Learning Catalog**](https://vba-tpi.vbatraining.org/lc/), **[Compensation Service FY26 Mandated Courses Released](https://vba-media1.vbatraining.org/VBA_Learning_Catalog/Comp_Svc/CS_FY26_Mandated_Courses.xlsx)**and
* assigned to the target audience’s TMS learning plan by Compensation Service.
* required in addition to CBTS assessment and refresher training requirements.

**Note:**Training may be assigned annually, monthly, quarterly, or as needed.  When assigned monthly, quarterly or as needed the training course **must be** completed within **30 days** of assignment unless otherwise noted in the assignment.

Compensation Service mandated training **must be completed** by all employees in the target audience.

**I.A.2.f. Compensation Service FY26 Competency Based Training System (CBTS)**

FY26 Pre VSR, Post VSR, Pre/Post VSR, BEST VSR, AQRS, RQRS, and RVSR directed training requirements are determined by the tailored results of the CBTS assessments. CBTS assessments are provided to experienced Pre VSRs, Post VSRs, BEST VSRs Pre/Post VSR, and RVSRs every FY. Employees with less than a year of service will be assigned the CBTS assessment upon reaching their one-year anniversary.

AQRS and RQRS personnel must take the appropriate CBTS assessment, as identified for the cohort that they are aligned by the respective RO. Each AQRS or RQRS should be aligned to an NTC primary cohort for training assignments based on the work they are reviewing.

Supervisors and managers who are responsible for claim processing transactions must also take the CBTS assessment corresponding to said actions. Supervisor and manager participation in CBTS is determined by OFO and monitored for compliance by OFO.

Notification of the requirement to take the CBTS assessment will be provided at least 30 days in advance via centralized direct assignment of "CBTS for Claims Processors" (VA 4632868), which is due 7 days from the assignment date.

Employees **must complete** the required remedial training identified by CBTS within **90 days of assignment** ofthe CBTS Diagnostic Assessment.

**Note:** Additional training may be assigned as a result of quality control trend analysis and as needed or directed by leadership.

**I.A.2.g. Compensation Service Station-Selected Training Pre D MSCs and CAs**

Station-selected training for Pre-D MSCs, CAs or any other cohort not identified in **[M21-3 I.A.2.f](#2f)** is determined by the RO based on analysis of local quality reviews, individual needs, Compensation Service Quality Assurance Reviews, national quality trends, and requirements for a particular position. Station-selected training is identified in the [**VBA Learning Catalog**](https://vba-tpi.vbatraining.org/lc/) and will count toward meeting the requirements for CSNTC.

Station-selected training must be assigned to employees that are members of the target audience as identified in the VBA Learning Catalog in order to receive credit toward meeting the requirements for CSNTC. In unique situations, training created for a different audience that is outside of an employee's usual position may be approved on a case-by-case basis.

To request consideration for a unique training circumstance, email the Compensation Service Training mailbox at **[CPtraining.VBACO@va.gov](mailto:VAVBAWAS/CO/C&PTraining%20%3CCPTraining.VBACO@va.gov%3E)**for approval.

**I.A.2.h. Curriculum Definition**

A course of study designed for a particular purpose.

***Example***:  The Training VAT curriculum contains all lessons a VSR or RVSR is required to complete within their first year in the position and prior to being administered the CBTS diagnostic assessments. If VAT has not been completed a station **must request an employee exemption from**OFO.

The VSR and RVSR FY directed curricula contain all courses a VSR/RVSR is required to complete during the FY in addition to any courses assigned or mitigated through the CBTS program.

**I.A.2.i. Talent Management System (TMS) Terms and CBTS Workbench**

TMS is the VA system of record for all VA training data.

Frequently used terms in TMS:

* TMS Cohort: A tool used to group users who have a common set of primary requirements (e.g., the VSR Primary Cohort groups all employees with VSR-related training requirements).
* TMS Specialized Cohort: A tool used to group users who have a common set of specialized requirements (e.g., the AQRS Specialized Cohort groups AQRS employees on the Quality Review Team). Employees are added to specialized cohorts in addition to the appropriate cohort for their NTC primary position.
* Level I Evaluations: Surveys administered to gather students’ feedback and thoughts regarding their training experience, and
* Level II Assessments: Exams that measure students’ knowledge, skills, and abilities as a result of the training.
* CBTS Student Workbench-Training Managers, Coaches, and Station Leadership have account access to the CBTS student workbench. The workbench provides information on:
  + Assessment Scores
  + TMS actions that have been identified for remediation
  + Required remediation assignments
  + Supervisor approval for individual learning plans

***Reference***:  For more information about

* TMS, see the [**TMS web site**](https://www.tms.va.gov/SecureAuth35/).

**I.A.2. j. Employee Position-Category Definitions for Training**

The status of an employee during their time in a position is defined by the timeframe needed to complete required training curricula and to demonstrate basic proficiency in job-related tasks.  Employee position-category definitions for training purposes are:

* Entry-Level: An employee that has been in a position for 0-12 months.
* Intermediate-Level: An employee that has been in a position for 13-24 months.
* Journey-level: An employee that has been in a position for at least 25 months.

***Reference***: For more information on available training based on employee position-category, see the [**VBA Learning Catalog**](https://vba-tpi.vbatraining.org/lc/?AspxAutoDetectCookieSupport=1).

**I.A.2.k. Training Definition**

Training is the process of providing for and making available to an employee, and placing or enrolling the employee in, a planned, prepared, and coordinated program, course, curriculum, subject, system, or routine of instruction or education, in scientific, professional, technical, mechanical, trade, clerical, fiscal, administrative, or other fields, which will improve individual and organizational performance and assist in achieving the agency’s mission and performance goals.

employees, see [**5 U.S.C. 4101(4)**](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/5/4101).

**I.A.3.f. WARTAC VSR and RVSR New Training Requirements**

Warrior Training Advancement Course (WARTAC) brings VIP training to military installations and conducts VSR and RVSR Training with transitioning, active duty, Service Members in order to ultimately hire them into Regional Offices, as VSRs or RVSRs upon separation from active duty.

**Training Manager (TM) responsibilities** begin when a WARTAC student is hired at their RO. Once the TM is notified that a WARTAC student has been hired, the TM should complete the following steps:

1. Request a TMS account domain transfer from the Enterprise Service Desk via Your IT or by calling 855-673-4357.
2. Review the employee data located within Profile Maintenance.
3. Ensure that the PAID database does not create a duplicate TMS account for the new employee. If a duplicate account is created, TM must send the request to **[VALITier2@va.gov](mailto:VALITier2@va.gov)** and they will merge the accounts.
4. Assign the WARTAC student to the appropriate TMS cohort, based upon his or her job position:

* ‘WARTAC Pre VSR Graduate Hires’ (VBA-721): The Pre VSR VAT directed, and Pre VSR fiscal year directed curricula will be automatically assigned to employees placed in this cohort.
* ‘WARTAC Post VSR Graduate Hires’ (VBA-746): The Post VSR VAT mandated, and the Post VSR fiscal year directed curricula will be automatically assigned to employees placed in this Cohort.
* ‘WARTAC Pre/Post VSR Graduate Hires’ (VBA-387): The Pre & Pre/Post VSR VAT mandated, and the Pre/Post VSR fiscal year directed curricula will be automatically assigned to employees placed in this Cohort.
* ‘WARTAC RVSR Graduate Hires’ (VBA-556): The RVSR VAT mandated and RVSR fiscal year directed curricula will be automatically assigned to employees placed in this cohort.

**Compensation Service responsibilities:**

Upon graduation, Compensation Service will award these employees TMS credit for completing the WARTAC training program. WARTAC graduates will be centrally added to the corresponding VSR or RVSR NTC primary cohorts upon completion of their trainee status (12 months following their hire date).

**I.A.3.i CSNTC FY Training Requirements for AQRSs and RQRSs**

AQRS and RQRS are NTC specialized positions.  This means that these positions are in addition to the employee’s VSR or RVSR NTC primary position.  Training is added to the AQRS and RQRS FY NTC curricula on an as-needed basis.  AQRS and RQRS employees must be added to the appropriate AQRS or RQRS cohort in addition to their primary position VSR or RVSR cohort.

AQRSs must complete any FY AQRS mandated assignments as well as fulfill their appropriate VSR NTC requirements (Pre, Post, Pre/Post, or BEST).

BEST VSRs are an exception to this.  BEST VSRs who are also AQRSs are not required to be added to the AQRS cohort or complete the FY mandated AQRS assignments. Once approved and TMS item number established, the training will count toward CSNTC station-selected training credit or if mandated to meet a national need, the training will count toward CSNTC mandated training requirements.

Compensation Service holds the sole authority to include training items in the CSNTC.

**I.A.5.e CSNT Make-up Training**

All employees considered members in a target audience are required to complete the mandated training. Therefore, each RO must develop a strategy for conducting make-up training sessions as necessary.  Procedures for the make-up training sessions should include the following:

* The Compensation Service lesson plan for each training item will be followed for all training offerings.
* The approved materials and handouts, including online exercises, media demonstrations and/or evaluations, provided by Compensation Service for each training offering, will be distributed to training participants.
* Typically, the same instructor will facilitate every offering of a required training item.
* If a session recording is provided as a make-up session, a subject matter expert (SME) on the training topic must be made available as the point of contact during the training, should questions arise
* All session recordings should be housed in a central location on each RO drive so that any member, in the target audience, may have access.
* Station-selected training make-up will be determined at the RO level and by individual training needs using approved materials.
* Make-up sessions must take place as soon as possible within the same FY for the Compensation Service mandated items. ,
* Roll call sheets are required to record instructor-led training attendance, including the name of the SME who facilitated the make-up session.

**I.A.6.c. Prorating Training Availability Time for Compensation Service CSNTC VSR**

Prorating training availability time for CSNTC training requirements will be allowed in the following circumstances for Pre-D MSCs and CAs:

* RO employees who assist as VIP Instructors: CSNTC requirements may be prorated for details lasting more than 30 business days.  However, VIP instructors will complete all items associated with the Compensation Service mandated/directed training items.
* Employees becoming eligible for CSNTC training after the first month of the FY will be prorated for the remaining months of the FY. ***Example***: An employee completes his/her entry level training and is assigned to the TMS primary cohort in January. The time available for training is reduced to nine months.

  Employees on extended periods of excused absence from their RO to include
* Consecutive active duty or active duty for training absence for 30 consecutive days or more
* Accumulative active duty or active duty for training absence for 30 days or more
* Consecutive leave for 30 days or more including approved leave under the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA)
* Accumulative sick leave or FMLA for 30 days or more; single days of sick leave that are not excused by a physician will not count as time applied for prorated hours
* Extenuating circumstances justified by employee supervisor and approved by management (in case final approval rests with OFO)

Proper documentation will be maintained with the Training Manager for the current and previous FY for compliance purposes.  A memorandum from the employee’s immediate supervisor will suffice as proper documentation to include the name of the employee, period of time, and number of days that have been approved as extended periods of leave.  The documentation will be kept confidential and will only be presented to the RO Director’s Office for compliance purposes.  The documentation will be kept for the current and previous FY.

Extended Leave Notes:

* Leaves of absence do not alter an employee’s trainee period or NTC primary cohort transition date.  Exception requests should be submitted to **[CPTraining.VBACO@va.gov](mailto:CPTraining.VBACO@va.gov)**.  If approved, Compensation Service will alert the VBA Curriculum Support Team (CST), who will then add the trainee to the appropriate trainee cohort.

* If an employee’s TMS account is temporarily deactivated, TMS will remove them from all cohorts.  When the employee returns, an RO TMS administrator will need to add the employee back to the appropriate cohorts.  If the employee is still a trainee, please submit the cohort update request to the VBA CST at **[VBATMSHelp@VBATraining.org](mailto:VBATMSHelp@VBATraining.org)**.
