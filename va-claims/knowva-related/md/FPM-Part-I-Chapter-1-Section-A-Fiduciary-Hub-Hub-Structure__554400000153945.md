**I.1.A.1.a . Structure of the Hub**

Each fiduciary hub (hub) is structured to support the Fiduciary Program in the overall mission of the Veterans Benefits Administration.  The basic functions of a hub include

* fiduciary promulgation
* field examination
* processing of mail
* accountings
* misuse, and
* appeals.

***References***:  For more information on

* fiduciary promulgation teams, see [FPM, Part I, 1.A.1.b](#1b)
* field examinations, see [FPM, Part I, 2.A](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153947/)

**I.1.A.1.b . Purpose of Fiduciary Promulgation Activity**

The purpose of fiduciary promulgation activity is to expedite Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) benefit payment actions to beneficiaries by limiting the transfers of jurisdiction between the regional office (RO) and the hub when VA has evidence that a beneficiary is unable to manage their VA benefits.

Fiduciary promulgation activity is responsible for completing all final determinations that a beneficiary cannot manage their VA benefits for which the due process period expired on or after August 1, 2014, except for cases indicated in [FPM, Part I, 1.A.2.b](#2b).

Fiduciary promulgation activity is also responsible for

* processing fiduciary adjustments for all cases requiring a fiduciary appointment
* ensuring the appropriate end product (EP) is established
* updating the follow-up diary information types, dates, and reasons for

  + beneficiary oversight per [FPM, Part I, 2.D.1](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153952/), and
  + fiduciary oversight per [FPM, Part I, 6.A](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000223127/) and [B](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000223126/) and [FPM, Part I, 3.A.1](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153954/)
* processing the appointment of the fiduciary and fee authorization
* completing all award adjustments that are ready for decision at the time the field examination work item is being completed per [FPM, Part I, 2.E.3.k](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153953/)
* ensuring the accuracy of system information, and
* ensuring fiduciary adjustments are completed within the established timeframe.

Fiduciary promulgation activity has authority to suspend, resume, or discontinue benefit payments when action is identified because of a finding by the hub of jurisdiction.  Fiduciary promulgation activity will take applicable payment actions in situations involving

* suspected misuse
* fiduciary appointment pending
* beneficiary’s or fiduciary’s whereabouts unknown
* beneficiary’s or fiduciary’s lack of cooperation in appointment or oversight procedures, or
* notices of death (NODs) for Veterans and non-Veterans.

***References***:  For more information on

**I.1.A.1.c . Purpose of Field Examination Activity**

The purpose of field examination activity is to complete all program and non-program field examinations, and other reports as required.

Field examination activity is responsible for

* assessing the beneficiaries’

  + competence
  + adjustment, and
  + personal welfare
* qualifying and selecting a fiduciary that is best suited to the needs and situation of the beneficiary
* recommending fees, and
* investigating and determining when a fiduciary’s misuse of a beneficiary’s funds is alleged or suspected.

Members of field examination activity must review reports and accuracy of information relating to the beneficiary and fiduciary.

---

**I.1.A.1.d . Purpose of Mail Activity**

The purpose of the mail teams is to ensure proper disposition of all mail received by the hub.

Mail teams are responsible for processing all documents and correspondence coming via acceptable sources.  Among these are incoming inquiries from beneficiaries, fiduciaries, and congressional requests.

***References***:  For more information on

* mail management, see [FPM, Part II, 1.A](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153964/), and
* centralized mail (CM) intake, see [M21-1, Part II, Subpart i, 1.A](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000174855/).

---

**I.1.A.1.e . Purpose of Accounting Activity**

The purpose of accounting activity is to conduct in-depth analysis of all accounting documents received in the hub.

The accounting team is responsible for

* auditing Federal and court accountings
* following up on accountings due from prior fiduciaries when successor fiduciaries have been appointed
* recommending changes of fiduciary when serious problems with fiduciary compliance are evident
* following up on miscellaneous diaries, and
* assisting with onsite reviews.

---

**I.1.A.1.f . Purpose of Misuse Activity**

The purpose of misuse activity is to work closely with District Counsel and the Office of Inspector General (OIG) to investigate and process misuse allegations.

Misuse activity is responsible for

* conducting an in-depth analysis, quasi-legal examination, detailed investigation and financial assessment of inquiries and documents pertaining to allegations of misuse
* establishing appropriate EPs for misuse allegations, investigations, and reports
* preparing development of misuse cases
* working with Support Services Division and the Debt Management Center (DMC) to collect, investigate, and prepare evidence for referral to OIG or District Counsel
* preparing misuse determinations and notifications for management concurrence
* collecting VA derived assets from fiduciaries found to have misused the beneficiary’s benefits
* reviewing evidence and responding to reconsideration requests
* referring misuse cases to an appropriate agency, OIG, and District Counsel
* providing recommendations to management, and
* assisting with onsite reviews.

**I.1.A.2.b . Cases Excluded From Fiduciary Promulgation Team Processing**

The following cases are excluded from the fiduciary promulgation team processing due to their unique processing requirements:

* Integrated Disability Evaluation System cases
* RACC cases
* foreign cases, and
* proposed ratings that contain adverse actions in addition to the competency issue.

Upon completing the final determination, the station of jurisdiction (SOJ) establishes the EP 290 *FID-Fiduciary Adjustment* and requests appointment of a fiduciary in accordance with [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 6.C](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177976/).

To control the fiduciary appointment in the system, the hub must

* review the request for fiduciary appointment
* cancel the EP 290 *FID-Fiduciary Adjustment*
* establish an EP 590 *Initial Appointment Field Exam*
* review and update the beneficiary profile in the system, as necessary, and
* follow the guidance in [FPM, Part I, 1.B.2.l](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153946/) to determine whether a referral to the National Instant Criminal Background Check System is required and take the associated required actions. .

Upon appointment of a fiduciary and where one of the exclusions noted above exists, the hub then

* completes the EP 590 *Initial Appointment Field Exam* in the system
* ensures the [*VA Form 21P-555, Certificate of Legal Capacity* *to Receive and Disburse Benefits and Fee Authorization*](http://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-555-ARE.pdf)*,* is uploaded to the electronic claims folder (eFolder)
* ensures the appropriate EP and claim label with the same date of claim as the cancelled EP 290 is active within the system for any follow-up actions required by the SOJ, and
* routes the EP to the SOJ using the appropriate procedures.

The SOJ completes the fiduciary adjustment award.

***Exception***:  Where fiduciary promulgation was initially excluded due to a proposed rating that contained adverse actions in addition to the competency issue and those adverse actions are no longer pending, the hub will release the retro amount under the appropriate EP 590.

***Notes***:

* For compensation-based claims requiring follow-up actions per this block, the appropriate EP and claim label is an EP 290 - *Fiduciary Adjustment*.
* For claims requiring follow-up actions per this block within the jurisdiction of a pension management center (PMC), the appropriate EP and claim label is an EP 290 - *PMC-Fiduciary Adjustment*.
* ***SOJ*** refers to the RO that is assigned to work a particular claim based on workload or other considerations.

***Reference***:  For more information on the routing of claims within the National Work Queue (NWQ), see the [*NWQ Playbook*](https://dvagov.sharepoint.com/sites/VACOOFOGeneral/SitePages/Home.aspx).

**I.1.A.3.a . Responsibilities of the FHM**

The Fiduciary Hub Manager (FHM) may

* delegate authority to perform any function for which the FHM is assigned responsibility, and
* restrict or suspend any such authority granted.

The FHM may delegate authority to sign certain document(s) on their behalf.  In these instances, the designee must sign the document(s) with their own name and indicate that they are the designee.

The FHM may identify the following delegations:

* Authority to sign [*VA Form 21P-555*](http://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-555-ARE.pdf) may be delegated to

  + fiduciary personnel to release benefits; however, in no instance may the employee who signed the [*VA Form 21P-555*](http://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-555-ARE.pdf) authorize the award to appoint the fiduciary, or
  + supervisory personnel or FSRs when fees are being authorized (limited to these individuals only).
* Authority to sign correspondence, such as letters and internal memos relating to fiduciary matters, may be delegated to supervisory personnel, FEs, FSRs, and Legal Instruments Examiners.  Delegations to sign correspondence should not be given to clerical personnel.  Employees are permitted to sign their own names to correspondence concerning cases for which they are responsible.

***Important***:  Any restriction or suspension of responsibilities that are assigned to subordinate personnel specifically by regulation or manual must be temporary.

***Example***:  An employee may be temporarily restricted from performing duties pending the employee’s qualification as a journeyman in the position.

**I.1.A.3.b . Responsibilities of District Counsel**

District Counsel, as field representative of the Office of General Counsel, is responsible for all legal activities in which VA is involved, including court-appointed fiduciary matters.

District Counsel may authorize hub employees who are also attorneys to represent VA in fiduciary matters.  Such employees are referred to as hub attorneys in this manual.

If there are hub attorneys, District Counsel may delegate authority to handle legal activities to those attorneys.  The delegation of authority must be in writing.

Legal activities that may be delegated to hub attorneys include the

* preparation of legal pleadings and briefs in court fiduciary proceedings, and
* presentation of cases before courts.

The legal work performed by hub attorneys is under the professional supervision of District Counsel.  Otherwise, hub attorneys are under the supervision of the FHM.

**I.1.A.3.c . Responsibilities of DMC**

The DMC, located in St Paul, MN, is responsible for collecting debts resulting from an individual’s participation in VA’s education, pension, or disability compensation programs under the DMC’s jurisdiction.  The DMC consults with Veterans and their families in the management and liquidation of their benefit debts.  Benefit debt (overpayment) collection is under the sole jurisdiction of the DMC.

DMC does not establish benefit debts, but solicits and collects all benefit debt (overpayment) following establishment of benefit debt by the RO of jurisdiction (ROJ).  Referrals for establishment of benefit debt must be processed by the ROJ.

Following the death of a beneficiary, hub personnel must not notify DMC to establish a benefit debt.  Benefit debts resulting from issuance of a monthly benefits following the death of a beneficiary must be established by the ROJ.

DMC may be contacted through hub management at:  [VAVBASPL/DMC/RECLAMATION](mailto:RECLAMATION.VAVBASPL@va.gov).

**I.1.A.3.e . Authority Regarding VA Determination of a Beneficiary’s Ability to Manage VA Benefits**

[38 CFR 3.353](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=c4a9b7ff7ceaa5eaa79cccba6e408cdc&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1353&rgn=div8) authorizes VA, in part, to make determinations regarding a beneficiary’s ability to manage VA benefits.  The determination is made after receipt and review of medical evidence that is clear, convincing, and leaves no doubt as to the beneficiary’s ability to manage VA benefits.

The RO rating activity is responsible for issuing a proposed rating and proposed determination that indicates the beneficiary cannot manage VA benefits.  At the time of the proposed determination, the RO must notify the beneficiary of the proposed action, the right to a hearing as provided in [38 CFR 3.103](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=c4a9b7ff7ceaa5eaa79cccba6e408cdc&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1103&rgn=div8), and any other notification required by law.

Hubs have the authority to finalize determinations of inability to manage VA benefits.  Additionally, hubs have expanded the authority for FSRs, who received qualified training, to

* complete a final rating regarding a beneficiary’s ability to manage VA benefits when

  + additional evidence is received during the due process period (unless one of the exceptions in [FPM, Part I, 1.A.2.b](#2b) applies), or
  + the FE submits a *Supervised Direct Pay Assessment* for restored competency, and
* conduct hearings that are requested during the due process period.

***Notes***:

* ***Rating activity*** refers to the VA employees who have authority under [38 CFR 3.353](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=4a28b571d8fe33a556ecc7ba435e2149&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1353&rgn=div8) to determine whether a beneficiary is unable to manage VA benefits.
* ***Proposed rating*** refers to the document detailing the decision proposed by the RO rating activity that the beneficiary is unable to manage VA benefits and includes the narrative of the decision and the codesheet.
* ***Proposed determination*** refers to the proposed rating and the notification to the beneficiary regarding the decision proposed by the RO rating activity that the beneficiary is unable to manage VA benefits.
* ***Final rating*** refers to the document detailing the final decision regarding the ability or inability to manage VA benefits.

***References***:  For more information on

* evaluating competency, see [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 6.A](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177974/)
* proposed rating decisions, see [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 3.D](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177968/)
* cases excluded from the expanded authority, see [FPM, Part I, 1.A.2.b](#2b)
* restored competency, see [FPM, Part I, 5.A.3](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000223123/)
* jurisdiction of

  + determinations of a beneficiary’s inability to manage VA benefits see [FPM, Part I, 1.A.2.c](#2c), and
  + evidence received after a final determination, see [FPM, Part I, 1.A.2.e](#2e), and
* additional evidence or hearing requests during due process period, see [FPM, Part I, 1.B.1.h](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153946/).

**I.1.A.3.f . Authority Regarding Judicial Determination of a Beneficiary’s Ability to Manage Affairs**

[38 CFR 3.353(e)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=58a3d5353dec0c16ff8e264f0dc32c3a&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1353&rgn=div8) prescribes that VA is not required to provide the beneficiary advanced notification of the determination that the beneficiary cannot manage VA benefits or the right to a hearing, when a court of jurisdiction

* declares a beneficiary unable to manage their financial affairs, or
* appoints a guardian based upon the beneficiary’s inability to manage their financial affairs.

VA will immediately complete a final determination and refer the court documents to the hub for appointment of a fiduciary as described in [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 6.D.2.g](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177977/).  In such cases, a proposed determination is not required.

If the beneficiary is a child and evidence is received indicating that they are permanently incapable of self-support, refer the case to the rating activity for a decision per [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 6.D.2.h](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177977/).

**I.1.A.3.g . Authority Regarding Withholding Retroactive Benefit Payments Pending Appointment of a Fiduciary**

Per [38 CFR 13.230,](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=58a3d5353dec0c16ff8e264f0dc32c3a&mc=true&node=se38.1.13_1230&rgn=div8) VA withholds all retroactive, one-time, or other lump-sum benefit payments until a fiduciary is appointed following the initial finding of incompetency and the fiduciary provides proof of a surety bond.

When a beneficiary is placed on supervised direct payment, all retroactive funds must remain in withholding until the beneficiary is rated competent or a fiduciary is appointed.

This policy protects any large, one-time benefit payment that the beneficiary may need for future care and services and that VA would not be able to reissue under [38 U.S.C. 6107](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/6107), if payments were made directly to the beneficiary prior to a fiduciary appointment.
