Decision Assessment Document
 VAOPGCPREC 13-98, Sep 23, 1998, Surviving Spouse’s Eligibility for Benefits - 38 U.S.C. 1311 (e) - Pub. L. No. 105-178, § 8207
 Office of General Counsel Precedent Opinion

**What the case is about**

Does a surviving spouse who regains eligibility for dependency and indemnity compensation (DIC) under 38 U.S.C. § 1311(e) as added by section 8207 of the Transportation Equity Act for the 21st Century also regain eligibility for medical care under the Department of Veterans Affairs Civilian Health and Medical Program (CHAMPVA), for dependents’ educational assistance, or for loan guaranty benefits?

**Impact on VBA**

A surviving spouse who regains eligibility for dependency and indemnity compensation (DIC) under 38 USC section 1311(e), either upon the termination of remarriage by death, divorce, or annulment, or upon the cessation of living with another person and holding himself or herself out openly to the public as that person’s spouse does not regain eligibility for medical care under CHAMPVA, for dependents educational assistance or for loan guaranty benefits.

**Summary on facts and findings**

The General Counsel noted that section 8207 of the Transportation Equity Act for the 21st Century, Pub. L. No. 105-178 section 8207, 112 Stat. 107, 495 (1998), did not amend any statute governing benefits other than DIC, nor did it change the definition of surviving spouse in 38 USC section 101(3).  It was concluded that, as a result, a surviving spouse who regained eligibility for DIC under section 1311(e) does not regain eligibility for benefits other than DIC, including medical care under CHAMPVA, dependents educational assistance (DEA or Chapter 35), and loan guaranty benefits.  The reasoning is that eligibility for those benefits is not based on eligibility for DIC, but rather on the claimant’s status as a surviving spouse as defined in section 101(3).

Various references in M21-1 discuss ancillary benefits.  These references are M21-1, Part VI, par. 3.10d(3) and par. 4.11a(1) which provide information about DEA benefits, M21-1, Part IV, par. 23.06 which covers eligibility for loan guaranty benefits, and Part IV, par 23.14a, which deals with CHAMPVA eligibility.  All of these references should be amended to include information concerning the nonreinstatement of eligibility to such benefits based upon this General Counsel opinion.

Ancillary benefits such as CHAMPVA, DEA, and loan guaranty benefits will not be awarded to recipients of DIC pursuant to 38 USC section 1311(e), for a surviving spouse who regained eligibility for DIC either upon the termination of remarriage by death, divorce, or annulment, or upon the cessation of living with another person and holding himself or herself out openly to the public as that person’s spouse.  (Apart from section 1311(e), eligibility for ancillary benefits may be established pursuant to 38 U.S.C. § 103(d) where a remarriage is terminated by annulment.)

RO training coordinators should conduct brief training sessions on this GC opinion.  Amend M21-1, Part VI, par. 3.10d(3) and 4.11a(1) which discusses ancillary benefits, specifically DEA.  Amend M21-1, Part IV, par. 23.06 which covers loan guaranty eligibility in death cases.  Amend M21-1, Part IV, par. 23.14a which discusses eligibility for CHAMPVA benefits.  Forward a copy of this assessment and the General Counsel opinion to the Director, Loan Guaranty Service and Director, Education Service.
