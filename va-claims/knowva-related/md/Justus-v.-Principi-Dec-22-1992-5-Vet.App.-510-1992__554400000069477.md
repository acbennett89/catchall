Decision Assessment Document
 Justus v. Principi, Dec 22, 1992, 5 Vet.App. 510 (1992)
 U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims (Court)

**What the case is about:**

The Court held that the ultimate credibility or weight of the evidence should be presumed initially, for the limited purpose of reopening the claim.

**Impact on VBA:**

The Court's holding has been incorporated into the Manual M21-1, III.iv.2.B.

**Summary of the facts and Court’s reasons:**

Service connection had been denied for a heart murmur noted at separation, as a residual of possible childhood rheumatic fever apparently.  A medical opinion was submitted for reopening, which indicated that the murmur more likely developed due to an undiagnosed episode of rheumatic fever during active duty, based on a history of repeated sore throat and chest cold symptoms provided by the veteran.  This opinion was not considered new and material by the BVA, because it relied on history conveyed by the veteran rather than on a review of contemporaneous clinical records.
