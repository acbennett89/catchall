**5.1.a . SOAR Definition**

A Strategic Oversight and Analysis Review (SOAR) is a formal review of an organizational element or an operational function of the Veterans Service Center (VSC), Benefits Eligibility Support Team (BEST), Disability Rating Activity Site (DRAS), Fiduciary Contact Center (FCC), Fiduciary Hub (hub), and Pension Management Center (PMC). SOARs are a self-audit technique to be used as a positive guide for operational analysis and improvement.

---

**5.1.b . Purpose of a SOAR**

A researched and well-written SOAR is a valuable management tool for reviewing operations to identify existing or potential problems and propose corrective actions, as well as identify best practices that may be applied to other areas.

---

**5.1.c . Primary Objectives of SOARs**

The primary objectives of a SOAR are to:

* assess the effectiveness and adherence to operational procedures
* monitor progress toward established goals and objectives pertinent to the subject area
* identify problems and recommend corrective actions
* identify operational procedures and oversight that are not currently included as part of the workload management plan (WMP)
* assess areas of vulnerability in operational procedures and oversight
* determine effectiveness in achieving program directives and in meeting Veterans Benefits Administration (VBA) priorities, targets, and goals
* verify records and reports accurately and properly reflect actual operations and results
* evaluate compliance with manuals, regulations, and other directive material
* refer recommendations for improvement in operations, policy or other directives, or other action by higher authority, and
* document a history of operational performance and analysis of performance.

**5.2.a . Management Responsibility**

The Veterans Service Center Manager (VSCM), Disability Rating Activity Site Manager (DRASM), Special Mission Program Officer (SMPO), Fiduciary Contact Center Manger (FCCM), Fiduciary Hub Manager (FHM), Pension Management Center Manager (PMCM), and Benefits Eligibility Support Team Manager (BESTM) are responsible for the ongoing analysis of division operations, to include SOARs required by this chapter, as well as those required by Regional Office (RO) policy.

Responsibilities include

* ensuring SOARs are completed according to this chapter
* ensuring the depth of analysis is sufficient to identify potential problem areas or establish best practices, and
* ensuring appropriate recommendations are tracked and completed.

***References***:  For more information on

* WMPs, see [M21-4, Chapter 2](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000097718/)
* claims and appeals processing timeliness, see[M21-5, Chapter 7](https://vaww.vrm.km.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_kanew/help/agent/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001034/topic/554400000018235/Chapter-7-Legacy-Appeal-Procedures), and
* control log for recommendations/action items, see [M21-4, Chapter 5, Topic 2.g](#2g).

---

**5.2.b . Who May Complete SOARs**

Completing SOARs is not limited to supervisory personnel. VSCMs, DRASMs, SMPOs, FCCMs, FHMs, PMCMs, and BESTMs  may assign SOARs to any personnel who are sufficiently experienced in division operations and who display analytical ability.

Consulting with subject matter experts and all levels of management during the SOAR planning, data gathering, and analysis portions is crucial to a successful result.

---

**5.2.c . Frequency of SOARs**

Each SOAR will be completed biannually or annually, during the designated SOAR windows.

| **If the SOAR is to be completed ...** | **Then the SOAR Window(s) is/are ...** |
| --- | --- |
| biannually | * December to February, and * June to August. |
| annually | June to August. |

***Note***:  The short time frame in between the two biannual windows allows for consistent and timely data collection. Additionally, efficacy of recommendations can be gauged and adjusted as necessary, to ensure improved compliance on a timely basis.

***Example***: The VSC/DRAS SOAR for Priority and Specialty Claims Processing will be submitted and concurred once during the December to February window and once during the June to August window.

The VSC/DRAS SOAR for Local Quality of Rating Veterans Service Representative (RVSR) Work will be submitted and concurred during the June to August window.

---

**5.2.d . Schedule of SOARs**

The VSCM/DRASM/SMPO/FCCM/FHM/PMCM/BESTM is responsible for preparing the SOAR schedule annually prior to the start of the fiscal year (FY). Schedules must align with the frequency requirement set forth in this chapter and must include:

* the person(s) responsible for completing the SOAR
* the position title of the person responsible for the report,
* the due date, and
* a tracker of the dates the completed SOARs were submitted.

---

**5.2.e . Necessity for More Frequent Analysis and/or Data Collection**

SOARs will be conducted on a schedule that, at a minimum, meets the requirements established in this chapter. However, analysis and data collection may be necessary more frequently than scheduled to determine trends, obtain facts for special reports, or determine quality in specified areas.

It may be necessary to analyze only a segment of an area of review more frequently than scheduled.

***Example***: If local quality reviews and findings were determined to be a problem, then this topic would be analyzed more frequently, assessing the effectiveness of recommendations until the situation is resolved. All findings must be retained and discussed in the next regularly scheduled SOAR on the topic.

---

**5.2.f . Extension Requests**

Extension requests must be documented, submitted to, and approved by the VSCM/DRASM/SMPO/FCCM/FHM/PMCM/BESTM prior to the scheduled due date. Extensions may be granted only within the current SOAR scheduling window.

***Note***: Extension approvals must be documented on the schedule and indicate the new approved due date.

***Example***: The SOAR scheduled due date is December 31. Coach submits a request for extension to the VSCM with reason for extension request on December 1. An extension may be granted by the VSCM within the current scheduling window to no later than February 28.

---

**5.2.g . Control Log for Recommendations**

A control log for the completion of SOAR recommendations is required.

This log must include the following:

* recommendation(s)
* action(s) to be completed
* evaluative criteria that will be used to measure success
* names of individual(s) and/or team(s) responsible for completing the   action(s)
* targeted due date(s), and
* actual completion date(s).

Control logs will be maintained for review as part of the associated SOAR for historical purposes. Documentation of the actions taken on the log with actual completion dates will help in monitoring compliance. This documentation is part of the originating SOAR, and must be discussed as part of the next scheduled SOAR.

***Reference***: For more information on the SOAR schedule, see [M21-4, Chapter 5, Topic 2.d](#2d).

**5.3.a . SOAR Format**

The format discussed in this section is required to ensure all topics, details, and analysis are contained in the SOAR.

***Reference***:  For an example of the required SOAR format, see [M21-4, Chapter 5, Appendix A](#A).

---

**5.3.b . Date and Title**

The date of completion of the SOAR must be included and the title will reflect the required SOAR topic reviewed.

***References***:  Form more information on SOAR topic requirements for

* VSC/DRAS SOARS  see [M21-4, Chapter 5, Topic 4](#4)
* BEST SOARS, see [M21-4, Chapter 5, Topic 5](#5)

* Hub SOARS, see [M21-4, Chapter 5, Topic 7](#7), and
* FCC SOARS, see [M21-4, Chapter 5, Topic 8](#8).

---

**5.3.c . Scope of Review**

The scope of review will include, at a minimum, a brief overview of the main topics covered, time period covered and date of review.

***Note***: Operational parameters should be explained, to provide context and clarity, if applicable (for example, changes during the review period, a pilot project that took place for three months out of the six-month review period, etc.).

---

**5.3.d . Review of Previous Recommendations**

Recommendations from the last SOAR report must be restated and include actions taken to implement the previous recommendations and the impact of those actions. The success of the implemented recommendations will be detailed in the findings and analysis sections.

If the previous recommendation was not implemented, an explanation of the mitigating circumstances will be included and/or the previous recommendation will be continued as a current recommendation in the next SOAR, if supported by the findings and analysis.

***Reference***:  For more information on the control log for recommendations, see [M21-4, Chapter 5, Topic 2.g](#2g).

---

**5.3.e . Current Findings and Analysis**

The current findings and analysis section will contain a summarization of the data reviewed and in-depth analysis of the data presented.

All required topics and sub-topics must provide:

* current facts, findings, and data to adequately address each topic under review
* in-depth (root cause) analysis of presented facts, findings, and data, to include
  + comparative analysis to that of another station, district, nation, (FY), or other review period, etc.
  + identified trends
  + discussion on identified deficiencies, potential problem areas, outliers, inefficiencies, and/or identified best practices, and
  + results of implemented recommendations/action items of any prior review.

***Important***:

Possible data and process review resources include (but not limited to): Tableau reports, Oracle Business Intelligence Enterprise Edition Reports, Workload and Time Reporting System, Quality Management System, M21-1 and M21-4 procedures, VBA policy or directives, and subject matter expert interviews, and must be cited appropriately in this section of the report.

* Ensure data is collected and maintained throughout the review period so all relevant data will be available for the SOAR.
* If existing data reports are not available, stations should use a sampling of local cases to conduct a review.
* When reviewing a process, a description of the process only is not sufficient and must include an in-depth analysis of what is/is not working and why.

---

**5.3.f . Conclusion**

The conclusion of the SOAR will provide a discussion of any identified potential problem areas and/or best practices.

***Important***:

* All conclusions should be supported by the facts and analysis presented in detail in the previous sections.
* Conclusions noted should be followed by recommendations for RO improvement or other process improvement ideas for District/Nation.

---

**5.3.g . Recommended Action to Improve or Resolve Problematic Areas**

When areas of improvement are identified by a SOAR, the report must include recommended actions to remedy the problem. Recommendations must be justified by the findings and analysis, which will be supported by data.

The recommended actions must include the

* action to be completed
* party responsible for completing the action
* date the action is to be completed, and
* evaluative criteria that will be used to measure success.

***Example***: On the first of every month, the Management Analyst will review deferral data to determine whether a decrease in avoidable deferrals caused by VSC/BEST is shown. Success will be measured by a reduction of X% by next SOAR review period.

***Reference***:  For more information on the control log for recommendations requirement, see [M21-4, Chapter 5, Topic 2.g](#2g).

---

**5.3.h . Signature Requirements and Approval of SOARs**

SOARs must include the following signatures and approvals:

* name and title of the SOAR author
* the date completed
* approval signature by the VSCM/DRASM/SMPO/FCCM/FHM/PMCM/BESTM, and
* concurrence signature by the Director (or designee no lower than an Assistant Director).

All concurrence signatures and dates will be captured and digitally saved to the electronic document and must be signed within the designated SOAR window.

**5.4.c . SOAR for Local Quality of RVSR Work**

The annual SOAR for Local Quality of RVSR Work must include analysis of

* clear and unmistakable errors (CUEs) citing your RO as RO in error, including
  + analysis of highest CUE category citing RO in error
  + analysis of trends and/or outliers in highest CUE category, to include who is causing highest CUEs, and
  + actions taken to reduce CUEs in this area
* created by deferrals (RFD to Open), including
  + analysis of highest category of deferrals created by RO
  + analysis of quality on created deferrals (was action requested accurate and necessary, most appropriate deferral reason selected, manual reference provided, and bookmarks identified), and
  + analysis of trends/outliers in highest category, to include who is creating most deferrals in this category
* actions taken to reduce deferrals in this area and validation to show improvement
* in process review (IPR) use, including

  + analysis of local IPR process to include selecting IPR categories, how and when corrective actions are taken and validation of such actions
  + analysis of error trends and outliers identified by IPRs
  + actions taken to reduce error trends and outliers identified by IPRs and validation to show improvement, and
  + analysis of IPR effectiveness to reduce errors within the claims process locally and nationally, and
* RO hearings, including

  + local procedures (how are requests placed under control and managed)
  + timeliness of scheduling, and
  + show rates.

**5.4.e . SOAR for Overdevelopment in Claims Processing**

The biannual SOAR for Overdevelopment in Claims Processing must include analysis of

* initial and supplemental development actions resulting in claim delays, including

  + whether procedural guidance for requesting federal or non-federal records was followed,
  + whether development for federal records was completed but not needed, and
  + whether development for non-federal records was completed but not needed.
* examination/opinion-related development actions resulting in claim delays, including

  + instances where both an examination and opinion were requested but not needed,
  + instances where only an examination was requested but not needed,
  + instances where only a medical opinion was requested but not needed,
  + whether examinations/opinions returned as insufficient were truly insufficient, and
  + whether examinations/opinions returned for clarification truly required clarification.
* other unnecessary development actions resulting in claim delays identified during the course of the review, including instances where a private Disability Benefits Questionnaire (DBQ) was of record and a VA examination was requested without merit.

***References***: For more information on

* duty to assist with obtaining records, see [M21-1, Part III, Subpart i, 2.C](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000014102/),

**5.4.f . SOAR for UVR**

The SOAR for Unclaimed Veteran Remains (UVR) must include an annual review and analysis of

* compliance with established targets (local, if applicable, and national)
* compliance with proper clearing/canceling of end products (EPs), including a review of a sample of EP 500s
* quality of completed claims using a sample review of completed work to include a root cause analysis of each error found
* accuracy of systems compliance
* local UVR standard operating procedures (SOPs) to ensure that all information is pertinent, current, and correct (if applicable), and
* local outreach efforts to ensure compliance with outreach requirements (M21-7) to include

  + a determination if the outreach hours are properly recorded and if the minimum number of required outreach hours per quarter is met
  + an assessment of the number and types of outreach engagements, and
  + quality of outreach activities.

The SOAR must also document that

* all Indigent Veterans and Unclaimed Remains Coordinators (IVURCs)

  + reviewed and acknowledged the UVR processing guidance in M21-1 annually
  + completed all UVR related Talent Management System (TMS) courses, and
  + are listed on the [IVURC Contact List](https://dvagov.sharepoint.com/sites/VBABASqualitytraining/bastrain/data/SiteAssets/coordinator/frame_pages/indigentBurial.aspx), and
* the RO

  + annually updates the point of contact (POC) sheet with designated VBA, Veterans Health Administration (VHA), and National Cemetery Administration (NCA) contacts, and
  + met quarterly with POCs from each site to discuss any personnel changes, challenges, metrics, and goals and review process flows to confirm the role of each administration.

***Note***:  The IVURC POC for each RO is determined by the RO Executive Director or designee.  This position is not limited to the VSC and may be an employee in the PMC or in the office of the RO Director.  There also may be multiple IVURCs at an RO.  Coordination of SOAR responsibility should be outlined when multiple IVURCs exist, to not duplicate efforts.

**5.5.a . Content of SOARs**

The review must cover all related factors outlined in each SOAR below.

***Notes***:

***Important***:  Each analysis must identify any potential problem areas drawn from assumptions and predictions based on the findings and analysis. **5.5.b . SOAR for Local Quality of VSR Work**

The annual SOAR for Local Quality of VSR Work must include analysis of

* local error trends on dependency claims (EPs 130 and 600), including

  + review of systems compliance
  + review of effective dates, and
  + communication of trends to leadership and employees.
* deferrals/reworks, including

  + highest category of deferrals/reworks created/caused by BEST (AWD back to VSR) to include who is creating and causing most in this category

  + local IPR process to include selecting IPR categories, how and when corrective actions are taken and validation of such actions

* BEST Hearings, including
  + local procedures to include communications with other ROs (how are requests placed under control and managed)
  + timeliness of scheduling, and
  + show rates.

  + [M21-1, Part X, Subpart v, 1.D](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000178000/), and

**5.5.c . SOAR for Internal Controls**

The biannual SOAR for Internal Controls must include analysis of

* review of compliance with proper clearing/canceling of EPs, including
  + review a sample of non-rating EPs and claim labels, and
  + review a sample of work items to include process for clearing/canceling work items.
* under/overpayments analysis oversight and compliance of controls to minimize compensation benefit under/overpayments (use of special issue) to include elections, waivers, and recoupments, including

  + review process for adding special issue
* effectiveness of the 2nd signature (Admin Decisions and $40K) including character of discharge (COD) determinations, including
  + review process and routing procedures, and
  + review who is responsible for signature, and
* proceeds, including local procedures for control and management

  + oversight and review
  + distribution, and
  + timeliness.

***References***:  For more information on

**5.6.a . Content of SOARs**

The review must cover all related factors outlined in each SOAR below.

***Notes***:

* The areas for review listed below indicate the minimum areas to be included.
* RO or Division management may expand the areas of consideration to ensure a thorough analysis is conducted in each process reviewed.
* National targets are those identified by the Directors Performance Plan.  Local targets are those identified by local management.  The latter is dependent on the station and if none exist, this should be noted in the final report.
* Sample reviews may need to be completed throughout the review period to accurately capture work in progress.  This data should be maintained by the PMCs for use in the SOAR.
* PMCs should identify any shortcomings or challenges which impact analysis of each sub-topic.  These items should then be submitted to the Pension and Fiduciary (P&F) Service, Quality, and Oversight mailbox ([VAVBAWAS/CO/P&F QUAL OVRST](mailto:VAVBAWAS/CO/P&F QUAL OVRST (Quality & Oversight) <PFTNGQUALOVRST.VBACO@va.gov>)) for consideration at the conclusion of each SOAR cycle.

**Important**:  Each analysis must identify any potential problem areas drawn from assumptions and predictions based on the findings and analysis.  All assumptions and predictions must be explained and supported with rationale that is clearly outlined and supported by quantitative and/or qualitative data (i.e., include sample reviews with results where applicable).

---

**5.6.b . PMC SOAR for Workload Assignment and Routing**

The annual SOAR for Workload Assignment and Routing must include analysis of

* non-rating workload
* rating workload
* VA priority workload (e.g.  Homeless, Former Prisoner of War)
* trainee workload
* non-VBMS work items, and
* capacity management.

Effectiveness of workload assignment and routing for the aforementioned sub-topics should be measured by

* accuracy of routing to appropriate teams and individuals
* manual routing by management and employees
* work queue management, both team and individual
* time in queue, and
* capacity management.

PMCs should include an analysis of impacts to timeliness and effectiveness (e.g., training needs, external prioritization of workload, etc.) in order to identify proactive methods of workload management to reduce these effects.

***Note***:  It is noted that there are no data sources available for the accuracy or manual routing of cases.  Instead, PMCs are encouraged to review a sample of cases, broken out by applicable teams and roles, and to conduct interviews with personnel to determine effectiveness.  PMCs should also review local routing rules for redundancies and incomplete or incorrect routing of workload.

***Reference***:  For more information on creating VBMS routing rules, see the [Workload Management Job Instruction Sheet](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/VBMS/docs/JIS_Workload_Management_20131106.pdf).

---

**5.6.c . PMC SOAR for Local Quality of Work**

The annual SOAR for Local Quality of Work must include analysis of

* compliance with established targets (local, if applicable, and national)
* local error trends to include
  + communication of trends to leadership and employees, and
  + remediation efforts for identified error trends, and
* timeliness of error corrections.

The SOAR for local quality of work should also include a sample review and analysis of

* local reconsideration decisions to include
  + overturn rates
  + quality of initial quality reviews, and
  + quality of reconsideration decisions
* locally completed reviews (i.e., those completed by the station’s Quality Review Team members) to include
  + quality of reviews, and
  + timeliness of review (from assignment to completion)
* in process reviews to include
  + quality of reviews, and
  + timeliness of feedback
* compliance with establishing claims in the correct system (e.g., VBMS, Share and Caseflow), and
* compliance with proper clearing/canceling of EPs and Work Items (must include sample review).

***Reference***:  For more information on proper claims establishment and maintenance, see [M21-4, Appendix B](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000011474/).

---

**5.6.d . PMC SOAR for Deferrals**

The annual SOAR for Deferrals must include analysis of

* quality of locally generated deferrals to include (must include sample review)

  + accuracy of created deferral, and
  + proper system action(s) to generate the deferral
* quality of locally resolved deferrals to include (must include sample review)

  + accuracy of actions taken, and
  + proper system action(s) to resolve the deferral
* manually resolved deferrals
* mitigated deferrals
* locally ‘Created by’ deferral trends to include

  + communication of trends to leadership and employees, and
  + remediation efforts for identified error trends
* locally ‘Caused by’ deferral trends to include

  + communication of trends to leadership and employees, and
  + remediation efforts for identified error trends, and
* timeliness of action (as measured by sample review).

***Note***:  It is noted that there are no data sources available to measure timeliness of action.  Instead, PMCs are encouraged to review a sample of cases, broken out by appropriate work types or teams, to determine average timeliness.

---

**5.6.e . PMC SOAR for Erroneous Payments**

The biannual SOAR for Erroneous Payments must include analysis of

* procedures and training for identifying erroneous payments
* administrative error decisions (must include sample review), to include
  + quality of decision, and
  + timeliness of action
* signature procedures for administrative error decisions, and
* maintenance of the erroneous payment log.

---

**5.6.f . PMC SOAR for Special Emphasis Review**

P&F Service will provide the PMCs with the designated topic for the annual SOAR for Special Emphasis Review The goal for this review is to conduct a deep dive analysis of a specific subset of the PMCs’ workload.  Upon completion, this report should be submitted to the P&F Service, Quality, and Oversight mailbox ([VAVBAWAS/CO/P&F QUAL OVRST](mailto:VAVBAWAS/CO/P&F QUAL OVRST (Quality & Oversight) <PFTNGQUALOVRST.VBACO@va.gov>)) for consideration.

The SOAR for the special emphasis review must include analysis of

* compliance with established
  + policy and procedure (must include sample review), and
  + targets and goals (local, if applicable, and national), and
* training materials and compliance.

***Example***:  Topics which may be chosen by P&F Service for the special emphasis review SOAR include, but are not limited to

* income adjustments
* notification letters (including Appeals Modernization Act (AMA) compliance)
* proper development (including medical examination/opinion and file/records requests), and
* temporary guidance.

**5.7.a . Content of SOARs**

SOARs must be performed annually or biannually during the designated SOAR windows and must cover all related factors outlined in each SOAR below. Each SOAR must include a control log as described in [M21-4, Chapter 5, Topic 2](#2).

***Notes***:

* The areas for review listed below indicate the minimum areas to be included.
* Division management may expand the areas of consideration to ensure a thorough analysis and that each SOAR meets the purpose and objectives described in M21-4, Chapter 5, [Topic 1.b](#1b) and [c](#1c).
* Hubs should identify any shortcomings or challenges which impact analysis of each sub-topic.  These items should then be submitted to the P&F Service, Quality, and Oversight mailbox ([VAVBAWAS/CO/P&F QUAL OVRST](mailto:VAVBAWAS/CO/P&F QUAL OVRST (Quality & Oversight) <PFTNGQUALOVRST.VBACO@va.gov>)) for consideration at the conclusion of each SOAR cycle.

***Important***:  Each analysis must identify any problem and/or potential problem areas drawn from assumptions and predictions based on the facts and findings.  All assumptions and predictions must be explained and supported with rationale that is clearly outlined and supported by quantitative and/or qualitative data (for example, include sample reviews with results where applicable).

---

**5.7.b . SOAR for Field Examinations and Onsite Reviews**

The annual SOAR for Field Examinations and Onsite Reviews must include analyses of

* timeliness, and
* quality, to include a root cause analysis of each error found

All analyses must address each of the following areas:

* annual written contact scheduling and compliance
* successor initial appointment (SIA) reports (timeliness and trend analysis of the reasons for the SIA request by category)
* supervised direct pay cases (reviewed for compliance with diary and follow-up procedures)
* out of control field examination cases
* fund usage field examination misuse allegation establishment compliance, and
* onsite reviews (adherence to policies and procedures by reviewing a sample of cases).

***Note***:  Hubs must review onsite reviews, broken out by scheduled and unscheduled, to determine average completion timeliness and resolution of outstanding items on the *Onsite Review Correction Log*.

---

**5.7.c . SOAR for Financial Reviews**

The biannual SOAR for Financial Reviews, for Federal and court accountings and fund usage reviews, must include analyses of

* ADC
* disapproved accountings (quality)
* accepted and rejected fund usage reviews (quality)
* appropriate and timely actions when potential misuse identified, and
* beneficiary profile diary dates to determine whether the financial oversight type is consistent with policies and procedures (for example, is an accounting required, or is the protection required of record).

---

**5.7.d . SOAR for Misuse**

The annual SOAR for Misuse must include analyses of overall timeliness and accuracy of allegation through investigation.  In addition, include a targeted outcome analysis of the following:

* investigations
* determinations
* reconsiderations
* *Additional Action Memorandum* trends, and
* Office of Inspector General referrals.

***Note***:  If formal reporting/data is not available, the hub should review a sample of cases.

---

**5.7.e . SOAR for Quality Review Teams**

The annual SOAR for Quality Review Teams must include analyses of

* compliance with [M21-4, Chapter 8](https://vaww.vrm.km.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_kanew/help/agent/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001034/content/554400000127439/Chapter-8-Fiduciary-Hub-Hub-and-Fiduciary-Contact-Center-FCC-National-Quality-Reviews-and-Quality-Review-Teams-QRT), to include

  + RO accuracy
  + local quality reviews and findings trend analysis
  + timeliness of national and local quality review error corrections, and
  + overturned local quality review error trend analysis
* methods of tracking and providing feedback/mentorship, to include trends and analysis, and
* WMP oversight and effectiveness on areas reviewed above identified as deficient or out of line.

---

**5.7.f . SOAR for Hub Administration**

The annual SOAR for Hub Administration must include analyses of WMP compliance, to include an analysis of the effectiveness of

* average lifecycle timeliness, to include

  + rating decision time
  + award generation
  + authorization time
* special claims processing such as

  + homeless cases
  + elderly
  + very seriously ill
  + the disbursement of VA-derived funds to deceased beneficiary’s estate plan, or
  + other prioritized cases
* supervisory visits
* zip code routing
* EP cancellations and closures
* beneficiary record management, to include the monthly review of an appropriate beneficiary record report, and
* deactivation of beneficiary records, to include the timely deactivation of appropriate minor beneficiary records upon reaching the age of majority.

---

**5.7.g . SOAR for Administrative Tasks**

The SOAR for Administrative Tasks must include a biannual review and analyses of

* completed administrative tasks, including

  + quality (for example, was the administrative task established consistent with the local WMP and/or procedures), and
  + trends/outliers of administrative task usage (for example, was the most appropriate administrative task title selected, are administrative tasks completed in line with WMP targets, was the administrative task created in error, and was the description consistent with the WMP)
  + quality of pending tasks (for example, was the administrative task established consistent with the local WMP and/or procedures), and
* the timeliness of the following pending and completed administrative tasks for the

  + status of

    - debt collections
    - corporate actions, and
    - successor funds transfers, and
  + receipt of

    - surety bonds
    - escheat funds returning to VA, and
    - certified court accountings.

***Note***:  Hubs must review a sample of cases, broken out by administrative task title to measure average timeliness.

---

**5.7.h . Hub SOAR for Special Emphasis Review**

P&F will provide the hubs with the designated topic for the annual special emphasis review, when necessary.  The goal for this review is to conduct a deep dive analysis of a specific subset of the hub’s workload.  Upon completion, this report should be submitted to the P&F Service, Quality, and Oversight mailbox ([VAVBAWAS/CO/P&F QUAL OVRST](mailto:VAVBAWAS/CO/P&F QUAL OVRST (Quality & Oversight) <PFTNGQUALOVRST.VBACO@va.gov>)) for consideration.

The SOAR for the Special Emphasis Review must include analysis of

* compliance with established

  + policy and procedure (must include sample review), and
  + targets and goals (local, if applicable, and national), and
* training materials.

***Example***:  Topics which may be chosen by P&F for the special emphasis review SOAR include, but are not limited to

* notification letters (including AMA compliance)
* appeal decisions relating to the appointment of a fiduciary, and
* temporary guidance.

***Reference***:  For more information on jurisdiction for issuing an appeal decision relating to a fiduciary appointment, see [M21-5, Chapter 7, D.1.b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000140652/).
