**II.A.1.a . PNTC Overview**

Per [5 CFR 410.201](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=1cf9397e5d7ca0c4954d3a9ae8b1efa8&mc=true&node=se5.1.410_1201&rgn=div8), Pension & Fiduciary (P&F) Service is responsible for creating training programs that support an agency workforce capable of achieving agency mission and performance goals, and facilitating continuous improvement of employee and organizational performance.

* The pension management center (PMC) Pension National Training Curriculum (PNTC) is consistent with the statutory definition in that it prescribes specific programs of training designed to improve both individual and organizational performance.
* The PNTC provides guidance to ensure all employees who process pension claims are empowered to develop and acquire the technical skills necessary to maintain competency in their positions, deliver excellent customer service, and improve the Veteran experience.

---

**II.A.1.b . PNTC Excluded Training**

PNTC does not apply to any positions outside of a PMC such as:

* Veterans Service Center employees
* fiduciary hub employees
* positions that fall under the jurisdiction of Benefits Assistance Service or any business line other than P&F Service
* informal training such as mentoring sessions, and
* Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)-wide mandatory training such as Cyber Security, No FEAR, Privacy, Ethics, and Prevention of Sexual Harassment.

---

**II.A.1.c . PMC/RO/TM Responsibilities**

PMCs/Regional Offices (ROs) will support and encourage employee training to improve the delivery of pension benefits to Veterans.

The responsibilities of the Training Manager (TM) and/or designee are to

* establish training plans at the beginning of every fiscal year (FY) to ensure that employees complete the designated number of PNTC hours
* submit the following to the P&F Service Training Team SharePoint [*Document Upload*](https://dvagov.sharepoint.com/sites/vbapftraining/SitePages/Document-Upload.aspx):

  + training plans within 45 calendar days of the start of the FY, and
  + a quarterly training compliance report within eight business days following the end of each quarter during the FY, unless otherwise directed by P&F Service, and
* submit the following to the P&F Service Training Team mailbox at [VBACOPFSTrainingTeam@va.gov](mailto:VBACOPFSTrainingTeam@va.gov):

  + a list of new hires with their Talent Management System (TMS) identification (ID), email addresses, and class dates (beginning and end date) to be added to a class in the Veterans Benefits Administration (VBA) Family Application Portal for Knowledge Check purposes, and survey purposes, no later than two weeks before the start of the new hire Core Pension Training (CPT), and
  + a list of the names, TMS IDs, email addresses, and class dates (beginning and end date) of CPT graduates and instructors upon graduation for end-of-program survey purposes.

The PMC will ensure

* compliance with this directive
* continuous monitoring for local quality trends to identify areas for improvement through training, and
* scheduling and delivery of instructor-led training (ILT).

PMCs must ensure that local instructors are

* qualified to deliver training
* utilizing training materials authorized by Compensation Service (CS) and P&F Service, and
* administering courses according to the approved lesson plans.

***Example***:  If the lesson plan states that the course is instructor-led, then the instructor must provide ILT by utilizing the associated training packet provided by P&F Service.

***Note***: If utilizing training material owned by CS, PMCs must ensure local instructors utilize training material authorized by CS and if the lesson plan states the course is instructor-led, the instructor must provide ILT by using the associated training packet provided by CS.

**II.A.2.a . PNTC Audience**

The PNTC requires annual training for all employees in the PMCs who process pension claims, including:

* Veterans Service Representatives (VSRs)
* Rating Veterans Service Representatives (RVSRs)
* Decision Review Officers (DROs), and
* Quality Review Team (QRT)
  + Authorization Quality Review Specialists (AQRSs), and
  + Rating Quality Review Specialists (RQRSs).

---

**II.A.2.b . FY Mandated Training Curriculum**

FY mandated training courses are developed for specific topics identified by P&F Service based on national quality trends and emerging issues.

All FY mandated training items are

* determined by P&F Service
* identified in the *[VBA Learning Catalog](https://vba-tpi.vbatraining.org/lc/)*, and
* assigned to the target audience’s TMS learning plan by P&F Service.

---

**II.A.2.c . PMC-Selected Training**

PMC-selected training is determined by the PMC based on analysis of local quality reviews, Systematic Technical Accuracy Reviews (more commonly known as STAR), national quality trends, and requirements for a particular position.  PMC-selected training is identified in the *[VBA Learning Catalog](https://vba-tpi.vbatraining.org/lc/)* and will count toward meeting the requirements for PNTC.

---

**II.A.2.d . TMS Terms**

TMS is the VA system of record for all VA training data.

Frequently used terms in TMS include

* ***TMS cohort*** - tool used to group users who have a common set of primary requirements (e.g., the VSR Primary cohort groups all employees with VSR-related training requirements)
* ***Level I evaluations*** - surveys administered to gather students’ feedback and thoughts regarding their training experience, and
* ***Level II assessments*** - exams that measure students’ knowledge, skills, and attitudes as a result of the training.

***Reference***:  For more information about TMS, see the [TMS website](https://launcher.myapps.microsoft.com/api/signin/3c9f19d2-1cb5-4bde-a0ff-f984f4e3c242?tenantId=e95f1b23-abaf-45ee-821d-b7ab251ab3bf).

---

**II.A.2.e . Definition: Training**

Training is the process of providing for and making available to an employee, and placing or enrolling the employee in, a planned, prepared, and coordinated program, course, curriculum, subject, system, or routine of instruction or education, in scientific, professional, technical, mechanical, trade, clerical, fiscal, administrative, or other fields, which will improve individual and organizational performance and assist in achieving the agency’s mission and performance goals.

***Reference***:  For more information on the definition of training for Federal employees, see [5 U.S.C. 4101(4)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/5/4101).

---

**II.A.2.f . Definition: Trainee Status**

The status of an employee during their time in a position is defined by the

* timeframe needed to complete the required training curricula, and
* ability to demonstrate basic proficiencies in job-related tasks.

---

**II.A.2.g . Learning Catalog**

The *[VBA Learning Catalog](https://vba-tpi.vbatraining.org/lc/)* is an online training catalog organized specifically to support VBA training programs.  The purpose of the catalog is to aggregate training materials so that employees can easily locate and complete training courses that provide further competency in P&F Service target areas.

Courses in the *[VBA Learning Catalog](https://vba-tpi.vbatraining.org/lc/)* are identified and searchable by

* *audience* - find a training topic for a certain audience (e.g., RVSR)
* *curriculum* - find a training topic for a certain curriculum (e.g., pension mandated training)
* *category* - find a training topic for a certain category (e.g., entry level), or
* *design/delivery* - find a training topic for a certain design/delivery option (e.g., online or webinar).

***Reference***:  For more information on the VBA Learning Catalog, see the user guide found on the [*VBA Learning Catalog*](https://vba-tpi.vbatraining.org/lc/?AspxAutoDetectCookieSupport=1) website.

**II.A.3.b . PNTC FY Training Hour Requirements**

All VSRs, RVSRs, DROs, and QRT members in the target audience must complete a minimum of 40 hours of PNTC training during the FY.  At least 20 of those hours are mandated training determined by P&F Service and the remaining 20 hours are selected training determined by the PMC.

***Note***:  Trainees do not fall under this requirement.

**II.A.4.e . Training Evaluation and Monitor Controls**

Training is recorded in TMS upon completion of all elements contained within the item.  Completion of the TMS evaluations and assessments are required for credit.

The Training Management and Performance Improvement staff in the Office of Human Capital Services will monitor, analyze, and complete reporting requirements for evaluations and comprehension assessments in TMS to ensure compliance with the PNTC standards.

Program participants, their supervisors, and their instructors should be aware of the data sources, collection methods, and time frames.

The table below provides the CPT formative data collection methods.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily Collection Method** | **Data Source** | **Timeframe** |
| Daily Participant Survey | Participant | Locally assigned at the end of each day |
| Knowledge Checks/Learning Assessments | Participant | As needed throughout all phases of training |
| End of Course Survey | Participant | At the end of each course, whether instructor-led or self-paced |

The table below provides the CPT summative data collection methods.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily Collection Method** | **Data Source** | **Timeframe** |
| End of Program (EOP) graduate survey | Graduates | Following completion of all portions of the program |
| EOP Instructor Survey | Instructors | Following instructor-led portions of the training |
| Three-month and six-month post-training graduate surveys | Graduates | Three and six months after the EOP survey |
| Three-month and six-month post-training instructor surveys | Supervisors of Graduates | Three and six months after the EOP survey |
| Nine-month and 12-month post-training graduate surveys | Graduates | Nine and 12 months after the EOP survey |
| Nine-month and 12-month post-training instructor surveys | Supervisors of Graduates | Nine and 12 months after the EOP survey |

***Notes***:

* A minimum response rate of 80 percent is preferred for all CPT participant surveys, although 100 percent is ideal.  If compliance does not reach 80 percent, the data collection window will be extended for one additional week.  A minimum response rate is not required for other data sources, such as supervisors or instructors.
* TMS evaluations and assessments are requirements of the training item and apply to all employees.  Training credit will be withdrawn if the employee fails to complete the TMS evaluation and/or comprehensive assessments.  TMs will be alerted before the credit is withdrawn.
* End of program surveys and follow-up survey items are assigned by P&F Service.
* The assessment and survey results are accessible to TMs via the [VBA Family Applications Portal/Core Vet](https://vba-tpi.vbatraining.org/vbaFamily/Account/Login?ReturnUrl=%2FvbaFamily%2F).

**II.A.5.a . Authorized Training Materials**

PMCs will only use training materials and lesson plans available from the *[VBA Learning Catalog](https://vba-tpi.vbatraining.org/lc/)* or [TMS website](https://launcher.myapps.microsoft.com/api/signin/3c9f19d2-1cb5-4bde-a0ff-f984f4e3c242?tenantId=e95f1b23-abaf-45ee-821d-b7ab251ab3bf) to ensure consistency of training across VBA.  PNTC courses must be taught according to the approved lesson plans provided by CS and P&F Service.  If the Learning Catalog design/delivery notes multiple delivery methods, the training coordinator may select the delivery mode to utilize.

All training delivered as part of the PNTC must have a nationally-assigned TMS number.  Training provided by ROs without a TMS number assigned will not be counted as part of the required PNTC.

---

**II.A.5.b . Attendance Roll Call for Instructor-Led Courses**

***In-person training session***:  Roll call sheets are required to record attendance for in-person instructor-led courses.  The instructor or PMC designated personnel will verify the roll call attendance sheet by certifying all names on the roster were in attendance.

***Online training session***:  Roll call sheets taken from virtual training sessions can be used to substantiate the attendance of individuals participating in an online training session.  The instructor or PMC designated personnel will verify the attendance sheet by certifying all names found were in attendance.

***Note***:  The RO TM or designee is required to keep the roll call sheets for the previous and current FY.

---

**II.A.5.c . Locally Created Training**

Training materials created by the RO for local use cannot be used for training until approved by P&F Service.  PMCs must submit the entire lesson content (e.g., lesson plan, student handout, PowerPoint presentation, practical exercises and answer keys, etc.) to the [Field Inquiry Tool](https://apps.gov.powerapps.us/play/e/default-e95f1b23-abaf-45ee-821d-b7ab251ab3bf/a/020fd6a3-0920-4fdd-aa7f-b2738264218e?tenantId=e95f1b23-abaf-45ee-821d-b7ab251ab3bf&hint=3033d727-bd9a-4996-ad9e-1dbd7ed2be3c&sourcetime=1727662207701&source=portal) and the P&F Training Team SharePoint [*Document Upload*](https://dvagov.sharepoint.com/sites/vbapftraining/SitePages/Document-Upload.aspx) for approval.

P&F Service requires a minimum of 30 business days to review the material and process the lesson through concurrence before it is approved for use in the field.  Upon lesson approval, P&F Service will create a national TMS item number and assign it to the appropriate TMS cohort within the PNTC.

P&F Service holds the sole authority to include training items in the PNTC.

**II.A.5.d . Training Material Comments and Updates**

PMCs are encouraged to submit comments or suggested updates for training materials to the [Field Inquiry Tool](https://apps.gov.powerapps.us/play/e/default-e95f1b23-abaf-45ee-821d-b7ab251ab3bf/a/020fd6a3-0920-4fdd-aa7f-b2738264218e?tenantId=e95f1b23-abaf-45ee-821d-b7ab251ab3bf&hint=3033d727-bd9a-4996-ad9e-1dbd7ed2be3c&sourcetime=1727662207701&source=portal).

Suggested material updates must include the following information

* lesson title
* document type (instructor lesson plan, student handout, visual, or handout, etc.)
* TMS number
* page and paragraph needing the update, and
* description and reference for the update.

***Important***:

* PMC employees will forward their comments to the RO TM or PMC QRT supervisor to forward to P&F Service.
* For comments about Training and Performance Support Systems modules, please utilize the comment selection located on each storyboard, which is automatically reported to CS or P&F Service.

**II.A.5.e . PNTC Make-Up Training**

All employees considered members of a target audience are required to complete the mandated training.  Therefore, each PMC must develop a strategy for conducting make-up training sessions as necessary.  Procedures for make-up training sessions should include the bullets below.

* The P&F Service lesson plan for each training item will be followed for all training offerings.
* The approved materials and handouts, including online exercises, media demonstrations and/or evaluations, provided by CS and P&F Service for each training offering, will be distributed to training participants.
* Typically, the same instructor will facilitate every offering of a required training item.
* If a session recording is provided as a make-up session, a subject matter expert (SME) in the training topic must be made available as the point of contact during the training, should questions arise.
* All session recordings should be housed in a central location on each PMC drive so that any member, in the target audience, may have access.
* PMC-selected training make-up will be determined at the PMC level and by individual training needs using approved materials.
* Make-up sessions must take place as soon as possible, and within eight weeks of the original training session.
* Roll call sheets and/or Microsoft Teams are required to record ILT attendance, including the name of the SME who facilitated the make-up session.  All sign-in sheets are strongly encouraged to be converted to an Excel spreadsheet; the converted Excel spreadsheet is highly recommended to be placed in a secure and accessible location in the event that verification of training attendance is requested.

**II.A.5.f . Instructor Expectations**

To improve the quality of training employees receive, VBA has initiated an instructor certification program.  The Employee Learning Inspires True Excellence (ELITE) Instructor Certification Program is a rigorous instructor candidate training and evaluation program that ensures VBA trainers have demonstrated the essential knowledge and skills needed to conduct effective virtual and face-to-face training.

The ELITE Instructor Certification Program is aligned with industry standards and ensures employees receiving training will receive consistent and enhanced instruction.

Instructors participating in the program will complete the

* ELITE Instructor Training Course prerequisite web-based training covering core instructor principles (20 hours)
* Comprehensive Exam that requires candidates to demonstrate knowledge of the core principles (50 questions - 1 hour), and
* ELITE Practicum and Observation/Evaluation event, which provides an opportunity for candidates to practice their instructor techniques, receive feedback, and present their refined skills for evaluation.  (9 hours)

***Important***:  Though not required at this time, it is ***highly recommended*** as a best practice for those with responsibility for the delivery of instruction materials that they complete ELITE training.

***Reference***:  For more information on the ELITE certification program, see the [Training Center of Excellence ELITE Program Overview website](https://dvagov.sharepoint.com/sites/vbatcoe/SitePages/How-Do-I-Become-an-ELITE-Certified-Instructor-.aspx).

---

**II.A.5.g . Instructor Preparation Time**

Instructors may receive TMS credit for time spent preparing for and instructing courses following the approved lesson plans.  PMCs may grant the following credit to instructors as noted in the bullets below.

* An RO TM or designee may grant an instructor up to 20 hours of credit for course preparation time each FY.  The TM will assign TMS item (VA 3786223) as completed.  This item is a 2-hour item that can be re-assigned as needed to match the actual preparation time.  These hours will count toward PNTC requirements.
* The TM may also add the item the instructor taught on the instructor’s learning plan.  Upon successful completion of any online content (assessment, instructor evaluations, etc.), the instructor may receive credit for PNTC items taught.

**II.A.6.a . Training Plan Reporting Requirement**

ROs are required to submit a training plan for each target audience within 45 calendar days of the beginning of the FY.  The training plans will be uploaded to the P&F Service Training Team SharePoint [*Document Upload*](https://dvagov.sharepoint.com/sites/vbapftraining/SitePages/Document-Upload.aspx).

Training plans should remain flexible throughout the year.  ROs are encouraged to adjust the training plans as needed to address changes in policy, procedures, and error trends.

The RO may use the training plans published on the CS [Training website](https://cptraining.vba.va.gov/C&P_Training/Trng_Plans/TP_RVSR.htm) as a guidance tool.

---

**II.A.6.b . Training Compliance Reporting Requirement**

All PMCs are required to submit quarterly compliance training reports to P&F Service.  The reports will demonstrate reasonable progress toward the yearly training requirement for PNTC.  TMs must submit the training compliance reports to the P&F Service Training Team SharePoint [*Document Upload*](https://dvagov.sharepoint.com/sites/vbapftraining/SitePages/Document-Upload.aspx).  The reports are due no later than seven business days following the end of each quarter (December, March, June, and September).

TMs are encouraged to utilize the [NTC Progress Report Tool](https://vba-tpi.vbatraining.org/ntcprogressreport/) when submitting the quarterly compliance reports for the PMCs.

The format for the report is based on readily available TMS reports.

---

**II.A.6.c . Prorating Training Availability Time for PNTC**

Prorating training availability time for PNTC training requirements will be allowed in certain circumstances as noted in the bullets below.

* PMC employees becoming eligible for PNTC training after the first month of the FY will be prorated for the remaining months of the FY (i.e., if an employee completes their entry level training and is assigned to the TMS primary cohort in January, then the time available for training is reduced to nine months).
* Employees on extended periods of excused absence from their PMC, to include

  + consecutive active duty or active duty for training absence for 30 consecutive days or more
  + accumulative active duty or active duty for training absence for 30 consecutive days or more
  + consecutive leave for 30 days or more including approved leave under the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA), or
  + accumulative sick leave or FMLA for 30 consecutive days or more; single days of sick leave that are not excused by a physician will not count as time applied for prorated hours.

Proper documentation will be maintained with the TM for the current FY for compliance purposes.  A memorandum from the employee’s immediate supervisor will suffice as proper documentation to include the name of the employee, period of time, and number of days that have been approved as extended periods of leave.  The documentation will be kept confidential and will only be presented to the RO director’s office for compliance purposes.  The documentation will be kept for the current and previous FY.

**II.A.6.d . Example: Documentation for Extended Excused Absence**

Below is an example documentation for extended excused absence.

***Memorandum***

*From:* **(Employee’s Immediate Supervisor’s Name)**
*To:* **(RO Name)** *Training Manager
Subject:* **(Employee Name)** *Approved Extended Leave*

*The above-mentioned employee has been authorized an extended absence as follows:*

* *March 1 to April 1, YYYY:  Extended approved sick leave 31 days*
* *June 1 to July 1, YYYY:  Extended approved military leave 30 days*

*Signed by* **(Immediate Supervisor)**

***Note***:  A wet signature is not required.

**II.A.6.e . Calculating Prorated Items**

Calculation of prorated items:

* Proration will only apply to PMC-selected training items.
* Mandated items typically represent changes in processes or procedures, and therefore must be completed by everyone.

***Note***:  Proration is automatically calculated in the VBA NTC Progress Reporting Application.
