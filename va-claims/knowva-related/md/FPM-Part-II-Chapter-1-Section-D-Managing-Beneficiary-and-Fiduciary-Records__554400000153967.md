## 1.  General Information Regarding Establishing and Deactivating Beneficiary Records

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains general information regarding establishing and deactivating beneficiary records, including

* [general information on establishing a beneficiary profile](#1a)
* [consolidating corporate records to establish a beneficiary profile](#1b)
* [discontinuation of Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) supervision](#1c), and
* [when to deactivate a beneficiary record](#1d).

---

**Change Date**

October 31, 2022

---

**II.1.D.1.a . General Information on Establishing a Beneficiary Profile**

In situations where the Veterans Benefits Management System (VBMS) does not automatically create a beneficiary profile for a beneficiary who requires fiduciary hub (hub) oversight, hubs must create the profile manually.

To avoid creating a duplicate beneficiary profile, hubs must confirm that the profile does not already exist in VBMS by performing searches against all available fields, to include

* FIRST NAME
* LAST NAME
* SSN, and
* FILE NUMBER.

***References***:  For more information on

* when VBMS automatically establishes beneficiary profiles, see [FPM, Part I, 1.B.2.i](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153946/), and
* creating and updating beneficiary profiles, creating insurance beneficiary profiles, or adding person profiles in VBMS, see the *VBMS Core User Guide*.

---

**II.1.D.1.b . Consolidating Corporate Records to Establish a Beneficiary Profile**

When establishing beneficiary profiles in the VBMS, the hub may encounter a beneficiary with two or more corporate records.  The hub must take appropriate action to consolidate the corporate records prior to establishing the beneficiary profile.

***Reference***:  For more information on processing duplicate corporate records, see the [Duplicate Record Consolidation Materials](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/21/publicat/Users/Index.htm).

---

**II.1.D.1.c . Discontinuation of VA Supervision**

Supervision of beneficiaries is discontinued when

* Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) benefits are terminated permanently, and no residual VA funds under management (FUM) exists, or
* the beneficiary no longer requires supervision in the handling of VA FUM.

***Note***:  Residual VA FUM does not include benefit debt/overpayment.

---

**II.1.D.1.d . When to Deactivate a Beneficiary Record**

Usually, a beneficiary record is deactivated when supervision is terminated.  Depending upon the type of beneficiary and fiduciary, certain actions may be required prior to deactivating the beneficiary record.

***Note***:  The beneficiary record must also be deactivated when the beneficiary cannot be located prior to the appointment of a fiduciary and benefits are suspended at the conclusion of the whereabouts unknown process outlined within [FPM, Part I, 2.B.2.f and h](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153948/).  If the beneficiary is located, the hub must reactivate the record, resume benefits, and complete an initial appointment (IA) field examination to appoint a fiduciary.

***Important***:  The beneficiary record should ***not*** be deactivated when

* whereabouts are unknown, and a fiduciary has already been appointed, or
* there are pending end products (EPs) or administrative tasks that must be resolved such as a request for a certified court accounting or funds transfer.

***References***:  For more information on

* the specific actions required for

  + deactivating the beneficiary record based on beneficiary type, see [FPM, Part II, 1.D.2](#2) and [3](#3), and
  + handling a change of fiduciary or payee, see [FPM, Part II, 1.B.2.d](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153965/), and
* deactivating a beneficiary record in VBMS, see the *VBMS Core User Guide*.

## 2.  Deactivating Beneficiary Records Based on Beneficiary Type

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information on the requirements for deactivating beneficiary records based on beneficiary type, including

* criteria for deactivating the beneficiary record of a(n)
  + [minor](#2a)
  + [adult with a VA-appointed fiduciary](#2b)
  + [beneficiary living in a contracted facility](#2c)
  + [deceased beneficiary](#2d)
  + [spouse fiduciary](#2e)
  + [temporary fiduciary](#2f)
  + [beneficiary whose competency has been restored](#2g), and
  + [beneficiary that is no longer eligible for VA benefits](#2h), and
* [review of a minor beneficiary record upon reaching majority](#2i).

---

**Change Date**

**II.1.D.2.a . Criteria for Deactivating the Beneficiary Record of a Minor**

Beneficiary records for cases involving minors are usually deactivated when the minor

* attains majority under the law of the State in which they reside
* marries
* enters military service
* has eligibility terminated and there is no VA FUM
* cannot be located and benefits are suspended for whereabouts unknown prior to the appointment of an initial fiduciary
* is paid via direct payment as described in [FPM, Part I, 2.A.2.h](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153947/), or
* dies and the criteria outlined in [FPM, Part II, 1.D.2.d](#2d) have been met.

***Notes***:

* If a minor attains majority under the law of the State in which they reside, the case should be deactivated even if the minor is moving to another state having a different age of majority.  When the beneficiary is entitled to benefits after majority, the hub must take action to notify the authorization activity and/or insurance center as provided in [FPM, Part II, 1.B.2.d](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153965/).
* A case must *not* be deactivated if the beneficiary requires supervision as a helpless child who is rated incompetent.
* If there is a VA-appointed fiduciary who is also court-appointed, the court must be notified when VA no longer has any interest in the case.

***References***:  For more information on

* the effect of a child's incapacity for self-support, see [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 6.B.1.a](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177975/)
* protection of FUM for minors, see [FPM, Part I, 2.C.3.f](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153950/)
* disposition of deceased beneficiaries’ funds, see [FPM, Part II, 1.C.3](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153966/), and
* court-appointed fiduciaries whom VA appointed before August 13, 2018, see [FPM, Part I, 2.C.4.a](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153950/).

---

**II.1.D.2.b . Criteria for Deactivating the Beneficiary Record of an Adult With a VA-Appointed Fiduciary**

Beneficiary records for cases in which there is a VA-appointed fiduciary for an adult should be deactivated when *any* of the following situations occur:

* the beneficiary has been rated competent and has acknowledged receipt of VA FUM
* payment is terminated because of ineligibility, there is no VA FUM, and termination of benefits appears to be permanent
* the beneficiary dies and there is no estate derived from VA benefits in the hands of the fiduciary, or
* the beneficiary dies and the criteria outlined in [FPM, Part II, 1.D.2.d](#2d) have been met.

***References***:  For more information on

* when a fiduciary must account, see [FPM, Part I, 3.A.2](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153954/)
* final accounting

  + actions, see [FPM, Part I, 3.B.3.d](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153955/), and
  + approval, see [FPM, Part I, 3.D.1](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153959/)
* disposition of deceased beneficiaries’ funds, see [FPM, Part II, 1.C.3](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153966/), and
* actions to take for restored competency, see [FPM, Part II, 1.D.2.g](#2g).

---

**II.1.D.2.c . Criteria for Deactivating the Beneficiary Record of a Beneficiary Living in a Contracted Facility**

Beneficiary records for cases involving a beneficiary living in a contracted facility should be deactivated when *all* of the following conditions are met:

* there is no VA FUM
* payment has been discontinued because of ineligibility and discontinuation of payment appears to be permanent, and
* the beneficiary is unlikely to be discharged from the facility.

---

**II.1.D.2.d . Criteria for Deactivating the Beneficiary Record of a Deceased Beneficiary**

Beneficiary records must be deactivated when a beneficiary has died.

The record of a deceased beneficiary must be deactivated when the beneficiary

* leaves a will or there are known heirs, and the

  + final accounting has been approved, if required, and
  + heirs or administrator/executor have been notified of any remaining VA FUM, or
* dies without a will and without known heirs, after completion of the escheat actions outlined in [FPM, Part II, 1.C.3.e](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153966/).

***Important***:  Before deactivating the beneficiary profile,

* take any required actions to complete all pending EPs and administrative tasks within hub jurisdiction, and
* notify the fiduciary of what to do with any remaining FUM as described in [FPM, Part I, 3.B.3.a and b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153955/).

***References***:  For more information on

* when a fiduciary must account, see [FPM, Part I, 3.A.2](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153954/)
* final accounting

  + actions, see [FPM, Part I, 3.B.3.d](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153955/), and
  + approval, see [FPM, Part I, 3.D.1](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153959/), and
* disposition of deceased beneficiaries’ funds, see [FPM, Part II, 1.C.3](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153966/).

---

**II.1.D.2.e . Criteria for Deactivating the Beneficiary Record of a Spouse Fiduciary**

Beneficiary records for cases in which there is a spouse fiduciary must be deactivated when the hub completes all final actions necessary to deactivate the beneficiary record, including processing of the beneficiary death in VA data systems and notification to the spouse payee of discontinuance of VA supervision.

---

**II.1.D.2.f . Criteria for Deactivating the Beneficiary Record of a Temporary Fiduciary**

Beneficiary records for cases in which there is a temporary fiduciary must be deactivated at the end of the temporary, 120-day period, per [FPM, Part I, 5.B.1.b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000223124/).

Prior to deactivating the beneficiary records, for a beneficiary that no longer requires supervision in the handling of VA FUM, fiduciary personnel must

* obtain and audit a final accounting from the temporary fiduciary
* ensure that any funds remaining in the hands of the temporary fiduciary have been released to the beneficiary, and
* notify the regional office of jurisdiction that the temporary period of supervision has expired.

***Reference***:  For more information on a temporary fiduciary, see

* [FPM, Part I, 5.B.1](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000223124/), and
* [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 6.C.1.f](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177976/).

---

**II.1.D.2.g . Criteria for Deactivating the Beneficiary Record of a Beneficiary Whose Competency Has Been Restored**

A case that involves a court-appointed fiduciary for an adult payee must be deactivated at the earliest practical time after the hub has been notified that the beneficiary has been rated competent.  The hub must refer cases to the District Counsel for necessary action if restoration of competency is in order and VA

* *is* providing legal services, or
* is *not* providing legal services, but

  + the fiduciary does not take action to terminate the court appointment in due course, or
  + there is a question of proper FUM.

If the beneficiary is rated competent by VA, the case must be deactivated when direct payment has been certified and the District Counsel has been requested to take restoration action, regardless of whether the court restores the beneficiary to competency.  The case does not remain active while the beneficiary works with the courts for restoration of competency.

***Note***:  Contact information for the Offices of Chief Counsel in the Districts may be found at the [Offices of Chief Counsel in the Districts SharePoint website](https://dvagov.sharepoint.com/sites/OGC-Client/SitePages/District_Offices.aspx?xsdata=MDV8MDJ8fGM3ZjU3ZGU5MGE2MTRmOGQxODNlMDhkZDA0ZDRkNzk1fGU5NWYxYjIzYWJhZjQ1ZWU4MjFkYjdhYjI1MWFiM2JmfDB8MHw2Mzg2NzIwMzQzNzI3NDIzNzh8VW5rbm93bnxUV0ZwYkdac2IzZDhleUpGYlhCMGVVMWhjR2tpT25SeWRXVXNJbFlpT2lJd0xqQXVNREF3TUNJc0lsQWlPaUpYYVc0ek1pSXNJa0ZPSWpvaVRXRnBiQ0lzSWxkVUlqb3lmUT09fDB8fHw%3d&sdata=aXd3Q08zbHVSbFVJb25IdGhjR2gwdDdzQXJndjZNcGM1VjVzbHBkaklZbz0%3d&clickparams=eyAiWC1BcHBOYW1lIiA6ICJNaWNyb3NvZnQgT3V0bG9vayIsICJYLUFwcFZlcnNpb24iIDogIjE2LjAuMTczMjguMjA2MTIiLCAiT1MiIDogIldpbmRvd3MiIH0%3D).

**II.1.D.2.h . Criteria for Deactivating the Beneficiary Record of a Beneficiary That Is No Longer Eligible for VA Benefits**

Hub personnel must deactivate the beneficiary record as soon as is practical when a beneficiary is no longer eligible for VA benefits, and that ineligibility is likely to be permanent.

If restoration of competency is not in order, the

* case must be deactivated upon receipt of the first satisfactory account showing no VA FUM, and
* fiduciary, or private counsel, as appropriate, and the court must be notified of VA’s withdrawal and the reason.

***Notes***:

* These deactivating criteria also apply to cases in which a court-appointed fiduciary is still required for non-VA purposes, such as in cases involving a beneficiary with a large non-VA estate.
* If it is subsequently determined that an incompetent beneficiary is again eligible for VA benefits, the hub will reactivate the beneficiary record and complete an IA field examination to appoint a fiduciary.

## 3.  Deactivating Beneficiary Records for SDP Cases

---

**Change Date**

March 12, 2021

---

**II.1.D.3.a . Requirements for Deactivating an SDP Case**

The beneficiary record, for cases that involve a supervised direct payment (SDP) beneficiary, must be deactivated when *any* of the following situations occur:

* the beneficiary is

  + rated competent by VA, or
  + determined to be competent by judicial determination for a non-Veteran adult, and
  + capable of handling VA benefits payable without supervision
* the beneficiary has no VA estate and is no longer eligible for VA benefits, or
* the beneficiary has died.

***References***:  For more information on

* authorizing an award for a beneficiary found competent by court decree, see [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 6.D.2.j](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177977/)
* considering the competency of other beneficiaries, see [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 6.A.2.d](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177974/), and
* determining restored competency, see [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 6.A.4.b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177974/).

**II.1.D.4.a . Merging Fiduciary Profiles**

To ensure proper oversight, hubs must routinely merge duplicate fiduciary profiles to accurately reflect the number of beneficiaries a fiduciary serves, and amount of funds managed by the fiduciary.

When hub personnel determine that two or more fiduciary profiles exist for the same individual or entity, duplicate profiles must be merged.

***Note***:  Do not manually deactivate fiduciary profiles when profiles can be merged.  When merging, secondary fiduciary profiles will be deactivated by the system.

***References***:  For more information on

* merging fiduciary profiles

  + in VBMS, see the *VBMS Core User Guide*, and
  + to identify required onsite reviews, see [FPM, Part II, 4.A.1.g](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153976/), and

---

**II.1.D.4.b . Deactivating Fiduciary Profiles**

Hub personnel should use caution and take steps to prevent erroneous establishment of fiduciary profiles.  When a fiduciary profile is created in error, the profile must be manually deactivated.  Manual deactivation of fiduciary profiles should only be completed by hub management when a single fiduciary profile exists due to erroneous creation by a hub employee.  Hub personnel must email the supervisor to request deactivation.  Prior to deactivating the profile, hub personnel must ensure there are no open work activities such as EPs or tasks.

***Reference***:  For more information on deactivating fiduciary profiles, see the *VBMS Core User Guide*.

| it is necessary to remove the flag as the bar to service no longer exists (for example, the criminal background findings are mitigated, or the flag was checked erroneously) | * removing the flag, and * documenting the justification, to include identification of any evidence in the eFolder, for removing the flag in the NOTES section of the fiduciary profile before closing the EP.   ***Example***:  The fiduciary profile note may state the following:  *The fiduciary hub received evidence on 3/29/2024 that the fiduciary criminal charges were for another person with the same name and the bar to service does not exist for this fiduciary.* |
| a beneficiary will remain associated to a fiduciary with an active DO NOT APPOINT flag (for example, the beneficiary is deceased or rated competent, a successor IA is not necessary, and the beneficiary profile will remain open pending the resolution of misuse activities or a transfer of funds) | documenting the justification for not removing the beneficiary association to the fiduciary in the NOTES section of the fiduciary profile.    ***Example***:  The fiduciary profile note may state the following:  *A misuse case remains open for deceased beneficiary (**beneficiary first name, beneficiary last name**).  Upon resolution of the misuse matter, the deceased beneficiary record will be closed, and the fiduciary association deactivated*.    ***Important***:  The hub must ensure that the beneficiary is no longer associated with the fiduciary with a DO NOT APPOINT flag when all pending issues for the beneficiary associated with the fiduciary are resolved. |

***Important***:

* Hub employees must review the fiduciary profile at every stage of the workflow and are responsible for ensuring that the DO NOT APPOINT flag and associated notes reflect the fiduciary’s status accurately before closing the work item.
* The DO NOT APPOINT flag must only be indicated when there is evidence that a fiduciary is barred from service consistent with [FPM, Part I, 2.C.5.b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153950/).
