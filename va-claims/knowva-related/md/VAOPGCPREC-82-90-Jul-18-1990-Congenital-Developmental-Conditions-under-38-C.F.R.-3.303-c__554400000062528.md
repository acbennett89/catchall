 VAOPGCPREC 82-90, Jul 18, 1990, Congenital/Developmental Conditions under 38 C.F.R. 3.303(c)
 Office of General Counsel Precedent Opinion

**What the opinion is about:**

In view of the provisions of 38 C.F.R. § 3.303(c), under what circumstances, if any, may service-connection be granted for disorders of congenital or developmental origin?

 (This, opinion, previously issued as General Counsel Opinion 1-85, dated March 5, 1985, is reissued as a Precedent Opinion pursuant to 38 C.F.R. § 2.6(e)(9) and 14.507.)

**Impact on VBA:**

Manual M21-1, IV.ii.2.B provides guidance consistent with the Court's decision.

**Summary of the facts and findings:**

Service-connection may be granted for diseases (but not defects) of congenital, developmental or familial origin. In the instant cases, service connection is warranted if the evidence as a whole establishes that the familial conditions in question were incurred or aggravated during service within the meaning of VA law and regulations.

 See OGC opinion for facts.
