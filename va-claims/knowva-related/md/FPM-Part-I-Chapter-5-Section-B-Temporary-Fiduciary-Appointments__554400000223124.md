## 1.  Temporary Fiduciary

**Change Date**

**I.5.B.1.a . Temporary Fiduciaries as Limited Emergency Fiduciaries**

[38 U.S.C. 5507(d)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/5507) permits Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) to appoint a temporary fiduciary in limited and emergent situations.

The appointment of a temporary fiduciary is reserved for situations where VA determines that the beneficiary has an immediate need for fiduciary services and VA

* has removed a fiduciary and cannot expedite the appointment of a successor fiduciary, or
* determines that it would not be in the beneficiary’s or the beneficiary’s dependents’ interest to pay benefits to the beneficiary until a fiduciary is appointed.

***Reference***:  For more information on removal of a fiduciary see [38 CFR 13.500](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=0f2c1ea018e187d6c8b55ed61182cac3&mc=true&node=se38.1.13_1500&rgn=div8).

---

**I.5.B.1.b . Limitations and Responsibilities Applicable to Temporary Fiduciary Appointments**

The following limitations and responsibilities apply to all temporary fiduciary appointments.  Temporary fiduciaries

* may serve no longer than 120 days
* must serve without a fiduciary fee
* may receive only the monthly VA benefits needed for the care of the beneficiary and their recognized dependents, with any retroactive funds withheld, and
* must provide an accounting of funds received during the period of the temporary fiduciary appointment.

Certification of one or more temporary fiduciaries may not exceed a total of 120 days under any circumstance.

Prior to expiration of the 120-day period

* a fiduciary must be selected based on a VA rating of inability to manage funds or a court-appointed guardianship, or
* the temporary fiduciary arrangement is terminated, and the beneficiary is paid directly.

***Reference***:  For information on fiduciary workflow stage indicators and administrative tasks, see [M21-4, Appendix F](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000181738/).

**I.5.B.1.c . Designating a Temporary Fiduciary**

Appointment of a temporary fiduciary allows VA to immediately deliver benefits to the beneficiary in temporary and emergent situations.  To expeditiously appoint a temporary fiduciary, the Field Examiner (FE) may waive the face-to-face interviews, criminal background inquiry (CBI), and credit check for a temporary fiduciary when the temporary fiduciary

* has already met the qualification criteria for fiduciary appointment
* is performing satisfactorily as a fiduciary for at least one other VA beneficiary, and
* has submitted an annual accounting for a beneficiary which VA has approved.

VA may appoint a temporary fiduciary without consideration of the beneficiary’s preference.

The FE must indicate in the field examination the temporary appointment and reason for such.  This information will serve as justification for the waiver of the face-to-face interviews, CBI, and credit check in qualifying the temporary fiduciary.

***References***:  For more information on

* criteria for deactivating the beneficiary record of a temporary fiduciary, see [FPM, Part II, 1.D.2.f](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153967/)
* certifying a temporary fiduciary, see [FPM, Part I, 1.B.3.d](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000153946/), and
