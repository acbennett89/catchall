Decision Assessment Document
 VDA De Landicho and Oseo v. Brown, Sep 23, 1994, 7 Vet.App. 42 (1994)
 U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims (Court)

**What the case is about:**

The Court held that it is without jurisdiction to review an appeal where the initial appellant was a veteran who dies while the Board of Veterans' Appeals decision was on appeal to the Court.  When a veteran dies, his or her claim does not survive, and the appeal must be dismissed for lack of jurisdiction.

The Court also held the claimants had filed sufficient indication of their intention to claimed accrued benefits based on the veterans' underlying disability compensation claims to have achieved the status of informal claimants.

**Impact on VBA:**

No new impact warranting regulatory revision or Manual change.  The regulations concerning informal claims were replaced by the intent to file and request for application provisions under 38 C.F.R. 3.155 effective March 24, 2015.
