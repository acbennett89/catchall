 VAOPGCPREC 60-90, Jul 18, 1990, Entitlement to SMC pursuant to 38 USC 314, SAH pursuant to 38 USC 801, & adaptive equipment pursuant to 38 USC 1901
 Office of General Counsel Precedent Opinion

**What the opinion is about:**

Entitlement to Special Monthly Compensation Pursuant to 38 U.S.C. § 314 [now 1114]; Specially Adapted Housing Pursuant to 38 U.S.C. § 801 [now 2101]; and Automobiles and Adaptive Equipment Pursuant to 38 U.S.C. § 1901 [now 3902].

1. Does a functional (as distinguished from organic) loss of use of lower extremities meet the statutory requirement of loss of use?

2. Does such functional loss of use satisfy the requisite standard of permanence?

Note: This opinion, previously issued as Administrator's Decision 994, dated June 10, 1974, is reissued as a Precedent Opinion pursuant to 38 C.F.R. §§ 2.6(e)(9) and 14.507. The text of the opinion remains unchanged from the original except for certain format and clerical changes necessitated by the aforementioned regulatory provisions.

**Impact on VBA:**

M21-1, IX.i.2 was revised in accordance with General Counsel opinion.

**Summary of the facts and findings:**

See OGC opinion for facts.

In view of the foregoing, it is, therefore, held:

1. When the requisite determination of "loss of use" is made with regard to a particular VA benefit, it controls respecting eligibility irrespective of whether such loss is functional or organic in origin.

2. The submitted assumption "that the prognosis for reversal is virtually nil from a medical standpoint" meets the requirements of permanence applicable to entitlement to the benefits in question.
