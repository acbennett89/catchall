 VAOPGCPREC 73-90, Jul 18, 1990, Scope of 38 U.S.C. 360 [now 1160] in Relation to 38 U.S.C. 351 [now 1151]
 Office of General Counsel Precedent Opinion

**What the opinion is about:**

Whether the provisions of 38 U.S.C. 360 are applicable where a veteran's loss of use of an extremity is compensable under 38 U.S.C. 351 and loss of use of the other paired extremity is neither service-connected nor quasi-service-connected under section 351.

(This opinion, previously issued as General Counsel Opinion 11-87, dated October 2, 1987, is reissued as a Precedent Opinion pursuant to 38 C.F.R. 2.6(e)(9) and 14.507.)

**Impact on VBA:**

Manual M21-1, IV.ii.2.G was revised in compliance with the precedent opinion.

**Summary of the facts and findings:**

General Counsel held that section 351 eligibility confers entitlement to disability compensation provided under section 360, if eligibility otherwise exists.

See OGC opinion for facts.
