**C.1.a . Purpose of Claim Labels**

Claim labels provide a more specific description of the claim type that a corresponding end product (EP) represents.  The most accurate claim label must be selected when establishing a claim and reviewed for any potential updates throughout the life of the claim.

***Example***:  Use the claim label *Drill Pay Adjustment* for an EP 600 established for due process procedures related to a drill pay adjustment rather than the more general claim label *Predetermination Notice*.

***References***:  For more information on

* establishing a claim, see [M21-1, Part II, Subpart iii, 3.A](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000174881/)
* proper use of EPs, see [M21-4 Appendix B](https://vaww.vrm.km.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_kanew/help/agent/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001034/content/554400000011474/Appendix-B.-End-Product-(EP)-Codes), or
* routine review of an electronic claims folder, see [M21-1, Part III, Subpart i.2. F.1](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000046277/).
