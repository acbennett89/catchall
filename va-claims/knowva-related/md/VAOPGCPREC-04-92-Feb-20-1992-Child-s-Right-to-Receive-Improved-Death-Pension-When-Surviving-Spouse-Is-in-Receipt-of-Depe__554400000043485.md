Decision Assessment Document
 VAOPGCPREC 04-92, Feb 20, 1992, Child's Right to Receive Improved Death Pension When Surviving Spouse Is in Receipt of Dependency and Indemnity Compensation
 Office of General Counsel Precedent Opinion

**What the case is about**

Payment of improved death pension benefits to a child not in the custody of the surviving spouse

**Impact on VBA**

If a child of a deceased veteran under age 18 and not in the custody of the veteran's surviving spouse has been awarded an apportionment of DIC otherwise payable to the surviving spouse, 38 USC 1317, which deals with the exclusivity of the DIC remedy, renders the child ineligible for payment of improved death pension by reasons of the deceased veteran's death.

Neither current statutes nor regulations governing concurrent benefits and elections prohibit an award of improved death pension authorized by 38 USC 1542 to a deceased veteran's child under the age of 18 who is not in the custody of the deceased veteran's surviving spouse and who is not eligible for an apportionment of the DIC payable to the surviving spouse.  If such a child were found to be entitled to pension, there would be no legal authority to withhold the additional amount of DIC payable to the surviving spouse on account of such child.

**Summary on facts and findings**

The surviving spouse is in receipt of DIC at the rate applicable to a surviving spouse with a child.  The child, not in the custody of the surviving spouse, has been awarded an apportionment of the DIC in the amount of $91.00 per month.  The child, who has no income, would be entitled to $101.00 per month if allowed to elect improved pension benefits.  When there is a surviving spouse who is entitled to DIC, the children of a deceased veteran who are under age 18, including those who are in the custody of the surviving spouse, have no independent entitlement to DIC.  Additional amounts are payable for children, regardless of whether the children are in the custody of the surviving spouse.  Payment of death pension benefits to the surviving spouses of deceased wartime veterans with additional amounts payable if children are in the custody of the surviving spouse is authorized by 38 USC 1541.  Payment of death pension benefits to children not in the custody of the surviving spouse is authorized under 38 USC 1542.  No statutory or regulatory provision specifically prohibits simultaneous payment of DIC to a surviving spouse and death pension to an out-of-custody child who is under age 18 based on deaths occurring after December 31, 1956.

Revise M21-1, Part IV, Chapter 20 to include elections between DIC and improved death pension benefits by a child under the age of 18 who is not in the custody of the surviving spouse.
