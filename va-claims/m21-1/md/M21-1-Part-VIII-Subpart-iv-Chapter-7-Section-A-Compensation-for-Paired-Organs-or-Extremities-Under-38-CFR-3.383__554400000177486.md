**VIII.iv.7.A.1.a . Basis for Awarding Compensation for Certain Paired Organs or Extremities Under 38 U.S.C. 1160**

Compensation is payable under [38 U.S.C. 1160](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1160) for disabilities involving certain paired organs or extremities, one service-connected (SC) and the other non-service-connected (NSC), provided the NSC disability is not the result of the Veteran’s own willful misconduct.

***References***:  For more information on

* special consideration for certain paired organs and extremities, see [38 CFR 3.383](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=8433f0ae9e102e2683073e102ec5444d&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1383&rgn=div8), and
* prohibitions against duplication of benefits, see [M21-1, Part VIII, Subpart iv, 7.B.1](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177487/).

---

**VIII.iv.7.A.1.b . Criteria for Establishing Entitlement**

Consider compensation under [38 U.S.C. 1160](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1160) for the following:

* impairment of vision  in one eye as a result of SC disability and impairment of vision in the other eye as a result of NSC disability

  + rated at a visual acuity of 20/200 or less in each eye, or
  + with a peripheral field of vision for each eye of 20 degrees or less.
* loss, or loss of use (L/LOU), of one kidney as a result of SC disability and involvement of the other kidney as a result of NSC disability
* deafness compensable to a degree of 10 percent or more in one ear due to SC disability, and deafness that meets the provisions of [38 CFR 3.385](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/retrieveECFR?gp=&SID=6f4d77d4ff6379afbb035f4d06714437&mc=true&r=SECTION&n=se38.1.3_1385) in the other ear due to NSC disability
* L/LOU of one hand or one foot as a result of SC disability and L/LOU of the other hand or foot as a result of NSC disability, and
* permanent SC disability of one lung rated 50 percent or more disabling, in combination with NSC disability of the other lung.

***Note***:  Compensation may be paid under [38 U.S.C. 1160](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1160) if compensation is being paid for one of the aforementioned conditions under [38 U.S.C. 1151](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1151) and there is manifestation of the specific NSC disability under [38 U.S.C. 1160](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1160).

**VIII.iv.7.A.1.d . Eligibility for Other Benefits Based on Paired Organs or Extremities**

Entitlement to disability compensation for a Veteran based on paired organs or extremities under [38 U.S.C. 1160](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1160) creates eligibility for the following benefits

* Dependency and Indemnity Compensation, and
* Dependents’ Educational Assistance (DEA) under [38 U.S.C. Chapter 35](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/part-III/chapter-35).

***Note***:  Compensation entitlement under [38 U.S.C. 1160](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1160) does not create entitlement to

* special allowance to a surviving dependent under [38 U.S.C. 1312](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1312)
* specially adapted housing (SAH) under [38 U.S.C. 2101(a)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/2101), or
* special home adaptation grant (SHA) under [38 U.S.C. 2101(b)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/2101).

***References***:  For more information on

* special allowance under [38 U.S.C. 1312](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1312), see [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.A](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000175223/)
* SAH and SHA entitlement, see [M21-1, Part XIII, Subpart i, 2.B](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000173639/), and
* DEA entitlement based on entitlement under [38 U.S.C 1160](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1160), see [*Kimberlin v. Brown*](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000070028/)*,* 5 Vet.App. 314 (1993).
