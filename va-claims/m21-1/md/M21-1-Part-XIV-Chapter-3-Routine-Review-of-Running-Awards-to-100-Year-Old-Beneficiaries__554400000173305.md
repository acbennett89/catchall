## **1.  General Information on the Routine Review of Running Awards to 100-Year-Old Beneficiaries**

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains general information on the routine review of running awards to 100-year-old beneficiaries, including

* [purposes of the review](#1a), and
* [identification of 100-year-old beneficiaries](#1b).

---

**Change Date**

April 6, 2020

---

**XIV.3.1.a . Purposes of the Review**

The purposes of the review discussed in this chapter are to verify

* the accuracy of dates of birth (DOBs) in the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) corporate record
* beneficiaries are alive, and
* Social Security income is being properly counted on the VA award in income-dependent cases.

***Note***:  Questions about the review may be e-mailed to [VAVBAWAS/CO/212A](mailto:212A.VBACO@va.gov).

---

**XIV.3.1.b . Identification of 100-Year-Old Beneficiaries**

The Hines Information Technology Center (ITC) generates a work item with message code 704 (*Over Age 100 Review*) when a beneficiary reaches age 100 based on the payee DOB in the corporate record.

***Note***:  The individuals the system identifies may not actually be 100 years old.  This occurs when a fictitious or erroneous DOB exists in the corporate record.  If the DOB is erroneous, update the erroneous data elements using the appropriate VA systems.

***Reference***:  For more information on evidence required to change or correct existing vital information about a beneficiary, see [M21-1, Part III, Subpart iii, 8](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000028396/).

## **2.  Processing the Work Item Generated to Initiate the Review of a Running Award to a 100-Year-Old Beneficiary**

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information on processing the work item generated to initiate the review of a running award to a 100-year-old beneficiary, including

* [first step in processing the work item](#2a)
* actions to take when the attempt to access data in Social Security Administration (SSA) records is

  + [successful](#2b), and
  + [unsuccessful](#2c), and
* [handling cases of fraud](#2d).

---

**Change Date**

April 6, 2020

---

**XIV.3.2.a . First Step in Processing the Work Item**

Following receipt of the work item referenced in [M21-1, Part XIV, 3.1.b](#1b), attempt to make the verifications described in [M21-1, Part XIV, 3.1.a](#1a), using data in Social Security Administration (SSA) records, which users may access through the Federal Online Query System (FOLQ) in Share.

***Note***:  If message code 704 does not appear in Share, upload the documents received as notification of the work item (such as an e-mail or spreadsheet) to the Veteran’s electronic claims folder.  Upload only the information specifically related to the beneficiary under review.

***References***:  For more information on the actions to take when the attempt to access data in SSA records through FOLQ is

* successful, see [M21-1, Part XIV, 3.2.b](#2b), and
* unsuccessful, see [M21-1, Part XIV, 3.2.c](#2c).

---

**XIV.3.2.b . Actions to Take When the Attempt to Access Data in SSA Records Is Successful**

Use the table below to determine the actions to take on the work item referenced in [M21-1, Part XIV, 3.1.b](#1b), when the attempt to access data in SSA records through FOLQ is successful.

| **If SSA records show ...** | **Then ...** |
| --- | --- |
| updated vital information, such as DOB, Social Security number (SSN), and so forth | * update any erroneous data element(s) in the appropriate VA systems, and * clear end product (EP) 130, *100-Year Review*.   ***Reference***:  For more information on evidence required to change or correct existing vital information about a beneficiary, see [M21-1, Part III, Subpart iii, 8](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000028396/). |
| * the beneficiary is not alive, and * the award is not in suspense due to the Death Master File (DMF) match | * discontinue the award under EP 130, *100-Year Review*, based on the date of death shown in SSA records, and * furnish contemporaneous notice of the action taken. |
| * the beneficiary is not alive, and * the award is in suspense due to the DMF match | follow the procedures in M21-1, Part XIV, 2.B. |
| the beneficiary receives Social Security benefits | verify that any Social Security benefits are being properly counted on any award of income-dependent VA benefits.   * If the income is being properly counted,    + clear EP 150, and   + take no further action. * If the income is not properly counted, and a corresponding adjustment to the beneficiary’s award    + would ***not*** result in a reduction of benefits, make the award adjustment under EP 150, or   + would result in a reduction of benefits,      - clear EP 150     - establish EP 600, and     - furnish a notice of proposed adverse action for an adjustment based on the rate and effective date of the Social Security benefits.   ***Notes***:   * If additional information is needed before proposing an adjustment, undertake development under EP 150. * Regional offices are entitled to the EP 130 referenced in the first row of this table ***or*** the EP 150 referenced in this row.  Under no circumstances is it appropriate to take both EPs for the same case.   ***Reference***:  For more information on providing a notice of proposed adverse action, see [M21-1, Part I, 2.B](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000014072/). |

***Reference***:  For more information on EPs, see [M21-4, Appendix B](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000011474/).

**XIV.3.2.c . Actions to Take When the Attempt to Access Data in SSA Records Is Unsuccessful**

Use the table below to determine the actions to take on the work item referenced in [M21-1, Part XIV, 3.1.b](#1b), when the attempt to access data in SSA records through FOLQ is unsuccessful.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Step** | **Action** |
| 1 | Does a review of the claims folder reveal a name, SSN, or DOB that differs from the name, SSN, or DOB in the corporate record?   * If *yes*,    + make another attempt to access data in the beneficiary’s SSA records through FOLQ, using the name, SSN, and/or DOB identified in the claims folder, and   + go to the next step. * If *no*, go to Step 3. |
| 2 | Was the attempt to access data in the beneficiary’s SSA records through FOLQ successful?   * If *yes*,    + disregard the remaining steps in this table, and   + follow the instructions in [M21-1, Part XIV, 3.2.b](#2b). * If *no*, go to the next step. |
| 3 | Establish EP 130, *100-Year Review*.    ***Important***:  Add the *Potential Under/Overpayment* special issue if the beneficiary is receiving disability compensation.    ***Reference***:  For more information about the purpose of the *Potential Under/Overpayment* special issue, see [M21-1, Part III, Subpart ii, 1.C.7.a](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000014112/). |
| 4 | * Attempt to contact the beneficiary by telephone, requesting confirmation of his/her    + name   + SSN, and   + DOB. * Advise the beneficiary that    + this information is required, and   + failure to provide the information will result in a termination of payments.   ***Note***:  Follow the instructions in [M21-1, Part III, Subpart iii, 1.B.1.e](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000014152/), when obtaining information from a beneficiary by telephone. |
| 5 | Was information required to successfully access data in the beneficiary’s SSA records through FOLQ obtained from the beneficiary?   * If *yes*,    + disregard the remaining step in this table, and   + follow the instructions in [M21-1, Part XIV, 3.2.b](#2b). * If *no*, write to the beneficiary,    + explaining that the information provided did not match SSA records   + asking him/her to furnish a statement from SSA showing his/her      - SSN, and     - DOB, and   + allow 60 days for a response. |
| 6 | If, after 60 days from the date of the written request, the beneficiary fails to provide the information required to successfully access his/her SSA records through FOLQ,   * clear the EP 130 * establish EP 600, and * send a notice of proposed adverse action proposing to terminate benefits from the date of inception under the provisions of [38 CFR 3.652(a)(1)](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=16d4b04a8b8ba1eda23c0c18d9f210a8&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1652&rgn=div8). |

---

**XIV.3.2.d . Handling Cases of Fraud**

If there is an indication that someone is or was fraudulently receiving the benefits of a deceased beneficiary, follow the procedures in [M21-1, Part III, Subpart vi, 5.A](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000014296/).
