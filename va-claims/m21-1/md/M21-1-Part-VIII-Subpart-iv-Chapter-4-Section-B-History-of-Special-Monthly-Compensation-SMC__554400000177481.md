## 1.  General Information on the History of SMC

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains general information on the history of SMC, including

* [purpose of this section](#1a)
* [content of this section](#1b)
* [Compensation Service assistance in resolving difficult SMC cases](#1c), and
* [an overview of the history of SMC changes from July 1, 1933, *Public Law (PL) 73-2*, to the present](#1d).

---

**Change Date**

May 12, 2015

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.1.a . Purpose of This Section**

This section has been designed for use by the rating activity when reviewing prior rating decisions for accuracy in the awarding of special monthly compensation (SMC).

Prior rating decisions addressing the issue of entitlement to SMC may appear to be erroneous based upon current criteria, when, in fact, they were totally correct based upon the criteria in effect at the time of the prior rating decision.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.1.b . Content of This Section**

This section

* contains only an overview of the history of SMC, not all of the historical aspects involving the payment of SMC
* provides a breakdown for each of the levels of SMC, and
* does not address the limited number of cases in which the criteria for SMC that were in effect before July 1, 1933, would be at issue.

***Note***:  Where appropriate, cross-references have been made to other levels of SMC where there are, or were, restrictions in the simultaneous payment of SMC at one or more levels.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.1.c . Compensation Service Assistance in Resolving Difficult SMC Cases**

If there is any question as to which level of SMC is appropriate in a given case, submit the claims folder to Compensation Service for an advisory opinion.

***Reference***:  For more information on requesting advisory opinions, see M21-1, Part X, Subpart v, 1.A.

**Change Date**

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.2.a . SMC Under PL 73-2**

*PL 73-2*, effective July 1, 1933, provided for payment of SMC for the anatomical loss or loss of use (L/LOU) of only

* one foot
* one hand, or
* one eye.

***Notes***:  SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) was

* payable in addition to the basic rate of compensation payable for disability ratings of 10 percent through 100 percent
* payable for only one disability meeting the criteria under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), even though multiple disabilities meeting the criteria may have existed, and
* ***not*** payable in addition to SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(l)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) through [38 U.S.C. 1114(n)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

***Example***:  A Veteran with loss of one foot, plus the loss of one eye, would have been entitled to compensation based on the disability evaluation assigned, plus SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) for *either* loss of the foot *or* loss of the eye, but not both.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.2.b . SMC Under PL 79-182**

*PL 79-182*, effective October 1, 1945

* clarified the standard for blindness of one eye as having light perception only (LPO), and
* provided for the payment of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), in addition to SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(l) through (n)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), for each L/LOU under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

***Note***:  The prohibition against the payment of SMC for more than one disability meeting the criteria under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) was continued.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.2.c . SMC Under PL 82-427**

*PL 82-427*, effective August 1, 1952, provided for the payment of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) for anatomical L/LOU of a creative organ.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.2.d . SMC Under PL 84-969**

*PL 84-969*, effective October 1, 1956, provided for the payment of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) for anatomical L/LOU of both buttocks.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.2.e . SMC Under PL 86-663**

*PL 86-663*, effective September 1, 1960, created a new level of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(s)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).  SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) was not payable in addition to SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(s)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

***Note***:  If the additional 60-percent disability establishing entitlement at the “s” level included entitlement under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), payment was to be made at the 100 percent + “k” level, as this was a greater benefit than the 100 percent + “s” level.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.2.f . SMC Under PL 88-20**

*PL 88-20*, effective July 1, 1963, provided for the payment of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) for deafness in both ears, with absence of air and bone conduction.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.2.g . SMC Under PL 88-22**

*PL 88-22*, effective July 1, 1963, provided for the payment of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) for organic aphonia with constant inability to communicate by speech.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.2.h . SMC Under PL 90-77**

*PL 90-77*, effective October 1, 1967

* provided for the payment of SMC

  + for each disability that met the criteria under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), and
  + under both [38 U.S.C. 1114(s)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) and [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), and
* removed the prohibition against using an additional disability, ratable as 50 percent disabling, to justify awarding an intermediate rate under [38 U.S.C. 1114(p)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), when the same disability had been used as the basis for establishing entitlement to SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

***References***:  For more information on awarding entitlement to SMC

* based on anatomical loss, see *PL 79-182*, and
* for additional disabilities, see *PL 86-663*.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.2.i . SMC Under PL 106-419**

*PL 106-419*, effective November 1, 2000, amended the criteria for payment to include a woman Veteran’s anatomical loss of one or both breasts, including loss by mastectomy.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.2.j . SMC Under PL 107-330**

*PL 107-330*, effective December 6, 2002, clarified that loss of one or both breasts includes

* loss of 25 percent or more of breast tissue from a single breast or both breasts in combination, and
* receipt of radiation treatment of breast tissue.

## 3.  SMC Under 38 U.S.C. 1114(l)

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information on SMC under 38 U.S.C. 1114(l), including information on SMC under

* [*PL 73-2*](#3a)
* [*PL 79-182*](#3b), and
* [*PL 97-66*](#3c).

---

**Change Date**

December 13, 2005

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.3.a . SMC Under PL 73-2**

*PL 73-2*, effective July 1, 1933, provided for the payment of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(l)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) for

* the anatomical L/LOU of

  + both hands or both feet, or
  + one hand and one foot, or
* being so helpless as to be in need of regular aid and attendance (A&A).

***Note***:  Since *PL 73-2* did not provide for the payment of intermediate levels of SMC, the level of L/LOU had no effect on the level of SMC payable.

***Example***:  Amputation of one arm above the elbow and amputation below the elbow of the other arm would establish entitlement to SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(l)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.3.b . SMC Under PL 79-182**

*PL 79-182*, effective October 1, 1945, provided for the payment of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(l)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) if the Veteran was

* blind in both eyes, with visual acuity of 5/200 (1.5/60) or less, or
* permanently bedridden.

***Notes***:

* Concentric contraction of the field of vision beyond 10 degrees was considered equivalent to visual acuity of 5/200 (1.5/60) until July 6, 1950, the effective date of a change to the 1945 rating schedule.  Under the revised rating schedule, concentric contraction of the field of vision beyond five degrees was considered equivalent to visual acuity of 5/200 (1.5/60).
* *PL 79-182* provided for payment of SMC at intermediate levels.

***Reference***:  For more information on payment of SMC at intermediate levels, see

* [38 U.S.C. 1114(p)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), and
* [M21-1, Part VIII, Subpart iv, 4.B.7](#7).

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.3.c . SMC Under PL 97-66**

*PL 97-66*, effective October 1, 1981, removed L/LOU of both hands as a condition warranting entitlement to SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(l)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

***Rationale***:  Effective October 1, 1981, the L/LOU of both hands became one of the criteria for entitlement to SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(m)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

***Reference***:  For more information on [38 U.S.C. 1114(m)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), see [M21-1, Part VIII, Subpart iv, 4.B.4](#4).

---

## 4.  SMC Under 38 U.S.C. 1114(m)

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information on SMC under 38 U.S.C. 1114(m), including information on SMC under

* [*PL 73-2*](#4a)
* [*PL 79-182*](#4b)
* [*PL 85-652*](#4c), and
* [*PL 97-66*](#4d).

---

**Change Date**

December 13, 2005

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.4.a . SMC Under PL 73-2**

*PL 73-2*, effective July 1, 1933, established the criteria for payment of [38 U.S.C. 1114(m)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) as

* the L/LOU of three extremities, or
* blindness in both eyes, having LPO.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.4.b . SMC Under PL 79-182**

*PL 79-182*, effective October 1, 1945, provided for payment of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(m)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) if the evidence showed

* L/LOU of two extremities at a level, or with complications, preventing natural elbow or knee action with prosthesis in place, or
* blindness in both eyes, leaving the Veteran so helpless as to require A&A.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.4.c . SMC Under PL 85-652**

*PL 85-652*, effective September 1, 1958, reinstated blindness in both eyes, having LPO, as a condition warranting entitlement to SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(m)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.4.d . SMC Under PL 97-66**

*PL 97-66*, effective October 1, 1981, elevated the level of SMC for

* L/LOU of both hands from [38 U.S.C. 1114(l)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) to [38 U.S.C. 1114(m)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), and
* L/LOU of both arms at levels, or with complications, preventing natural elbow action with prosthesis in place from [38 U.S.C. 1114(m)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) to [38 U.S.C. 1114(n)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

## 5.  SMC Under 38 U.S.C. 1114(n)

---

**Introduction**

---

**Change Date**

December 13, 2005

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.5.a . SMC Under PL 73-2**

*PL 73-2*, effective July 1, 1933, provided for payment of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(n)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) for blindness in both eyes, having LPO, ***plus*** the L/LOU of one extremity.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.5.b . SMC Under PL 79-182**

*PL 79-182*, effective October 1, 1945, provided for payment of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(n)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) if the evidence showed anatomical loss of

* two extremities so near the shoulder or hip as to prevent the use of a prosthetic appliance, or
* both eyes, or total blindness of both eyes, having no light perception, accompanied by

  + phthisis bulbi
  + evisceration, or
  + other obvious deformity or disfigurement.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.5.c . SMC Under PL 97-66**

*PL 97-66*, effective October 1, 1981, elevated the level of SMC for

* L/LOU of both arms at levels, or with complications, preventing natural elbow action with a prosthesis in place from [38 U.S.C. 1114(m)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) to [38 U.S.C. 1114(n)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), and
* anatomical loss of both arms so near the shoulder as to prevent the use of a prosthetic appliance from [38 U.S.C. 1114(n)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) to [38 U.S.C. 1114(o)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.5.d . SMC Under PL 97-306**

*PL 97-306*, effective October 1, 1982

* added blindness without light perception in both eyes as a condition warranting entitlement to SMC, and
* removed the requirement that blindness must be accompanied by

  + phthisis bulbi
  + evisceration, or
  + other obvious deformity or disfigurement.

***Reference***:  For more information on SMC for anatomical loss or blindness, see *PL 79-182*.

**VIII.iv.4.B.6.a . SMC Under PL 73-2**

*PL 73-2*, effective July 1, 1933, established the criterion for payment of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(o)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) for two or more conditions warranting entitlement to SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(l) through (n)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

***Important***:  No condition could be considered twice.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.6.b . SMC Under PL 79-182**

*PL 79-182*, effective October 1, 1945, provided for payment of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(o)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) for

* bilateral blindness, with visual acuity of 5/200 (1.5/60) or less, in combination with bilateral total deafness, and/or
* transverse myelitis, with loss of use of both legs and loss of anal and bladder control.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.6.c . SMC Under PL 85-782**

*PL 85-782*, effective October 1, 1958, created a new level of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(r)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

***References***:  For more information on

* SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(r)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), see [M21-1, Part VIII, Subpart iv, 4.B.9](#9), and
* determining the maximum rate payable under [38 U.S.C. 1114(p)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), see [M21-1, Part VIII, Subpart iv, 4.B.12](#12).

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.6.d . SMC Under PL 89-311**

*PL 89-311*, effective December 1, 1965, amended the criteria under [38 U.S.C. 1114(o)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) (*PL 85-782*) to read:  bilateral deafness, with the hearing impairment in one or both ears service-connected (SC), rated at 60 percent, or more, plus SC total blindness with 5/200 (1.5/60) visual acuity, or less.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.6.e . SMC Under PL 97-66**

*PL 97-66*, effective October 1, 1981, elevated the level of SMC for anatomical loss of both arms so near the shoulder as to prevent the use of prosthetic appliances from [38 U.S.C. 1114(n)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) to [38 U.S.C. 1114(o)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.6.f . SMC Under PL 98-223**

*PL 98-223*, effective October 1, 1983, provided for payment of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(o)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) to include bilateral blindness, having LPO or less, with

* SC total deafness in one ear, or
* bilateral hearing loss, rated 40 percent or more disabling, with SC hearing impairment in at least one ear.

---

**VIII.iv.4.B.6.g . SMC Under PL 110-157**

*PL 110-157*, effective December 26, 2007, amended the visual impairment criteria for entitlement under [38 U.S.C. 1114(o)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) to provide for payment of SMC for

* total SC blindness with 20/200 visual acuity or less, combined with
* bilateral deafness rated at 60 percent or more (and the hearing loss in either ear is SC).

**VIII.iv.4.B.7.a . SMC Under PL 79-182**

*PL 79-182*, effective October 1, 1945, established intermediate steps of SMC for a level of disability that exceeded the requirements for entitlement to any of the levels of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(l) through (n)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

The table below lists the criteria set forth in *PL 79-182* for establishing entitlement to each intermediate step of SMC.

| **Intermediate Step** | **Criteria** |
| --- | --- |

**VIII.iv.4.B.12.a . Determining the Maximum Rate Under 38 U.S.C. 1114(p) for the Purpose of Determining Entitlement to SMC Under 38 U.S.C. 1114(r)**

*PL 85-782*, effective October 1, 1958, created a level of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(r)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).  One of the ways to establish basic entitlement under [38 U.S.C. 1114(r)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) was for the Veteran to be entitled to the maximum rate under [38 U.S.C. 1114(p)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), that is, [38 U.S.C. 1114(o)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

The question arose as to what constituted the maximum rate under [38 U.S.C. 1114(p)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).  For example, if a Veteran was entitled to “n½” under [38 U.S.C. 1114(p)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), this rate was less than the maximum under [38 U.S.C. 1114(o)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).  However, if the Veteran also had independent entitlement to SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), that is, “n½ +k,” the rate payable exceeded the maximum under [38 U.S.C. 1114(p)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), that is, [38 U.S.C. 1114(o)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

***Question***:  The question to be resolved then was whether the SMC payable under [38 U.S.C. 1114(k)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) could be added to the rate payable under [38 U.S.C. 1114(p)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), in this example, “n½,” to establish basic entitlement to [38 U.S.C. 1114(r)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

***Response***:  For a history of the relevant decisions that have addressed the question of what constitutes the maximum rate of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(p)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), see [M21-1, Part VIII, Subpart iv, 4.B.12.b](#12b)-[d](#12d).

**VIII.iv.4.B.12.b . History of Decisions Affecting Determinations of the Maximum Allowable Rate Under 38 U.S.C. 1114(p)**

The table below describes the history of relevant decisions affecting determinations of the maximum rate of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(p)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

| **Decision and Date** | **Opinion** |
| --- | --- |

This meant that Veterans who were entitled to SMC at the level of “n+2k,” “n+3k,” or “n½ +k,” all of which exceeded the rate of SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(o)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), were to be paid SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(r)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114). |
| Reinterpretation of *GC Opinion 9-63* in 1977 | * Determined that *GC Opinion 9-63* did *not* say that all Veterans entitled to the maximum rate under [38 U.S.C. 1114(o)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) were also entitled automatically to SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(r)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), and * held that basic eligibility to the SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(r)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) was established, and if the need for A&A was demonstrated, SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(r)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) could then be paid. |

**VIII.iv.4.B.12.e . Disabilities Warranting Consideration Under 38 U.S.C. 1114(o) or (p), as Well as 38 U.S.C. 1114(r)**

The need for A&A may be based on one of the same disabilities that is used to establish entitlement to SMC under either [38 U.S.C. 1114(o)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114) or to the maximum rate under [38 U.S.C. 1114(p)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114).

***Example***:

***Situation***:  The Veteran is entitled to

* SMC at the “n½” level, based on amputation of the left arm at the shoulder and amputation of the right arm above the elbow, and
* an additional half step of SMC to the "o" level under [38 U.S.C. 1114(p)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), based on the existence of a separate disability rated 50-percent disabling.

***Result***:  If evidence shows the Veteran requires A&A by reason of the bilateral amputations, award entitlement to SMC under [38 U.S.C. 1114(r)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1114), using SMC code 55.

***Note***:  Because Veterans entitled to SMC at these levels are, by definition, very seriously disabled, apply a liberal interpretation of the law in determining the need for A&A.
