## **1.  Award Information for the Special Allowance Under 38 U.S.C. 1312(a)**

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains award information for the special allowance under 38 U.S.C. 1312(a), including

* [general information for a special allowance award under 38 US.C. 1312(a)](#1a), and
* [determining the ending dates for awards involving a child or children](#1b)
* [new awards under 38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](#1c), and
* [entering award data for a running award under 38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](#1d).

---

**Change Date**

July 19, 2015

---

**XII.ii.2.D.1.a . General Information for the Special Allowance Award Under 38 U.S.C. 1312(a)**

Refer to the table below for general award information for the special allowance payable under [38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](http://www4.law.cornell.edu/uscode/38/1312.html).

| **Award Data** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| Payee Number | Use payee number 70 to establish an initial for award under [38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](http://www4.law.cornell.edu/uscode/38/1312.html). Use payee numbers 71-78 for subsequent payees. |
| Veteran’s Social Security Number (SSN) | Ensure that the Veteran’s SSN, as shown in the Beneficiary Identification and Records Location Subsystem (BIRLS), is the same as that shown on   * *SSA Form 101*, *Determination of Award* * *SSA Form 101C*, *Determination of Award*, or * *SSA Form 101U3*, *Determination of Award*.   If the number is incorrect, enter the correct number in BIRLS. |
| Monthy Rate | Pay the amount certified by the Social Security Administration (SSA).  If the amount certified is not a whole-dollar amount, round down to the nearest dollar. |
| Effective Date | The commencement date of an award is always the first day of the month in which the individual is eligible to receive benefits as certified by the SSA.    ***Example***:  If *SSA Form 101*, *SSA Form 101C*, or *SSA Form 101U3* shows *4/02* as the commencing date, use 04/01/2002 as the effective date. |
| Ending Date - Child Involved | Do not provide an ending date on awards to a surviving spouse, parent, or child incapable of self-support (when they are the only child on the award, unless certified by SSA.) |
| Ending Date - Child or Children Involved | Provide an ending date on all awards to, or on behalf of, a child or children other than children that are incapable of self-support. |

---

**XII.ii.2.D.1.b . Determining Ending Dates for Awards Involving a Child or Children**

Use the table below to determine the ending dates for awards involving a child or children.

| **If the award is on behalf of …** | **Then …** |
| --- | --- |
| a child or children | terminate the benefits effective the first day of the month in which the youngest child on the award reaches age 18, per [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.D.7](#7). |
| a child that is incapable of self-support | do not show a termination date if the child is the only child on the award. |

***Notes***:

* A child attains the age of 18 years the day preceding the anniversary date of their birth.  Therefore, a child born on July 1 is considered to have attained the age of 18 years on June 30, and the last date of entitlement for the child is May 31, requiring an ending date of June 1.
* Use an earlier date in those cases in which an earlier termination date is certified by SSA for reasons such as death or marriage.

---

**XII.ii.2.D.1.c . New Awards Under 38 U.S.C. 1312(a)**

All running awards for the special allowance under [38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1312) have been converted from the Benefits Delivery Network to the Veterans Benefits Management System (VBMS).

New awards under [38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1312) are rare.  For additional information on starting a new award under [38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1312) in VBMS, contact Pension & Fiduciary Service by e-mail at [VAVBAWAS/CO/P&F POL & PROC](mailto:PFPOLPROC.VBACO@va.gov).

---

**XII.ii.2.D.1.d . Entering Award Data for a Running Award Under 38 U.S.C. 1312(a)**

Use the table below to enter award data in VBMS for running awards of the special allowance payable under [38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](http://www4.law.cornell.edu/uscode/38/1312.html)

| **Step** | **Action** |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | In the RECORD DECISIONS screen, select BUILD AWARD. |
| 2 | Select ADD or EDIT to amend the award, and enter the following information:   * award effective date * award reason * service period * gross rate, and * number of children. |
| 3 | Select ACCEPT, OK, and then GENERATE AWARD. |

***Reference***:  For more information on VBMS awards, see the *VBMS Awards User Guide*.

## 2.  Sending Notification of the Original 38 U.S.C. 1312(a) Award

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information on sending notification of the original 38 U.S.C. 1312(a) award, including information on

* [notifying claimants of award approval](#2a)
* [information to include in the award letter](#2b)
* [wording to include in the award letter](#2c), and
* [sending a copy of the award to SSA](#2d).

---

**Change Date**

July 19, 2015

---

**XII.ii.2.D.2.a . Notifying Claimants of Award Approval**

Notify the claimant when an original award is approved using a locally-generated letter containing both the claim number and SSN.  Use the copy of the award notice letter furnished by SSA as a pattern.

---

**XII.ii.2.D.2.b . Information to Include in the Award Letter**

In the award letter,

* identify the SSA district office as shown on the *SSA Form 101*, *SSA Form 101C*, or *SSA Form 101U3*, and
* include any enclosures SSA furnished with *SSA Form 101*, *SSA Form 101C*, or *SSA Form 101U3* if it is indicated that these would have been sent to the claimant if they had been in an insured status under old-age and survivor’s insurance.

---

**XII.ii.2.D.2.c . Wording to Include in the Award Letter**

Include the following wording when sending the award letter to the claimant:

*This award is made by the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) as a result of claims development by the Social Security Administration District Office,* **[office name]***, and is in accordance with a determination made by the Social Security Administration Program Service Center,* **[service center]***.  If you have any questions about this award, you should discuss them with the Social Security Administration District Office.  The occurrence of any of the events referred to in the enclosed instructions should be reported promptly to the Social Security Administration and VA.  These enclosures contain important information and instructions which should be carefully followed.*

*Immediate notice should be given of the death or marriage of any beneficiary for whom benefits are being paid under this award.  If either event should occur, any checks received thereafter must not be negotiated.*

*Submit a statement to the below address giving the date of death or marriage (along with the married name and address) and whereabouts of any children under age 18 who may be entitled to payments.*

**Philadelphia Pension Management Center*
 *P.O. Box 8079*
 *Philadelphia, PA 1910**1**

**Return uncashed checks* *to the Department of Treasury at the following address:**

**U.S. Department of Treasury*
 *Fiscal Service*
 *P.O. Box 51316*
 *Philadelphia, PA 19115-6316**

**If* *you received* *payments by Direct Deposit, you may request the* *financial institution return the funds to VA**.**

***VA and the Social Security Administration should also be notified immediately if you change your address.  Show both the claim number and Social Security number shown above when writing to either of these agencies.***

---

**XII.ii.2.D.2.d . Sending a Copy of the Award to SSA**

Send one copy of the award document print and two copies of the award letter being sent to the claimant to the SSA Program Service Center (PSC) from which the *SSA Form 101*, *SSA Form 101C*, or *SSA Form 101U3* was received.

## **3.  Handling Death of the Claimant Before  38 U.S.C. 1312(a) Payment Is Made**

---

**Change Date**

November 16, 2004

---

**XII.ii.2.D.3.a . Handling Death of the Claimant**

Use the table below to determine what action is needed if the claimant dies before or after certification, but before [38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1312) payment is made.

| **If there is …** | **Then …** |
| --- | --- |
| a remaining eligible survivor(s) | SSA furnishes a certification to the survivor(s), adding the accrued amount payable for the deceased claimant to the certification. |
| no remaining eligible survivor | the finance activity transmits inquiries to the General Accounting Office to request a settlement. |

## 4.  Determining Children’s Awards

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information on determining children’s 38 U.S.C. 1312(a) awards, including

* [determining the monthly rates for children](#4a)
* [payment of the child’s award when the surviving spouse’s payments are discontinued](#4b), and
* [payment when the surviving spouse’s payments are resumed](#4c).

---

**Change Date**

February 3, 2011

---

**XII.ii.2.D.4.a . Determining Monthly Rates for Children**

Use the table below to determine the monthly rates for children.

| **If the award of the special allowance involves a child or children in the custody of a …** | **Then …** |
| --- | --- |
| surviving spouse | * combine the monthly rates payable for the child or children with the surviving spouse’s rate and show the award as a total monthly rate payable to the surviving spouse, or * prepare a separate award for each child, if the surviving spouse is not entitled to the benefit. |
| divorced spouse | * prepare separate awards to    + the spouse, if eligible, and   + each eligible child in the spouse’s custody, and * do *not* combine    + the children’s separate entitlements in a consolidated award, or   + the award payable to an eligible divorced spouse based on their entitlement with the award(s) payables for each eligible child in the spouse’s custody. |

---

**XII.ii.2.D.4.b . Payment of the Child’s Award When the Surviving Spouse’s Payments Are Discontinued**

If payments to a surviving spouse are discontinued because of excessive earnings as certified by SSA, authorize payment of the share for each child, as certified by SSA on *SSA Form 101*, *SSA Form 101C*, *SSA Form 101U3*, or *SSA* *Form 656*, *Information as to Continuing Eligibility Under Title 38 U.S.C., Section 412, Veterans Benefits*, to a

* parent under [38 U.S.C. 5502](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/5502), or
* fiduciary.

Unless the SSA certifies otherwise, continue payments until the youngest child reaches age 18.

***References***:  For more information on

* continuing payments until the youngest child reaches age 18, see [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.D.7](#7), and
* authorizing an award under [38 U.S.C. 5502](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/5502) and notifying the custodian, see [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 6.E.4.d](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177978/).

---

**XII.ii.2.D.4.c . Payment When the Surviving Spouse’s Payments Are Resumed**

If the surviving spouse again becomes entitled, make a separate award to the surviving spouse even though the children are in custody.

Notify both the surviving spouse and the parent under [38 U.S.C. 5502](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/5502) or fiduciary for each child that if, at any time, a child is no longer in the custody of the surviving spouse, VA and SSA must be informed immediately.

## **5.  Discontinuing 38 U.S.C. 1312(a) Payments**

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information on discontinuing 38 U.S.C. 1312(a) payments, including information on

* [temporary suspension of payments by VA](#5a)
* [termination of payments by SSA](#5b)
* [action initiated by VA to suspend payments](#5c), and
* [action taken by VA to notify the payee of termination of payments](#5d).

---

**Change Date**

May 3, 2019

---

**IX.ii.1.D.5.a . Temporary Suspension of Payments by VA**

VA may temporarily suspend payments, pending a final determination by SSA, on receipt of any of the following notices:

* death of a beneficiary
* marriage of a child beneficiary
* marriage of a surviving spouse, or
* a surviving spouse under age 60 who does not have in their custody at least one child that is

  + under age 18, or
  + over age 18 and incapable of self-support.

***Note***:  When payments to a surviving spouse are being terminated because a child under age 18 or child incapable of self-support is no longer in custody, include in the letter to the payee notice of potential entitlement at age 60 upon the filing of a new application.

---

**XII.ii.2.D.5.b . Termination of Payments by SSA**

The SSA

* uses *SSA* *Form 656* to request VA to stop payments, and
* includes a letter, or draft of the letter, and attachments which SSA would have sent to the beneficiary had insured status existed.

---

**XII.ii.2.D.5.c . Action Initiated by VA to Suspend Payments**

Follow the steps in the table below to suspend payments on receipt of the notices described in [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.D.5.a](#5a).

| **Step** | **Action** |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Suspend payments and enter the reason for suspension in the REMARKS field. |
| 2 | Send a locally-generated letter to the claimant   * giving the reason for the suspension, and * advising that further information will be furnished when a final determination has been made by SSA. |
| 3 | Send a copy of the award print and the original or a copy of the evidence upon which the action was based to the servicing SSA PSC.    ***Note***:  If the original evidence is sent, make a copy for the deceased Veteran's claims folder. |
| 4 | Take action to discontinue the award or to resume payment *only* upon authorization by SSA. |

---

**XII.ii.2.D.5.d . Action Taken by VA to Notify the Payee of Termination of Payments**

Follow the steps in the table below to notify the payee of termination of payments upon authorization by SSA.

| **Step** | **Action** |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Notify the payee of the action taken and the reason for the termination by locally-generated letter,   * using the letter furnished by SSA as a pattern, and * enclosing the SSA attachments with the letter. |
| 2 | Send a copy of the letter to the claimant to the servicing SSA PSC. |

## **7.  Terminating 38 U.S.C. 1312(a) Payments for Children Reaching Age 18**

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information on terminating 38 U.S.C. 1312(a) payments for children reaching age 18, including information on

* [Hines Information Technology Center (ITC) message six months prior to the child’s 18th birthday](#7a)
* [VA notice to SSA on the child’s approaching 18th birthday](#7b)
* [action taken by SSA upon receipt of VA notice of a child’s approaching 18th birthday](#7c), and
* [action taken by VA on receipt of the certification from SSA](#7d).

---

**Change Date**

November 16, 2004

---

**XII.ii.2.D.7.a . Hines ITC Message Six Months Prior to the Child’s 18 th Birthday**

Six months prior to the child’s 18th birthday, the Hines Information Technology Center (ITC) furnishes message code 688, *Child Reaching Age 18 Special Allowance SEC 1312(A)*.

---

**XII.ii.2.D.7.b . VA Notice to SSA on the Child’s Approaching 18 th Birthday**

Six months in advance of the 18th birthday of each child for whom payments are being made under [38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](http://www4.law.cornell.edu/uscode/38/1312.html), VA

* notifies the SSA PSC by letter of the approaching 18th birthday, and
* includes the following information in the letter:

  + SSN
  + name of the child
  + date of birth of the child, and
  + name and address of the person to whom payments are being made for the child.

---

**XII.ii.2.D.7.c . Action Taken by SSA Upon Receipt of VA Notice of a Child’s Approaching 18 th Birthday**

Upon receipt of the notice sent by VA, SSA furnishes VA with the following:

* a copy of a letter concerning termination of benefits that would be sent to the beneficiary if they were being paid by SSA
* an amended certification, if required, for the remaining beneficiaries, and
* a copy of the letter that SSA would send the remaining beneficiaries, if they were being paid by that agency, together with enclosures.

---

**XII.ii.2.D.7.d . Action Taken by VA on Receipt of the Certification from SSA**

Follow the steps in the table below when the SSA furnishes the appropriate letter and the amended certification.

| **Step** | **Action** |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | * Send a locally-generated letter concerning the termination of benefits to the payee for the child who is attaining age 18, using the copy of the letter furnished by SSA as a pattern, and * send a copy of the letter to the servicing SSA PSC. |
| 2 | * Take any necessary award action that may be required by the receipt of an amended certification, and * send a copy of any letter written to the payee to the servicing SSA PSC. |

---

## **8.  Handling 38 U.S.C. 1312(a) Disagreements**

---

**Change Date**

April 19, 2005

---

**XII.ii.2.D.8.a . Handling 38 U.S.C. 1312(a) Disagreements**

A disagreement with VA’s decision on a claim for benefits under [38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1312) must be submitted on an approved standardized decision review form.

Refer any protests regarding determinations made by SSA to the servicing SSA PSC and inform the claimant of the referral.

***References***:  For more information on

* the eligibility determinations made by VA or SSA, see [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.B](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000175224/), and
* appeal procedures, see [M21-5, Chapter 4](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000127204/).
