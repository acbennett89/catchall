**III.i.1.A.1.a . Definition: Veteran**

A ***Veteran*** is a person who

* served in the active military, naval, air, or space service, and
* was discharged or released under conditions other than dishonorable.

***References***:  For more information on

* the definition of the term Veteran for purposes of compensation, Dependency and Indemnity Compensation (DIC) and death pension, see

  + [38 CFR 3.1(d)](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=6bd5a1037bb61467140fc98d81984f51&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_11&rgn=div8), and
  + [*Paulson v. Brown*](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000067574/), 7 Vet.App. 466, 470 (1995), and
* groups approved for Veteran status under *Public Law 95-202* and *106-259*, see [M21-1, Part III, Subpart i, 1.C](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000181426/).

**III.i.1.A.1.b . Definition: Active Service**

***Active* *service*** (i.e., *active military, naval, air, or space service*) includes

* active duty
* any period of active duty for training (ADT) during which a person is disabled or dies from a disease or injury incurred or aggravated in the line of duty, or
* any period of inactive duty for training (IADT) during which a person is disabled or dies from an injury incurred or aggravated in the line of duty or from any of the following conditions that occurred during training:

  + acute myocardial infarction
  + cardiac arrest, or
  + a cerebrovascular accident.

***Notes***:

* According to [VAOPGCPREC 8-2001](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000043823/), sexual assault constitutes an injury for the purposes of this block.
* Time spent proceeding directly to and from ADT and IADT must be considered as part of the ADT or IADT, as specified in [38 CFR 3.6(e)](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=29ee2aeaa585ebd068955b1b7dae7252&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_16&rgn=div8).

***Reference***:  For more information on the regulatory and statutory definitions of active duty, ADT, IADT, and active service, see

* [38 CFR 3.6](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=5dea2e5ae38d4f4810bf7ca791a5efee&node=se38.1.3_16&rgn=div8)
* [38 U.S.C. 101(21) through (24)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/101), and
* [38 U.S.C. 106(d)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/106).

**III.i.1.A.1.c . Definition: Qualifying Service**

***Qualifying service*** is any type of service that qualifies or potentially qualifies a claimant for Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) benefits.  Types of qualifying service are listed in [38 CFR 3.6](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=2f538904a32ee3bb43cde35d87149c24&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_16&rgn=div8) and [38 CFR 3.7](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/retrieveECFR?gp=&SID=2f538904a32ee3bb43cde35d87149c24&mc=true&r=SECTION&n=se38.1.3_17) and include (but are not limited to)

* active service
* ADT when a claim for service connection (SC) is based on a disability or death resulting from a disease or injury incurred or aggravated in the line of duty, and
* IADT when a claim for SC is based on disability or death resulting from an injury incurred or aggravated in the line of duty or myocardial infarction, cardiac arrest, or a cerebrovascular accident which occurred during such training.

**III.i.1.A.1.d . Primary Eligibility Criteria for Benefits**

The primary factor in determining basic eligibility to VA benefits is Veteran status.

Eligibility for

* *live* benefits is based on the claimant’s Veteran status, and
* *survivors* benefits is based on the claimant’s status as the spouse, parent or child of a deceased Veteran.

***Reference***:  For more information on the criteria for establishing Veteran status, see

* [38 CFR 3.1(d)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=ca62a3dfe712186f8857e457f5f81456&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_11&rgn=div8), and
* [M21-1, Part III, Subpart i, 1.A.1.a](#1a) and [b](#1b).

---

**III.i.1.A.1.e . Secondary Eligibility Criteria for Benefits**

If initial review of the evidence available establishes potential Veteran status, consider the secondary eligibility criteria listed below.

* Are the service dates and character of discharge (COD) verified, including

  + periods of active duty, and/or
  + periods of ADT or IADT upon which a claim is based?
* If the evidence does not show a discharge under conditions other than dishonorable, has VA made a determination of COD?
* Have the benefit-specific eligibility criteria been met?

***Note***:  As noted in [M21-1, Part X, Subpart i, 6.C.3.d](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177951/), Benefits Delivery at Discharge claims are rated while the service member is still on active duty.  Therefore, the current period of service will not be verified until after discharge.  These claims must not be deferred for service verification.

***References***:  For more information on

* verification of service dates and character of discharge, see [M21-1, Part III, Subpart i, 1.B.2](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000181425/)
* forms of evidence for verification of service and COD, see [M21-1, Part III, Subpart i, 1.B.3](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000181425/), and

**III.i.1.A.2.a . Initial Duty of a Reservist or National Guard Member**

Generally, reserve and National Guard members without prior active service undergo a period of initial ADT.  The training consists of basic training and specialized job-based skill training.  This training may be taken in a single period or as two separate periods.  When it is taken as two separate periods, this is referred to as split option training and is normally undertaken by members enrolled in school.

Individuals with prior active service generally are not required to undergo these periods of initial training when joining the National Guard or Reserve, although there are situations when individuals transfer from one branch of active duty to another branch of National Guard or reserve service that initial training is required.

***References***:  For more information on

* reserve service, see [M21-1, Part III, Subpart i, 1.A.3](#3), and
* National Guard service, see [M21-1, Part III, Subpart i, 1.A.4](#4).

---

**III.i.1.A.2.b . ADT and IADT Service in the Reserves and National Guard**

Service as a traditional reservist or National Guard member is not active service and is not adequate service upon which to establish Veteran status as defined in [38 CFR 3.1(d)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=ca62a3dfe712186f8857e457f5f81456&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_11&rgn=div8).  This service includes performing one period of weekend drill monthly and two weeks of annual training yearly.  This service is referred to as

* IADT, which includes traditional drill weekends, and
* ADT, which includes the

  + initial period of basic training and specialized job skill training, and
  + two week (fifteen-day) annual training period.

***Exception***:  For the purpose of establishing entitlement to VA benefits, Veteran status may be established based upon ADT or IADT under certain circumstances as discussed in [M21-1, Part III, Subpart i, 1.A.1.b](#1b) and [c](#1c).

***Reference***:  For more information on the criteria for establishing active service based on ADT or IADT, see [38 CFR 3.6](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/retrieveECFR?gp=&SID=2f538904a32ee3bb43cde35d87149c24&mc=true&r=SECTION&n=se38.1.3_16).

**III.i.1.A.2.d . ADS Service**

Individuals on ADS orders must waive compensation for a period equal to the period for which they received pay.

All ADS periods are generally initiated and recorded on an official order document in the service personnel records.  A *DD Form 214* is routinely issued, however, only for discharge from ADS periods of 90 days or more.

***Exception***:  In certain scenarios, a *DD Form 214* will be issued for ADS service of less than 90 days, as discussed in [M21-1, Part III, Subpart i, 1.B.5.a](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000181425/).

***Note***:  All periods of ADS for the Reserves *for operational support* are considered active service regardless of length of service.

***Reference***:  For more information on handling ADS reported on *VA Form 21-8951 and VA Form 21-8951-2, Notice of Waiver of VA Compensation or Pension to Receive Military Pay and Allowances*, see [M21-1, Part VI, Subpart ii, 3.A.4](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000179479/).

**III.i.1.A.2.e . Service Department Records for Verifying Periods of ADT and IADT**

Verification of specific periods of ADT or IADT (other than the initial period of ADT for which a *DD Form 214* is issued) is most easily accomplished via requesting the service personnel records and reviewing the ***detailed report*** of the member’s Retirement Points Annual Statement.  Although the detailed report version of the Retirement Points Annual Statement is the most complete reflection of all periods of verified service for an individual, including periods of active duty, ADT, and IADT, consider all other evidence of record from the service records when attempting verification of individual periods of ADT and IADT.

Other service records may be utilized to verify periods of ADT and IADT as long as the information provided

* reflects the type of service
* documents the dates of the period of service, and
* does not otherwise conflict with the remaining evidence of record.

Examples of other records that may be used to verify ADT and IADT service include, but are not limited to:

* a line of duty investigation documented on *DD Form 261, Report of Investigation Line of Duty and Misconduct Status,* as long as the form

  + is complete, and
  + bears the signature of the final approval authority
* service treatment records documenting that a member was injured during a period of ADT or IADT
* an official order directing the member to a period of annual training
* a pay stub documenting the period of service, or
* a Master Military Pay Account (MMPA) print.

***Notes***:

* *NGB Form 22, National Guard Report of Separation and Record of Service*, documents spans of service in the Army and Air National Guard.  However, this document does not verify individual periods of ADT or IADT during the span of the member’s service.
* There is no formal document issued at the time of a reservist’s separation or retirement.
* ADT and IADT service is not characterized by the service department.  Handle the characterization of service according to procedures at [M21-1, Part X, Subpart iv, 1.B.2.l](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000178524/).
* The MMPA may be requested by following the instructions in [M21-1, Part IIII, Subpart i, 1.A.5](#5).

***Reference***:  For more information on the issuance of *DD Form 214* for certain periods of ADT, full time training, or operational support duty, see [M21-1, Part III, Subpart i, 1.B.5.a](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000181425/).

**III.i.1.A.2.f . Deciding Line of Duty Status for Claims Based on ADT or IADT**

Formal line-of-duty investigations are documented on *DD Form 261*.  However, not all injuries, diseases, or deaths incurred or aggravated in the line of duty will be documented on *DD Form 261*, and a formal line-of-duty investigation is not a requirement to establish that a disease, injury, or death was incurred or aggravated in the line of duty during a period of ADT or IADT.

Consider all evidence of record in determining whether the injury, disease, or death was incurred or aggravated in the line of duty.

***Note***:  “In the line of duty” does not always mean while the reservist or National Guard member was at work.  Individuals with AGR service, including in the National Guard, are on continuous duty 24 hours a day, seven days a week just as are their active duty counterparts.  They receive the same pay and are eligible for retirement with 20 years of service.  If no intentional negligence or willful misconduct is noted, an injury or disease that occurs during AGR service is presumed to be in the line of duty.

***References***:  For more information on

* duty status related to injuries sustained by reservists or National Guard members while traveling to or from periods of ADT or IADT, see [38 CFR 3.6(e)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/retrieveECFR?gp=&SID=2f538904a32ee3bb43cde35d87149c24&mc=true&r=SECTION&n=se38.1.3_16), and
* determining line-of-duty status, see [*Holton and Bryant v. Shinseki*](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000015468/), 557 F.3d 1362 (Fed.Cir. 2009).

**III.i.1.A.2.g . Definition of Injury for ADT and IADT Purposes**

VA Office of General Counsel has issued precedent opinions to assist in defining the definition of “injury” for the purposes of considering claims for SC based on ADT and IADT service.

* [VAOPGCPREC 8-2001](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000043823/) held that for the purposes of considering a claim for SC for posttraumatic stress disorder, a sexual assault that occurred during IADT is considered an injury.
* [VAOPGCPREC 4-2002](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000043841/) held that an individual who suffers from a disabling condition as a result of administration of an anthrax vaccination during IADT may be considered disabled by an “injury” incurred during such training.  While [VAOPGCPREC 4-2002](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000043841/) does not address disabling conditions resulting from administration of other vaccines during IADT, the same principle applies for other vaccines, such as the Coronavirus disease (COVID-19) vaccine.

***References***:  For more information on

**III.i.1.A.2.i . Considering Presumptive SC Based on ADT and IADT**

Presumptive SC has three applicability criteria, including

* Veteran status
* service in the active military, naval, air, or space service, and
* service of
  + 90 continuous days or more before December 31, 1946, with at least one day occurring during a wartime period, or
  + any period of 90 days after December 31, 1946

As ADT and IADT is not considered active service and Veteran status is not achieved on the basis of ADT or IADT, presumptive provisions under [38 CFR 3.307](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=5869175dfdf92550071adb62ba2db3f2&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1307&rgn=div8) cannot apply to periods of ADT or IADT.

***Exceptions***:

* Per [38 CFR 3.307(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=5869175dfdf92550071adb62ba2db3f2&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1307&rgn=div8), any period of service is sufficient for the purpose of establishing presumptive SC under the conditions listed in

  + [38 CFR 3.309(c)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=5869175dfdf92550071adb62ba2db3f2&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1309&rgn=div8) for diseases specific to former prisoners of war
  + [38 CFR 3.309(e)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=5869175dfdf92550071adb62ba2db3f2&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1309&rgn=div8) for diseases associated with exposure to certain herbicide agents, and
  + [38 CFR 3.309(f)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=5869175dfdf92550071adb62ba2db3f2&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1309&rgn=div8) for diseases specific to exposure to contaminants in the water supply at Camp Lejeune, as long as the period of service also satisfies the requirements as defined in [38 CFR 3.307(a)(7)(iii)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=5869175dfdf92550071adb62ba2db3f2&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1307&rgn=div8).
* Per [38 U.S.C. 1164](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/1164) and [M21-1, Part VIII, Subpart iii, 10](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000203897/), special provisions apply for presumptive SC for COVID-19 and its residuals associated with periods of ADT and IADT.
* If SC for a disability or death due to a disease or injury is established based on a period of ADT or SC for a disability or death is established based on an injury or myocardial infarction, cardiac arrest, or cerebrovascular accident based on a period of IADT, then the ADT or IADT is considered active service per [38 CFR 3.6(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/retrieveECFR?gp=&SID=2f538904a32ee3bb43cde35d87149c24&mc=true&r=SECTION&n=se38.1.3_16) and may be utilized in determining whether the applicability criteria have been met for the presumptive claim.

***Example***:  A Veteran establishes SC for bladder cancer as a result of exposure to contaminated water at Camp Lejeune during a period of ADT that extended for more than 90 days under [38 CFR 3.307(a)(7)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=5869175dfdf92550071adb62ba2db3f2&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1307&rgn=div8) and [38 CFR 3.309(f)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=5869175dfdf92550071adb62ba2db3f2&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1309&rgn=div8).  The Veteran develops amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) and claims SC for the disease.  By virtue of the grant of SC for a disability associated with the period of ADT, the ADT is considered active service and the Veteran is now eligible for presumptive SC for ALS under [38 CFR 3.318](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=5869175dfdf92550071adb62ba2db3f2&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1318&rgn=div8).

***References***:  For more information on

* processing Camp Lejeune claims see [M21-1, Part VIII, Subpart iii, 8](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177465/), and
* presumptive SC, see

  + [38 CFR 3.307](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=5869175dfdf92550071adb62ba2db3f2&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1307&rgn=div8), and
  + [M21-1, Part V, Subpart ii, 2.B.1](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000180482/), and

**III.i.1.A.2.j . Considering Aggravation in Claims for SC Based on ADT and IADT**

The presumption of aggravation is triggered when a Veteran establishes that a preexisting condition, which was noted upon entry into service, worsens during service.  When this presumption arises, the burden shifts to VA to show a lack of aggravation by establishing that the in-service increase in disability is due to the natural progress of the disease.

Since ADT or IADT service does not satisfy the requirements for Veteran status and associated active service, the presumption of aggravation does not apply.  For a period of ADT or IADT, the evidence must ***additionally*** show that the worsening was ***caused by*** the period of ADT or IADT.  However, the presumption of aggravation will apply to periods of ADT and IADT when SC is first established for a disease or injury incurred during the period of service, as specified in [38 CFR 3.6(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/retrieveECFR?gp=&SID=2f538904a32ee3bb43cde35d87149c24&mc=true&r=SECTION&n=se38.1.3_16).

***References***:  For more information on

* adjudicating claims for SC based on aggravation of a preexisting disability, see

  + [M21-1, Part V, Subpart ii, 2.C](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000180483/)

**III.i.1.A.3.a . Eligibility of Reservists for Compensation and Pension**

**III.i.1.A.4.b . Full-time National Guard Service Under 32 U.S.C.**

Since 1964, there has been authority, under [32 U.S.C. 502(f)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/502), to assign to full-time operational duty National Guard members who provide full-time support to the Guard components, even though they are not activated.  Full-time National Guard service is considered ADT under [38 U.S.C. 101(22)(C)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/101) if performed under [32 U.S.C. 316](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/32/316), or [32 U.S.C. 502, 503, 504, or 505](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/32/chapter-5). * performing operational duty, including
  + AGR, and
  + ADS, or
* undergoing training.

**III.i.1.A.4.c . Qualifying National Guard Service Under 10 U.S.C.**

If a National Guard unit, or an individual member, is activated under the authority of [10 U.S.C.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/10), the members who report for active duty, which is characterized as Federal active duty, have qualifying service for [38 U.S.C.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38) purposes until deactivated.

If an individual’s orders specify activation to temporary duty under [10 U.S.C.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/10), further development regarding the purpose of the activation is not needed *unless* there is evidence in the claims folder showing that the purpose of the activation was to train the individual.

The order to active duty *must* state that service is under [10 U.S.C.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/10)  This fact is frequently reflected on the *DD Form 214*.

***Note***:  In some cases, a member may be ordered to ADT under the authority of [10 U.S.C. 12301(d)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/10/12301).  This does constitute ADT for [38 U.S.C.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38) purposes, and when *DD Form 214* indicates the activation was for training, do not consider the ADT period as active service.

---

**III.i.1.A.4.d . Verification of Full-time National Guard Service**

When a National Guard member completes a period of active service under [10 U.S.C.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/10) or a period of AGR service or other full-time service under [32 U.S.C.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/32), a *DD Form 214* will be issued. The type of National Guard service should be identified on the *DD Form 214*.  If the information is not indicated on the *DD Form 214*,develop with the appropriate component listed in [M21-1, Part III, Subpart ii, 2.H](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000037154/).

***Note***:  If the *DD Form 214*, or the response to development with the service department, indicates that service was under both [10 U.S.C.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/10) and [32 U.S.C.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/32), consider the service to be under [10 U.S.C.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/10)

## 5. Requesting Military Pay Records

**III.i.1.A.5.a . Requesting Pay Records**

When pay records are required to verify periods of IADT and ADT, or make other determinations, submit the request through the [AskDFAS Military Pay Request Portal](https://corpweb1.dfas.mil/askDFAS/custCategories.action?tsm=1485548562128&pgModId=5243).

***Exception***:  Contact the Compensation Service [MilPay corporate mailbox](mailto:VAVBAWAS/CO/MILPAY?subject=Please%20assist%20obtaining%20Coast%20Guard%20pay%20records) for assistance obtaining Coast Guard pay records.

Each branch has a specific set of selections claims processors must use beyond the default required information.  Use the following table to determine which specific selections are necessary to properly request pay records for each branch of service.

| **Branch of Service** | **Selections or Information to Provide** |
| --- | --- |
| * Army * Air Force/Space Force | **CATEGORY**:  *Military Pay Records Requests*  **SUBCATEGORY**:  Select appropriate request based on the date range of records.  **REASON FOR REQUEST** (if required):  *VA Claims* |
| Marine Corps | **CATEGORY**:  *Separation Pay Verification*  **SUBCATEGORY**:  *USMC VA DSP/Seps Pay Verification*  **SPECIAL INSTRUCTIONS**:  [**Provide details about the records VA is requesting.**] |
| Navy | **CATEGORY**:  *Separation Pay Verification*  **SUBCATEGORY**:  *Navy Member Information*  **SPECIAL INSTRUCTIONS**:  [**Provide details about the records VA is requesting.**] |

***Important***:

* Save a copy of the submitted request for association with the claims folder, following instructions in [M21-1, Part III, Subpart i, 1.A.5.b](#5b).
* Attaching a copy of the *DD Form 214*, *DD Form 256, Honorable Discharge Certificate*, *DD Form 257, General Discharge Certificate*, or *NGB Form 22*, is required when submitting a request via the portal.

***Reference***:   For more information about the [AskDFAS Military Pay Request Portal](https://corpweb1.dfas.mil/askDFAS/custCategories.action?tsm=1485548562128&pgModId=5243), see the [*AskDFAS User Guide*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/21/212/milpay.htm).

---

**III.i.1.A.5.b . Adding a Copy of the Request to the Claims Folder**

After the pay request is submitted, the [AskDFAS Military Pay Request Portal](https://corpweb1.dfas.mil/askDFAS/custCategories.action?tsm=1485548562128&pgModId=5243) will provide a confirmation page that contains a hyperlink to the records request, enabling a claims processor (with the passcode created during the submission process) to retrieve the requested records.

Using the web browser’s PRINT function, save the page as a portable document format (PDF), as opposed to using a screen capture or snipping tool to create a photo of the confirmation page.  This will maintain the hyperlink that can be used to quickly access the requested documents.

***Important***:  Ensure the passcode is visible on the confirmation page that is uploaded to the electronic claims folder (eFolder).  If the passcode is not, identify the passcode using the annotation function in VBMS.

***References***:  For more information on

* determining the appropriate document indexing values when adding a copy to the eFolder, see the [*AskDFAS User Guide*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/21/212/milpay.htm), and
* bookmarks, annotations, and uploading documents to the eFolder, see the *VBMS Core User Guide*.
