## 1.  Obtaining Information From SSA to Determine Eligibility for the Special Allowance Under 38 U.S.C. 1312(a)

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information on obtaining information from the SSA to determine eligibility for the special allowance under 38 U.S.C. 1312(a), including

* [using *VA Form 21P-4180*, *Request for Certification by Social Security Administration,* to obtain information from SSA](#1a)
* [when to use *VA Form 21P-4180*](#1b)
* [preparing *VA Form 21P-4180* for referral to SSA](#1c)
* [completing *VA Form 21P-4180*](#1d), and
* [addressing and mailing *VA Form 21P-4180*](#1e).

---

**Change Date**

May 18, 2015

---

**XII.ii.2.C.1.a . Using VA Form 21P-4180 to Obtain Information From SSA**

Use [*VA Form 21P-4180, Request for Certification by Social Security Administration*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf), to obtain information from the Social Security Administration (SSA) as to whether the special allowance under [38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/38/1312.html) may be payable to an eligible survivor or survivors.

---

**XII.ii.2.C.1.b . When to Use VA Form 21P-4180**

Send [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf) to SSA if

* the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) receives a specific inquiry or claim for the special allowance, *and*
* *SSA Form 101*, *Determination of Award*, *SSA Form 101C*, *Determination of Award*, or *SSA Form 101U3*, *Determination of Award,* is ***not*** of record.

***Reference***:  For more information on the certification of entitlement by SSA, see [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.C.2](#2).

---

**XII.ii.2.C.1.c . Preparing VA Form 21P-4180 for Referral to SSA**

Follow the steps in the table below to refer [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf) to SSA.

| **Step** | **Action** |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Prepare [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf), Part 1.    ***Reference***:  For more information on how to complete the items on [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf), Part 1, see [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.C.1.d](#1d). |
| 2 | * Address and mail [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf) to the appropriate SSA Program Service Center (PSC), and * establish a 90-day suspense.   ***Reference***:  For more information on addressing and mailing [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf), see [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.C.1.e](#1e). |
| 3 | Has a reply been received from SSA before the end of the 90-day suspense period?   * If *yes*,take appropriate action as shown in [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.C.2.d](#2d). * If *no*,    + send a follow-up by submitting another [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf), which may be a reproduction of the claims folder copy, prominently marked *SECOND REQUEST*, and   + maintain a 30-day suspense for response to the follow-up. |
| 4 | Has the reply been received from SSA before the end of the 30-day follow-up suspense period?   * If *yes*, take appropriate action as shown in [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.C.2.d](#2d). * If *no*,    + follow up by fax or telephone, and   + maintain a 30-day suspense. |

***Note***:  Do ***not*** withhold the referral of [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf) to develop for any evidence that may be required to complete the claim.

---

**XII.ii.2.C.1.d . Completing VA Form 21P-4180**

Use the table below for instructions on how to complete the items on [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf), Part I.

| **Item** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| 1, 5, and 6 | Provide the Veteran’s   * full name * date of birth, and * date of death.   ***Note***:  This information is of particular importance for SSA purposes when the Social Security number (SSN) is unknown or not correct. |
| 2 | Provide the complete address of the regional office (RO) submitting the form.    ***Note***:  Since SSA returns these forms in a window envelope, write the address so that the complete address can be seen in the window. |
| 3 | Enter the VA claim number. |
| 4 | Enter either   * the Veteran’s SSN, if known, or * *Unknown*. |
| 7 | List the following claimants, as applicable:   * the surviving spouse * all children under age 18 or children incapable of self-support, and * parents.   ***Important***:   * It is necessary to list ***all*** the possible claimants whether a claim has been filed, such as    + surviving spouse   + children, and   + parents. * When parents are listed, make a notation as to whether they have filed a claim for VA benefits. |
| 8 | Obtain the signature of the Veterans Service Center Manager. |
| 14 | Enter the appropriate SSA PSC as described in [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.C.1.e](#1e). |

---

**XII.ii.2.C.1.e . Addressing and Mailing VA Form 21P-4180**

Use the table below to determine how to address and dispatch the completed [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf) to SSA.

| --- | --- |
| the Veteran’s SSN is known | appropriate SSA PSC as described in [M21-1, Part III, Subpart ii, 1.B.6](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000014111/). |
| the Veteran’s SSN is unknown | SSA district office nearest the transmitting RO as described in [M21-1, Part III, Subpart ii, 1.B.5.b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000014111/). |
| the Veteran is a foreign claimant | Division of Foreign Claims  Social Security Administration  P. O. Box 1756  Baltimore, MD 21203. |

## 2.  Handling Certification of Entitlement for the Special Allowance Under 38 U.S.C. 1312(a) by SSA

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information on the certification of entitlement for the special allowance under 38 U.S.C. 1312(a), including

* [certifying entitlement for the special allowance under 38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](#2a)
* [SSA action after determining eligibility](#2b)
* [information that must be provided on *SSA Forms 101*, *101C*, or *101U3*](#2c)
* action taken by VA

  + [when SSA returns **VA Form 21P-4180**](#2d)
  + [if entitlement certified by SSA is not payable](#2e), and
  + [on receipt of an incomplete *VA Form 21P-4180*](#2f), and

**Change Date**

---

**XII.ii.2.C.2.a . Certifying Entitlement for the Special Allowance Under 38 U.S.C. 1312(a)**

The table below describes the process followed by SSA to certify entitlement for the special allowance under [38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/38/1312.html).

| **Stage** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Receives notice of death from the service department. |
| 2 | Determines whether there are survivors eligible for the special allowance under [38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/38/1312.html).    ***Reference***:  For more information on the actions taken by SSA after determining the claimant’s entitlement to the special allowance, see [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.C.2.b](#2b). |
| 3 | Takes action as appropriate depending on the eligibility determination.    ***References***:  For more information on   * the appropriate SSA actions after determining eligibility, see [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.C.2.b](#2b), and * reviewing the [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf) returned by SSA to determine entitlement or non-entitlement, see [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.C.2.d](#2d). |

---

**XII.ii.2.C.2.b . SSA Actions After Determining Eligibility**

The table below describes the action SSA takes to certify a claimant’s entitlement to the special allowance under [38 U.S.C. 1312(a)](http://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/38/1312.html).

| **When SSA determines that …** | **Then SSA …** |
| --- | --- |
| there are survivors eligible for the special allowance | * returns [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf) with the appropriate notations * furnishes a copy of an award letter * furnishes enclosures for the award letter as described in [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.D.2](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000175226/), and * furnishes one of the following forms to VA as authority for payment:    + *SSA Form 101*   + **SSA Form 101C**,** or   + ***SSA Form 101U3*.** |
| * there is ***no*** survivor eligible to receive the special allowance, or * the special allowance is ***not*** payable because there is entitlement to an old-age and survivors insurance payment | * denies the claim, and * notifies the claimant.   ***Note***:  VA is ***not*** responsible for furnishing a notice of denial or review rights, except for an adverse determination as described in [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.B.1](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000175224/), and then only if there is a specific claim for the special allowance. |

---

**XII.ii.2.C.2.c . Information That Must Be Provided on SSA Forms 101, 101C, or 101U3**

Do ***not*** take award action to pay the special allowance unless the *SSA Forms 101*, *101C*, or *101U3* furnished by SSA contain the

* name and address of each beneficiary
* monthly rate of payment
* first month of entitlement, and
* notation *Do not certify.  VA Payment Only*.

---

**XII.ii.2.C.2.d . Action Taken by VA When SSA Returns VA Form 21P-4180**

Use the table below to determine the action to take when SSA returns [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf).

| **If …** | **Then …** |
| --- | --- |
| [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf), Item 10A, is checked | * the special allowance is payable * take adjudicative action on all pending claims, and * when all claims are finalized, transfer the deceased Veteran’s claims folder to the Philadelphia Pension Management Center (PMC) for payment of the special allowance.   ***Reference***:  For more information on the jurisdiction for payment of the special allowance, see [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.A.2](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000175223/). |
| [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf), Item 11A, is checked | * the special allowance is ***not*** payable * add the [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf)to the deceased Veteran’s claims folder, and * take no further action. |
| [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf), Item 11B, is checked | * send a letter to the surviving spouse advising of possible entitlement at age 60, and * inform the surviving spouse to contact VA at age 60.   ***Note***:  This does not affect the jurisdiction of the claims folder. |
| [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf), Item 11C, is checked | there is no one eligible to receive Social Security benefits.    ***Note***:  Take no further action. |

***Note***:  When the certification of entitlement by SSA is received in an RO and not the Philadelphia PMC, take action as provided in [M21-1, Part XII, Subpart ii, 2.A.2.b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000175223/).

---

**XII.ii.2.C.2.e . Action Taken by VA if Entitlement Certified by SSA Is Not Payable**

If VA determines that entitlement certified by SSA on *SSA Forms 101*, *101C*, or *101U3* is ***not*** payable for any reason, notify the SSA PSC that made the certification.

---

**XII.ii.2.C.2.f . Action Taken by VA on Receipt of an Incomplete VA Form 21P-4180**

Use the table below to determine what action to take when SSA returns an incomplete [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf) because the claimant failed to respond to correspondence from SSA.

| **If the …** | **Then …** |
| --- | --- |
| [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf) contains the most recent address of the claimant as shown in the claims folder | * add the [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf) to the deceased Veteran’s claims folder * annotate [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf) with *NAN* (**n**o **a**ction **n**ecessary), and * include    + the initials of the employee making the annotation, and   + the current date. |
| claims folder contains a more recent address | * send another [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf) containing the most recent address of record to the SSA PSC, and * annotate [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf) to show it is being submitted due to the receipt of a current address. |

***Note***:  If the claims folder is an electronic claims folder, add the annotation in the Veterans Benefits Management System in the SUBJECT field under DOCUMENT PROPERTIES of the [*VA Form 21P-4180*](https://vbaw.vba.va.gov/bl/20/cio/20s5/forms/VBA-21P-4180-ARE.pdf).

---

**XII.ii.2.C.2.g . When Recertification for the Special Allowance Under 38 U.S.C. 1312(a) Is Required**

If an award of special allowance is delayed by VA for more than one year after the date of approval of the *SSA Forms 101*, *101C*, or *101U3* by the servicing SSA PSC, contact that office by letter requesting verification of continued eligibility before processing an award.
