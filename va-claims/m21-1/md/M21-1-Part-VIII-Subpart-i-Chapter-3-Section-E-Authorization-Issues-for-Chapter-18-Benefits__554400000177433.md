## 1.  Effective Date for Reductions and Terminations

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information on the effective date for Chapter 18 benefits, including the effective date for

* [reduction of Chapter 18 benefits](#1a), and
* [termination of Chapter 18 benefits](#1b).

---

**Change Date**

May 14, 2015

---

**VIII.i.3.E.1.a . Effective Date for Reduction of Chapter 18 Benefits**

Whenever it is necessary to reduce or discontinue a monthly allowance, generally, reduce or discontinue based on the facts found, except as provided in [38 CFR 3.105(g)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-3.105), and [38 CFR 3.114(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-3.114).

The table below provides information to determine the correct effective date of a reduction or discontinuance.

| **If benefits were paid erroneously due to ...** | **Then the effective date of the reduction or discontinuance is the date of ...** |
| --- | --- |
| beneficiary error | the erroneous award. |
| Department of Veterans Affairs administrative error | last payment. |

***Reference***:  For more information on resumption of discontinued benefits under [38 U.S.C. Chapter 18](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/part-II/chapter-18), see [38 CFR 3.158](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-3.158).

---

**VIII.i.3.E.1.b . Effective Date for Termination of Chapter 18 Benefits**

In the event of death, discontinue Chapter 18 benefits on the last day of the month *before* the month in which the child died.

***Note***:  Handle abandoned claims for Chapter 18 benefits in the same manner as for compensation, pension, or Dependency and Indemnity Compensation.

***Reference***:  For more information on the effective date for termination of benefits, see [38 CFR 3.503(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-3.503).

## 2.  Processing Awards and Denials

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information on processing awards and denials of Chapter 18 monetary allowances, including

* [description of the final stage in the Chapter 18 claims process](#2a)
* [roles and responsibilities for processing Chapter 18 awards and denials](#2b), and
* [award and denial process for Chapter 18 claims](#2c).

---

**Change Date**

December 31, 2019

---

**VIII.i.3.E.2.a . Description of the Final Stage in the Chapter 18 Claims Process**

The final stage in the Chapter 18 claims process is to promulgate the rating decision or prepare an administrative denial and notify the claimant of the decision.

---

**VIII.i.3.E.2.b . Roles and Responsibilities for Processing Chapter 18 Awards and Denials**

The authorization activity is responsible for processing awards and denials.

---

**VIII.i.3.E.2.c . Award and Denial Process for Chapter 18 Claims**

The processing of Chapter 18 awards is presently accomplished using the Veterans Benefits Management System.
