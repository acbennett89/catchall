**II.iii.4.B.1.a . Purpose of ARP**

The Accredited Representative Portal (ARP) is a secure online tool for accredited representatives (including Veterans Service Organizations (VSOs), private attorneys, and agents) to efficiently establish representation and support claimants through the benefits claim process.

Through ARP these representatives can

* digitally submit an intent to file
* manage representation requests and agreements received online through a Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) claims submission service website
* submit VA forms, documents, and supporting evidence to the Centralized Mail Portal
* view submission history, and
* review claimant details.

***Important***:  With the exception of [*VA Form 21-22*, *Appointment of Veterans Service Organization as Claimant’s Representative*](http://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-22-ARE.pdf), ARP *does not* generate VA forms. Accredited representatives must manually complete the forms and can subsequently submit the files via ARP.  Select forms are eligible for data validation to ensure accurate form completion.

***Notes***:

* The [*VA Form 21-22a, Appointment of Individual As Claimant’s Representative*](http://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-22a-ARE.pdf), is *not* available for private attorneys and claims agents to manage through ARP.
* Before the ARP was implemented, accredited representatives used

  + eBenefits, which was decommissioned in December 2025, and
  + the Stakeholders Enterprise Portal (SEP), which was decommissioned in January 2026.

***References***:  For

* form submission via ARP, see [M21-1, Part II, Subpart iii, 4.B.1.d](#1d)
* more information on handling documents that fail to upload, see

  + [M21-1, Part II, Subpart iii, 4.B.1.c](#1c), and
  + [M21-1, Part II, Subpart ii, 2.A](void(0)), and
* historical information on SEP and its functionality, see *Historical\_M21-1\_II\_iii\_4\_SecB*.

**II.iii.4.B.1.b . Establishment of POA Through ARP**

Accredited representatives utilize ARP to complete the power of attorney (POA) establishment process once a claimant initiates the process in VA.gov.

ARP functionality

* allows accredited representatives to
  + accept the representation request, and
  + complete required fields on *[VA Form 21-22](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-22-ARE.pdf)*
* automatically update the corporate record with the POA information, and
* generate and upload the *[VA Form 21-22](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-22-ARE.pdf)* directly to the claimant’s electronic claims folder (eFolder).

The table below outlines the process that occurs after a claimant selects an accredited representative as the POA through VA.gov.

| **Stage** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | [*VA Form 21-22*](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-22-ARE.pdf) loads into the selected representative’s portal for review. |
| 2 | The organization has sixty days to review the representation request. |
| 3 | ARP acts based on the representative’s selection.  If POA representation is   * *rejected*, the claimant is notified of the rejected representation via VA Notify, or * *accepted*, the    + [*VA Form 21-22*](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-22-ARE.pdf) is uploaded to the eFolder within minutes   + corporate database is immediately updated to include the newly elected POA information, and   + claimant is notified of the accepted representation via VA Notify.   ***Exception***:  If the representative does not approve or reject representation within sixty days, the   * request is removed from ARP due to inaction, and * claimant is notified through VA Notify. |

***Important***:

* Any automated POA established through ARP is valid for VA purposes and not subject to the requirements of paper *[VA Form 21-22](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-22-ARE.pdf)* processing.
* The *[VA Form 21-22](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-22-ARE.pdf)* will indicate electronic submission using the *Lighthouse via api.va.gov* watermark with the time and date of the submission.
* As mentioned in [M21-1, Part II, Subpart iii, 4.B.1.a](#1a), private attorneys and claims agents are not able to use this process to manage POA establishment in ARP.

***Note***:  The signature blocks on the *[VA Form 21-22](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-22-ARE.pdf)* will format the signature text as indicated by

* First Name + Last Name of claimant
* First Name + Last Name of the accredited representative, and
* the notation “Signed via api.va.gov” following each signature.

**II.iii.4.B.1.c . Failure to Upload VA Form 21-22 for ARP-Established POAs**

In rare circumstances, the *[VA Form 21-22](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-22-ARE.pdf)* may not correctly upload to the eFolder.  Claims processors may presume that a POA was legitimately established using the ARP process if a review of the POA history in the Veterans Benefits Management System (VBMS) shows that the POA appointment update was performed by *VAGOVAPI*.

Claims processors must contact [RepresentativePortalHelp@va.gov](mailto:RepresentativePortalHelp@va.gov?subject=VA%20Form%2021-22%20failure%20to%20upload) if the *[VA Form 21-22](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-22-ARE.pdf)* is not of record and more than 48 hours have passed since the appointment of the POA via ARP.  In this specific situation, do not follow the failure-to-upload procedure described in [M21-1, Part II, Subpart ii, 2.A](void(0)).

The email to the ARP help desk must

* identify the issue as a *[VA Form 21-22](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-22-ARE.pdf)* upload failure
* include the claimant’s Integration Control Number (ICN), and
* state the approximate date and time of the form submission.

***Notes***:

* Add a permanent Veteran-level note indicating the *[VA Form 21-22](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-22-ARE.pdf)* failed to upload and actions are being taken to recover the document.
* Upload a copy of the email to the claimant’s eFolder.
* The approximate date and time of submission can be determined by reviewing when the update was performed by *VAGOVAPI* in the POA history.

***Reference***:  For more information on locating the claimant’s ICN or viewing POA information in VBMS, see the *VBMS Core User Guide*.

**II.iii.4.B.1.d . Form Submission via ARP**

ARP directs the accredited representative to the [VA Forms publication webpage](https://www.va.gov/forms/) to download the current version of VA forms.  The representative completes the form and uploads the document to ARP.

Additional supporting documents (such as *[VA Form 21-4138, Statement in Support of Claim](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-4138-ARE.pdf)*[,](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-4138-ARE.pdf) medical records, etc.) may be uploaded with the claim in different media formats.  All documents uploaded through ARP are processed by VA mail automation services and uploaded to the claimant’s eFolder.

***Important***:  When a claims processor becomes aware that documents (other than *[VA Form 21-22](https://www.vba.va.gov/pubs/forms/VBA-21-22-ARE.pdf)*) failed to upload properly, follow the failure-to-upload procedure described in [M21-1, Part II, Subpart ii, 2.A](void(0)).
