## 1.  Rating Decision Review

---

**Change Date**

July 22, 2024

---

**V.iv.1.E.1.a . Review of Rating Decisions**

When the rating decision is completed, it may be reviewed prior to promulgation by individuals including

* a designated second signer, including the Veterans Service Center Manager or Pension Management Center Manager
* a supervisor
* local quality review team members, *and/or*
* the authorization activity.

Individuals who may review promulgated and issued rating decisions include

* the Compensation Service staff, to include Quality Assurance
* a decision maker in connection with a legacy appeal or request for decision review under [38 CFR 3.2500](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=c08a3c62e4c2007c26da4379059b6e93&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_12500&rgn=div8), *and/or*
* Veterans Service Center (VSC) and pension management center (PMC) management and/or quality review personnel.

***References***:  For more information on

* correction of errors found in rating decisions, see [M21-1, Part V, Subpart iv, 1.E.3](#3)
* processing rating decisions, see [M21-1, Part VI, Subpart i, 1.A.2](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000179468/)
* quality reviews, see [M21-4, Chapter 3](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000033436/), and
* the legacy appellate process, see [M21-5, Chapter 7](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000140915/).

## 2.  Handling Dissent and Differences of Opinion in Rating Decisions

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information about handling dissenting opinions on two-signature ratings, including

* [dissenting opinion versus difference of opinion](#2a), and
* [who is responsible for resolving dissenting opinions](#2b).

---

**Change Date**

July 22, 2024

---

**V.iv.1.E.2.a . Dissenting Opinion vs. Difference of Opinion**

For the purpose of this section:

* a ***dissenting opinion*** scenario occurs when multiple decision makers whose signatures are required on a rating decision do not concur on some aspect of an *unpromulgated* decision, and
* a ***difference of opinion*** scenario occurs when a decision maker is of the opinion that revision or amendment is needed (for reasons that do not qualify as clear and unmistakable error (CUE)) of a *promulgated* rating decision completed by another decision maker.

***Reference***:  For more information on handling differences of opinion , see [M21-1, Part X, Subpart v, 1.A.1 and 4](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177996/).

---

**V.iv.1.E.2.b . Who Is Responsible for Resolving Dissenting Opinions**

A designated reviewer will resolve case of a dissenting opinion by providing the required second signature in place of one of the decision makers.

***Reference***:  For more information on second signature procedures, see [M21-1, Part V, Subpart iv, 1.B.7](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000180524/).

## 3.  Correcting Deficiencies in Rating Decisions

---

**Introduction**

This topic contains information about correcting deficiencies in rating decisions whether found prior to promulgation or after promulgation and issuance, including

* correcting

  + [narrative deficiencies before or after authorization](#3a), and
  + [the *Codesheet* before or after authorization](#3b)
* [procedure for correction of rating decisions](#3c), and
* [revising erroneous anatomical qualifiers](#3d).

---

**Change Date**

June 9, 2025

---

**V.iv.1.E.3.a . Correcting Narrative Deficiencies Before or After Authorization**

The rating activity must correct all *Narrative* section deficiencies when those are noted *prior* to promulgation and/or authorization.

The rating activity must also correct the *Narrative* section of a rating decision if *after* the claimant has been notified of the decision it is discovered that the decision provided

* inaccurate information regarding factors such as service dates or entitlements, and/or
* incomplete information regarding factors such as criteria for the next higher evaluation, or a change of law applicable to the pending claim.

***References***:  For more information on

* the procedure applicable when errors must be corrected before or after issuance of a rating, see [M21-1, Part V, Subpart iv, 1.E.3.c](#3c)
* revising erroneous anatomical qualifiers, see [M21-1, Part V, Subpart iv, 1.E.3.d](#3d), and
* effect of statutory or regulatory changes, or new court precedents, while a claim is pending, see [M21-1, Part V, Subpart ii, 4.A](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000180492/).

---

**V.iv.1.E.3.b . Correcting the Codesheet Before or After Authorization**

It is critical to correct errors on the *Codesheet**,* whether identified before or after promulgation or authorization, that

* affect payment or eligibility to a benefit, or
* could affect payment in the future.

Such errors include, but are not limited to

* determinations on entitlement (service connection (SC), special monthly compensation (SMC), etc.)
* disability evaluations
* effective dates
* combined evaluations
* diagnostic codes (DCs)
* SMC codes, and
* bilateral factors.

***Important***:  In addition to the above, as provided in [M21-1, Part II, Subpart iii, 3.B.3.a](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000174882/), periods of active military service must be updated and verified in the system prior to rating.  Such errors may not affect payment, but if the *Codesheet* does not reflect the correct service dates, return the rating decision for correction.

---

**V.iv.1.E.3.c . Procedure for Correction of Rating Decisions**

Use the table below when correcting a rating decision for the reasons discussed in [M21-1, Part V, Subpart iv, 1.E.3.a](#3a) or [b](#3b).

| **If the rating decision has** **...** | **Then refer the rating decision to** **...** |
| --- | --- |
| not been issued | * the decision maker who made the decision, or * to VSC or PMC management for assignment of the rating correction.   ***Notes***:    * If the erroneous rating decision has not been promulgated, pulling the record back into the Veterans Benefits Management System - Rating (VBMS-R) anew will overwrite the former, errant decision. * If the underlying end product is improperly cleared before the non-promulgated rating decision can be corrected, follow the guidance in [M21-1, Part II, Subpart ii, 2.A.2.b](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000174866/), to request VBMS document deletion of the erroneous rating decision. |
| been issued | VSC or PMC management for assignment to a decision maker to issue a new decision. |

***Note***:  VSC or PMC management may assign rating corrections to another decision maker if the decision’s original author is not available.

***Important***:

* When a correction of an entitlement outcome is required for an issued decision that is binding, there must be

  + CUE under the provisions of [38 CFR 3.105(a)](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=6ba19c3976405f04945fc37f84cbfc6b&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1105&rgn=div8) and [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 5.A](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177971/), ***or***
  + approval of difference of opinion under [38 CFR 3.105(b)](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=6ba19c3976405f04945fc37f84cbfc6b&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1105&rgn=div8) and [M21-1, Part X, Subpart v, 1.A.1 and 4](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177996/).
* Due process procedures under [38 CFR 3.103](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=3b2b36e3377ee3c3773f96c3a81b88cc&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1103&rgn=div8) may apply where correction of an error in a rating decision would constitute an adverse action.

***References***:  For more information on

* obtaining rating decision signatures via Draft Rating Approval (DRA) functionality, see

  + [M21-1, Part V, Subpart iv, 1.B.7](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000180524/), and
  + the *[DRA Job Aid](https://dvagov.sharepoint.com/sites/vbaofficeofautomatedbenefitsdelivery/SitePages/Job-Aids-and-Demos.aspx)*
* updating the master record and DC in VBMS-R, see the *VBMS Rating User Guide*
* impact of a change in DC, see [M21-1, Part V, Subpart ii, 3.D.1.d](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000180489/)
* adverse action resulting from a CUE, see [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 5](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177971/), and

**V.iv.1.E.3.d . Revising Erroneous Anatomical Qualifiers**

Revise an erroneous qualifying description of one part of the body for another that has been previously compensated.  This situation is usually the result of an unwarranted substitution of left for right, or right for left.

***Exception***:  If an *original* award of SC contains an erroneous qualifying description of one part of the body for another, revise the rating decision under the CUE provisions of [38 CFR 3.105(a)](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=845e99308330a576fc2c8f6a61421563&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1105&rgn=div8) and [M21-1, Part X, Subpart ii, 5.A](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000177971/).

***Example***:  An original award of SC of gunshot wound to the left thigh, when the records clearly showed the wound was to the right thigh, is a CUE subject to correction under [38 CFR 3.105(a)](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=845e99308330a576fc2c8f6a61421563&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1105&rgn=div8).

***Notes***:

* A notice of proposed adverse action is not required simply due to a change in anatomical site, unless it results in a reduction in compensation.
* This change will not violate the protection of SC under [38 CFR 3.957](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=f45497283140de15f8d76e4c288699e9&node=se38.1.3_1957&rgn=div8), or protection of a service-connected evaluation under [38 CFR 3.951](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=f0e9c482b557c237095744c488c4a30b&node=se38.1.3_1951&rgn=div8).
* A CUE decision, under [38 CFR 3.105(a)](http://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=6ba19c3976405f04945fc37f84cbfc6b&mc=true&node=se38.1.3_1105&rgn=div8), is for application when previous determinations are binding.

***Reference***:  For more information on revisions to anatomical qualifiers, see

* *[Read v. Shinseki](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000014455/)*, 651 F. 3d 1296 (2011), and
* [*Gifford v. Brown*](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000084384/), 6 Vet. App. 269 (1994).
